Privacy policy

Last reviewed Nov 21, 2026. This policy describes how GTA 6 Guides handles information.

Cookies

Third-party services on the published site may store or read cookies on your device, including for advertising.

Advertising

The published site uses Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and information about earlier visits to this site or other websites to serve personalized ads. You can turn off Google ad personalization in Google Ads Settings.

Analytics and retention

The guide runs no first-party analytics, Google Analytics, or Cloudflare Web Analytics. The operators collect and retain no analytics records. When pages are served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub manages those service logs under its own privacy statement.

Data and external links

The guide consists of static pages. It has no user accounts or web forms. There is no file upload, and files on your device are not sent to the guide. Source links, including video time links, open third-party sites, which apply their own privacy practices.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and the details you share to respond and handle the request. For privacy questions, use our contact page.
