GTA 6 Chapter 1 missables checklist

Keep a manual Chapter 1 save and check the map for hourglass markers. If you’re chasing trophies, do the early balcony meditation when it appears.

Checklist before the chapter ends

  • Keep a Chapter 1 save. When you are in free roam, save in a slot you can leave untouched. The launch version offers no standalone replay for finished missions.
  • Follow hourglass markers for optional scenes you want. PowerPyx identifies that icon as a time-sensitive optional objective. Check the map before committing to a chain of story missions.
  • Do the balcony meditation if offered. When Lucia heads to the balcony at Jason’s house, look for the optional invitation and accept it. The scene is Chapter 1 only. Its necessity for Positive Mental Attitude remained unresolved in the checked walkthrough.
  • Check your money before the chapter transition. The linked cash guide explains the bank categories and hides the story checkpoint behind a spoiler label.

Separate 100%, trophies and a personal collection

The launch trophy roadmap puts the 100% tracker under Progress → Stats → 100% Completion after the story. Earlier progress still counts. Its category targets do not require every optional scene or every collectible.

For meditation, the published 100% target is any 15 of 18. The separate Positive Mental Attitude trophy is about meditation spots. The first visit is worth keeping for a trophy run while the requirement is unresolved. If you want a particular car, weapon or one-time scene for a personal collection, give that goal its own save rather than assuming it is part of the 100% checklist.

Check the map before repeating a stunt jump

A player capture shows an already discovered stunt jump listed without completion. Check an individual marker and your own Progress menu before repeating a jump, especially if the landing did not register. Exact category totals and tracking can differ from a fan checklist.
