All 80 GTA 6 challenges

Complete any 50 challenges for One Last Thing. The Story Mode 100% checklist asks for any 70 of the 80. Use the categories below to check exact requirements.

CombatCriminalExplorationFitnessShoppingSportsSurvivalVehicle

Where to check progress

  1. Open Progress → Challenges and select a category.
  2. Highlight the challenge and check for the Completed mark at the bottom of the card.
  3. Check the 100% checklist separately. For example, Basketball Pro takes all 100 hoops, while the checklist needs 90 hoops and any 70 challenges.

On a fresh save, group simple checks with existing routes. Scan four landmarks while working on the 100 scans, score in each county during the hoop route, and pick up three cards per county while filling sets. That leaves room to skip challenges with long routes or tight timers.
