What to check when the next GTA 6 mission won’t appear

Open Map and select the pink book to see pending Story objectives. Check the listed character and active objective before waiting for a phone call.

Find the next tracked objective

  1. Leave the previous activity and open Map → Story using the pink book. Track a main objective there. In launch coverage the main markers have black letters on pink.
  2. Open Progress → Story Recap to check what was completed. If a guide marks your next objective as character-specific, use that character once switching is available.
  3. Check the exact objective before deciding a call is overdue. An open activity, a prerequisite objective, and a missing trigger can all look like an empty map at first.

Where launch players got stuck

Swamped and Brian’s call
Finish Day Release, Clear Skies and Building a Life. As Jason, track Meet Brian and look for Brian and Lori in the downstairs hangar at Brian’s marina.
Vicki after Coffee Break
Finish Coffee Break, then clear the other pending main objectives. The recorded route gets Vicki’s call after clearing those jobs. Check Map → Story before waiting.
The motel after Afternoon Delight
Finish Afternoon Delight, then wait for Cal’s message and check the room at Starlet Motel for Move to Vice City. Check your actual Story list for the GTA$15,000 target in Prepare for Vice City. IGN reports that having this balance in BuckMe Checking can make the motel marker appear sooner. If you already have the money, look for remaining Story objectives, including ones that require Jason. See the motel and motorcycle route.
Cal and Feng Shui
After Island Dreams, check for Cal’s message and use the outside stairs at Jason’s house. Finish the furniture objective first. In the documented run you can leave Tenebrae Lux through its Exit Game menu or let the Street Trash 3 run end to return to the room. Progress comes from completing the scene with Cal.
House repairs
Track the house objective from the map. If the roof, steps and paint are done, check the deck near the workout equipment for the damaged railing. Use the objective list for any other repairs still pending.
Hey Arnold! is stalled
If Cal is still outside, check the live objective before driving away. A launch report describes him staying outside the liquor store. Retry the checkpoint if the game offers it.
The other character is greyed out
Open-world switching unlocks after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. In free roam, hold D-pad Right and use R1/RB once it is unlocked. If the other character is greyed out at home, try outside. Keep an earlier slot if the problem began after a side activity.
Halifax is not calling
Check the current Story objective and the active character first. A Chapter 3 launch report describes a stalled call. A repeatable solution has yet to be confirmed for that report.

If the objective still won’t change

Keep the slot that opens the older playable state. Before contacting support, write down the last completed mission, active character, current objective, platform, and whether an older save behaves differently. A repeatable trigger after a reload is more actionable than the length of time spent driving around.
