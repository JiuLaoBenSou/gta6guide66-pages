What to do with Lucia’s clothes in GTA 6

Collect the bag from Andy at Manatee’s Rest. If you take it by car, use your own vehicle’s trunk. The prison pickup can proceed without the optional bag.

Collect the clothes at the marina

  1. Track Get Lucia’s Clothes and go to Manatee’s Rest. Look for the marina sign and blue canopy by the water.
  2. Speak to Andy and follow her out to her car. Use the on-screen action to take the bag.
  3. If you’re driving, open the trunk of your personal vehicle and choose the clothes storage action. Check that the bag actually appears among the stored items before you leave.

If the clothes disappear

First check the vehicle you used. The game warns that clothes left in a vehicle you don’t own may be lost. Check the Pick Up Lucia map card. Bringing clothes is marked optional and the reception objective can continue without the bag.

If you want the clothes detour to count and you still have a save from before you collected them, use that slot to retry with your personal vehicle. Players report different results when the bag is carried by hand or stored. A reliable handoff at the prison has yet to be confirmed for every report.

See the optional clothes objective Screenshot source: GTA6DATA’s Coming Home walkthrough · Game imagery © Rockstar Games.

Pick up Lucia at the prison

Track Pick Up Lucia, drive to Hamlet Correctional Institution, and follow the reception marker to speak with the guard. Once the game gives a new waypoint, follow it. A hard deadline for the pickup has yet to be verified.

For the separate groceries task, take the marked items from the shop shelf and track the delivery objective. PowerPyx recorded payment at the shelf. If the items are gone and the shelf no longer lets you buy them, keep the save you have and use an earlier slot if you want to retry that optional errand.
