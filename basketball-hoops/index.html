Basketball hoops

Find all 100 reported hoop positions and the ball at each stop. To advance Story Mode 100%, make a basket at any 90 different hoops.

Shoot and check progressMariana CountyVice-Dale CountyKelly CountyLeonard CountyLummox County

Pick up the ball and make one basket

  1. Find the loose ball near the basket and use the pickup prompt. If you already picked it up, check the Items tab in the weapon wheel.
  2. Hold L2 on PlayStation or LT on Xbox to aim. Hold R2 or RT to charge the shot. Release the shot trigger when its ring lines up with the target ring.
  3. After a make, open Progress → Activities → Basketball. At a court with two hoops, score at both ends for two entries. Repeated shots into the same hoop can help the streak challenge.

If a shot won’t release, check that the ball is equipped in Items, keep holding aim, and line up from nearer the basket. If the ball rolls away, look around the court before moving to the next stop.

Start with Jason’s house in Key Lento, then use Map → Exploration → Points of Interest → Basketball Hoops to display nearby hoops you have discovered. The guide numbers follow the community catalogue. Use the landmarks to tell close pins apart. For the 90-hoop target, leave up to ten awkward stops for later.
