Where to find an early helicopter in GTA 6

Look for a Buckingham Maverick at the open dock in Southwind, by Sherwin Boatworks and Chicki’s Tiki Bar.

Get to the Southwind dock

  1. From Key Lento, take Sunbelt Highway (Route 1) west through Abismo Key toward Southwind.
  2. As the road bends north, look for the SBW (Sherwin Boatworks) and Chicki’s Tiki Bar signs. Turn into the parking lot by the signs.
  3. From the lot entrance, look east toward the open dock for the Maverick. What is parked there can vary when you arrive.

See the boatworks turnoff · See the Maverick dock Screenshot source: PC Gamer’s Southwind guide · Game imagery © Rockstar Games.

Nearby seaplane

From the same lot, face the water and look right, toward the south-side hangar and nearby dock, for a Mammoth Dodo.
