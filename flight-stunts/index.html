GTA 6 flight and parachute stunts

Add 267 air stunts to your Leonida checklist. Use a parachute for BASE jumps, fly under the marked bridges, and roll a plane through the marked city gaps.

  • Under the Bridge · 202
  • Knife Flights · 40
  • BASE Jumps · 25

Air stunts and the 130 road jumps share a completion total. You need any 250 for Cunning Stunts and any 300 for Story Mode 100%. If one gap is giving you trouble, choose another marker from the same list or switch categories.

Use the entries below to search Leonida Map. Keep directional suffixes such as North or West in your search when several markers are close together.

Under the Bridge

  1. Pick up a helicopter in Southwind or a small plane. Use a wide crossing for your first pass.
  2. Line up before reaching the bridge. Pass beneath the marked span while airborne. At a crossing with multiple named markers, reacquire the next marker before attempting another span.
  3. Fly clear and check the progress entry. If it stayed purple, compare the marker with the span you took before trying again.

Knife Flights in Vice-Dale County

  1. Take a plane and approach the named gap on a straight line. Give yourself space to roll before you reach the buildings.
  2. Roll until the wings are close to vertical, keeping your line through the middle of the gap. Roll back to level once the aircraft is clear.
  3. Look for credit in the tracker. If it did not count, check both the exact opening and the plane’s angle.

BASE Jumps

  1. Get and equip a parachute. A free pickup is at the top of the Badger tower in Key Lento.
  2. Reach the listed rooftop, mast or cliff. At buildings with multiple jumps, match the direction in the marker before stepping off.
  3. Open the chute and steer through the marked rings. Finish the course to earn progress. Its target time is an optional challenge.

Check whether a stunt counted

  1. Open Progress → Activities → Stunts and select the category. On Stunt Jumps, press the right stick to check your numbers by county.
  2. On the pause map, choose Exploration → Stunt Spots for the named entries. Purple means found. White means completed. Check the named entry before repeating a jump you already landed.
