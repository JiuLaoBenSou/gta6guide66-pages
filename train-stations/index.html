All 25 GTA 6 train stations

Use the blue Train Stations filter to choose a platform. For Rail Tour, ride one mile in each of Leonida’s five counties on the same in-game day.

Rail TourMariana CountyVice-Dale CountyLeonard CountyKelly CountyLummox County

How to attempt Rail Tour

  1. Reveal the areas first. In the pause map select Vehicles → Train Stations, then choose a station in each county before starting the timed day. Use the screenshots below to distinguish nearby city stops with similar names.
  2. At the station, wait for a train and board. Stay on the ride for a mile inside the county. Avoid the skip option, which the challenge excludes.
  3. Check Progress → Challenges → Exploration → Rail Tour after visiting all five counties. Millport or Grayson covers Lummox; Waning Sands is the one mapped Leonard stop. The whole route within a single day still needs an in-game timing check.

Mariana County · 2 stations

  • Amloco Railway

    Inland stop on the tracks beside the highway ramps and a small cluster of buildings.

  • Capri

    Look for the stop by the channel, near the bridge into Capri.

Vice-Dale County · 17 stations

  • Mule Airport Line

    On the airport grounds by the terminal roads and runways.

  • Mule City Line

    By the canal and the yellow court or playing field. Use this map image to distinguish the three Mule City Line markers.

  • Tequesta

    On the Tequesta street grid across the water from the seawall.

  • Mule City Line 2

    In the waterfront street grid by the blocks adjoining the bridge.

  • Downtown

    Near the Downtown road leading east over the water, at the end of the city grid.

  • Shoreline Park

    Follow the waterfront south toward the next bridge. The marker is next to the green area.

  • Downtown North

    Use the streets beside the green shoreline north of the Downtown stop.

  • Central Line

    Use the marker on the east side of the expressway in the central waterfront grid.

  • Mule City Line 3

    The third Mule City Line marker is in the rectangular blocks west of the waterfront park.

  • Courthouse

    On the city grid immediately below the east–west expressway near the coast.

  • Vice City Arts Center

    Near the waterfront park and the bridge approach. Check that the marker says Arts Center.

  • Crosstown

    At the curved junction in the street grid inland from the bay.

  • Central Line

    Use the separate Central Line marker beside the large oval stadium and the canal.

  • Vice City Airport Village

    In the grid along the northern side of the multi-ramp airport interchange.

  • Central Line 2

    By the freeway ramps and next to the canal. The game map uses the line name for this marker.

  • Vice City Central

    Use the named station on the inland side of the canal by the interchange.

  • Vice City Airport Central

    Close to Vice City Central and the same tangle of ramps. Select Airport Central from the map list to get the correct waypoint.

Leonard County · 1 station

  • Waning Sands

    Beside the main north–south road, west of the shoreline.

Kelly County · 3 stations

  • Ambrosia

    Just north of the major east–west road, by the industrial service tracks and storage tanks.

  • Port Gellhorn

    In the eastern part of town’s street grid, where the streets give way to open land.

  • Draper Island

    On the waterfront near the bridge onto Draper Island.

Lummox County · 2 stations

  • Grayson

    Between the tracks and the switchback roads at the edge of Grayson.

  • Millport

    Use the Millport grid near the coast, on the inland side of the beach.
