Pilot Pete’s delivery missions

After Pool Day in Chapter 4, switch to Jason and answer Pete’s call. Recover his plane at Sundown Private Airfield, then follow his texts to four more jobs.

  • Passing the Torch · Recover Pete’s Plane
  • Backseat Pilot · Wild Hog Airfield
  • Southpoint Airfield · 20:00 deadline
  • Stormy Weather · Avery Allen & Co.
  • Over and Out · Leonard County

Passing the Torch

  1. Track Recover Pete’s Plane in the Story list. Go to Sundown Private Airfield in Leonard County.
  2. Go through the red hangar door and pick up Pete’s KZ-59 Assault Rifle from the desk inside.
  3. Enter 3718 at the optional office safe near the desk.
  4. Fly through each of the three drop points and press Cross on PS5 or A on Xbox when the drop prompt appears. Land at the marked airstrip south of Port Gellhorn, park near the hangar and exit. The recorded BuckMe payment is GTA$12,000.

Backseat Pilot

  1. Wait for Pete’s next text and head to Wild Hog Airfield in Kelly County, near Port Gellhorn.
  2. Land the seaplane alongside the supplier on the water. Collect the packages and make seven drops in two in-game hours.
  3. Give the large red circles a wide berth to avoid an in-game wanted level. Return to the hangar. All seven on time paid GTA$20,000 in the recorded run.

Southpoint Airfield

  1. Pete offers Southpoint and Avery Allen after Backseat Pilot. You can do either next. For Southpoint, track the airfield off Boundary Road in the Grassrivers.
  2. Use the supplier’s bag prompt in the field. Make all seven drops by 20:00 game time and follow the return marker.

Stormy Weather at Avery Allen & Co.

  1. Follow Pete’s other marker to Avery Allen & Co. in Kelly County. Land next to the supplier on the water.
  2. When the HUD reads Stormy Weather, make the seven drops by 14:00 game time and follow the return marker.

Over and Out

  1. Finish Southpoint and Avery Allen and wait for another text. Track Deliver Packages in Leonard County.
  2. Pick up the shipment in the field, make the seven drops before 08:00 game time and follow the return marker.

Each of the four follow-ups paid GTA$20,000 when every drop was on time in the recorded run. Finishing Over and Out completes one strand for Strangers & Freaks. See the Story Mode 100% checklist for the completion counters.
