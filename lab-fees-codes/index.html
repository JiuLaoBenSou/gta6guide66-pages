GTA 6 Lab Fees Door Codes — 0420 and 7700

Use 0420 at the Moonstone Boutique lab and 7700 at Brian’s Lockup. Find both keypads and complete the bag deposit.

0420 · The lab at Moonstone Boutique

  1. Track Collect Brian’s Rent. At Moonstone Boutique in Artist Village, make the call to Brian at the entrance if prompted. Go in and speak to the clerk until you can access the rear room.
  2. Behind the counter, find the keypad on the green wall. Enter 0420 with the first zero. Pick up the marked acid at the red-lit workbench until the next call to Brian appears.

7700 · Brian’s Lockup at Isla Tropical

  1. Call Brian again and follow the new marker to the Isla Tropical private club. Use the fish mural in the lot to find the lockup door. Enter 7700.
  2. Inside, approach the strongbox next to the red tool chest. Use Deposit Narcotics with Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox. Check that the deposit objective clears.

If the objective stays open

If the lab still asks you to collect items, return to the workbench. If you opened the lockup but the delivery is still pending, use the strongbox inside. These codes belong to the two marked mission doors. For a robbery safe with a variable combination, use the clue in your current save.
