GTA 6 races and race challenges

Find all six racing groups, unlock their events and see which results count toward the trophy and Story Mode 100%.

Which results count?

  • Unique Race wins: 20 different wins for Earn Cash, Not Kudos, and 30 of 40 for Story Mode 100%.
  • Race Challenge medals: 10 of 13 golds for 100%. These time, score and stunt runs have their own counter.
  • Drone trials: Use the Top Fun Drones guide for the separate set of 42.

Unlock street and track races

  1. Vice City street races. After Dealer’s Choice, switch to Jason and read Manuel’s group message. Start Join a Street Race in Salton.
  2. Gellhorn Raceway. Run the Introduction to open Keep It PG. Place in its amateur events to open more layouts. The tested PS5 menu accepted road ratings from 500 to 780 for Keep It PG. If your car is ineligible, select one of the track rentals.
  3. Thrillbilly Mud Club. Follow Billy’s Check Out the Mud Club objective in North Kelly County. Once you have access to the races, select a dirt or oval event at the club.

Check Map → Activities → Racing for the blue markers. Drive near a venue if its marker has yet to appear. When the game locks the next event, finish the currently available one and recheck that venue’s menu. Your wins are in Progress → Activities → Racing.

All 40 unique races

Start the Salton race after Dealer’s Choice. Other starts are in La Perle, Ocean Beach and Gloriana Key.

  • Beach Blast
  • Freeway Run
  • Little Cuba Run
  • Mural Tour
  • Port Cruise
  • Rialto Run
  • Shoreline
  • The Tunnel
Animal Crackers · 8

Look for pairs of races at Grayson, Redshaw’s, Ranchers and Big Pine.

  • Farm Tour
  • Golf Tour
  • Landfill
  • Log Jam
  • Mountainside
  • Red Canyon Run
  • Strip Mine
  • Wetlands Circuit
Gellhorn International Raceway · 11

Go to the road circuit south of Port Gellhorn. Check the track’s event menu after each result to see which layout opened.

  • Intermediate Loop

Find the oval and figure-eight starts at the southwest edge of Hamlet.

Approach the circuit north of Ambrosia for its two demolition events.

  • Figure 8
  • Oval

All 13 Race Challenges

Start a trial from its blue marker. Check whether gold is determined by time or score on the event card. After a run, look at the medal under Progress → Activities → Race Challenges. Once you have ten golds, you can leave up to three challenges for later.

  • Ambrosia Raceway - Raceway Trial
  • Boone Speedway - Speedway Trial
  • Gellhorn Raceway - Dragway Trial
  • Gellhorn Raceway - Raceway Trial
  • Hamlet - BMX Stunt Run
  • Mount Kalaga - Cycling Trial
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club - Air Time Stunt Run
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club - Dirt Track Trial
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club - Drag Trial
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club - Hill Climb Trial
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club - Oval Trial
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club - Swamp Run Trial
