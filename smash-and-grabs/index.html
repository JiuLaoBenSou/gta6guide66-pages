All 35 Smash ’n’ Grab locations

Find all 35 Smash ’n’ Grab map cards by county. For Story Mode 100%, complete 34 different locations.

Unlock the jobs35 locationsCheck completion

Unlock pawn shops and jewelers

Pawn shops appear after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. Jewelry stores appear after Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs in Chapter 3. Reveal the surrounding area and select Map → Robberies → Smash ’n’ Grabs.

Why is the job still incomplete?

If the marker stays dark green after the display cases, check the staff area for a safe. Some shops have a security panel you can use before opening the cases. After leaving, recheck the map card and sell any jewelry at an available fence. The 100% checklist accepts 34 of the 35 jobs.
