Yago’s street racing missions

Play as Jason after Dealer’s Choice. Start with Manuel’s message and the Salton race, then follow Yago’s contacts until Back Up Yago in Little Cuba.

  • Join a Street Race · Salton
  • Bumper to Bumper · Mentor Yago
  • Race Against Yago · Limited time
  • Brother to Brother · Back Up Yago

Join a Street Race

  1. Finish Dealer’s Choice in Chapter 3 and read Manuel’s text as Jason. Track Join a Street Race at the Hit ’N’ Run VC marker in Salton.
  2. Talk to Santiago. The recorded menu offered three rentals from GTA$600 to GTA$900. Rent a car, finish the race and talk to Yago at the meet. First place paid GTA$3,000 before the rental cost.

Bumper to Bumper

  1. Wait for Mentor Yago in Little Cuba and stay on Jason. If the marker shows a lock, try later in the game day. An afternoon or evening start was shown in the published run.
  2. Take his car to the marked mod shop. Choose Pro Builds → Full Kits → Custom Street Racing Build, then Performance → Boost → Nitrous Boost. Yago pays for these upgrades.
  3. Return to the Salton meet, follow Yago into the race, finish it and clear the in-game search area. Follow the meetup marker in northern Vice City. The recorded payout is GTA$500.

Race Against Yago

  1. Watch for Race Against Yago after Bumper to Bumper. The hourglass means the opportunity is limited. Start it while the marker is visible. It appeared after a few in-game days in the published run.
  2. Talk to Santiago and choose Freeway Run, the second option in the documented menu. Select an eligible car. First place paid GTA$3,800 in the recorded run.

Brother to Brother

  1. After the follow-up texts, track Back Up Yago. Go around El Rincon Notorio in Little Cuba and use the rear door.
  2. Drive to the west Vice City marker, park beside the race car and follow Yago and Manuel. After the scene, collect the bag at the deal marker.
  3. Stay close to Yago on the way to the car. Follow his second car, then move to him when the objective reads Go to Yago. Completing the mission counts as one strand for Strangers & Freaks.
Ending and race car

Yago died even after the rescue input was completed in the recorded run. If you want to keep the remaining race car, follow the garage and registration prompts.

For the regular race calendar and licenses, see the street and track racing guide.
