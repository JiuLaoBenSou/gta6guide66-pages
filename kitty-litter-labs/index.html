Find the Kitty Litter Labs and finish Spin On This

As Jason, finish Here, Kitty Kitty in Chapter 3. Start with Tyrone’s first purple lab marker, then use the maps and entry clues below. For the trophy, follow the Grayson finale once it appears.

Start Take Out Kitty Litter

  1. Complete Here, Kitty Kitty. In free roam as Jason, check the texts from Tyrone or Dook and look under Map → Robberies → Take Out Kitty Litter for purple search areas.
  2. Find the building with pink or purple smoke. Yellow pipes, containers or cat graffiti can narrow it down. Check the windows and side alleys before trying a locked front door.
  3. Read documents or use the optional Question prompt before destroying the marked equipment. In the game, a lab can be cleared with fire or explosives. If you use the marked canister in the room, back away from the doorway before triggering it. Check BuckMe for Tyrone’s GTA$2,000 or GTA$2,500 payment.
  4. If you need another location, look for a follow-up text or revisit Tyrone or Dook. The marked labs remain available after the story. A burnt room can help you recognize a site you cleared earlier.

13 mapped Kitty Litter Labs

Clear the first marker, then group the Balsero and Crosstown stops to cut down on driving. Published guides cite 14 lab locations, but the list below has 13 individually mapped stops. Check the separate Santiago Avenue lead if your save still shows an open marker in Rockridge.

  • First marked lab · trailer

    Start with the first purple objective after Here, Kitty Kitty. In the southern row of the marked block, look for a trailer with yellow hoses sticking out of the windows. Pink smoke and the guards at the door identify the right one. Reward: GTA$2,000.

  • Balsero · NW 12th Avenue garage

    Look between NW 16th and NW 17th Streets for a little garage on the north side of the search zone. The chimney and purple containers are easier to spot than the door. Check the paper on the left just inside. Reward: GTA$2,000.

  • Balsero · central garage keypad

    Go to the roller door at the center of the circle. Enter 1999 on the wall keypad. The nearby billboard gives the number. Look for the receipt toward the left at the back before clearing the marker. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Balsero · southern trailer

    Go to the second trailer from the south in its search area. Look for the Spin on This van outside. Use the Snap Gun or Break Open prompt at the trailer door. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Central Rockridge · west alley

    Find the darker-roofed garage around the middle of the marked block. Circle around the building and enter from the alley on its west side. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Crosstown · NE 10th Street apartment

    Use the exterior stairs on the north side of the search zone. Find the upstairs door with containers outside it. Read the note on the kitchen counter if available. Back away after opening the door in case the person inside sets the room alight. Reward: GTA$2,000.

  • Crosstown · Cross My Heart Tattoos

    Use the tattoo shop sign at street level, then go behind the same building to its garage. Expect a guard outside the door. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Crosstown · east-facing garage

    Go to the building in the middle of this circle and check the door on its east side. If the cook offers a Question prompt, you can take the lead before clearing the objective. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Decor District · garage by the highway

    Find the building tucked beside the highway at the northwest edge of the search zone. Approach the garage and check for the lab prompt. You may be able to get another location from the cook. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Little Cuba · between NW 3rd Avenue and I-97

    Look for the long building near the center of the map marker. Check the parking spaces for discarded materials and use the door near the window with the pipe. Pick or force the lock when prompted. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Salton · SW 7th Avenue window

    Go around the east side of the large apartment building within the circle. Climb the dumpster below the open window. There are two people inside, so find the route back out before moving deeper into the room. Reward: GTA$2,000.

  • Tequesta · upper motel floor

    Choose the C-shaped motel on the east side of the marked area. Go upstairs and look for the locked room. Step clear when you open it so the cook’s first move cannot trap you inside. Reward: GTA$2,500.

  • Vice-Dale · trailer with an east window

    At the trailer in the middle of the search circle, climb through its east window. You can tell the person inside to leave. Check the counter near the equipment for the Rocky Chavez business card before finishing the objective. Reward: GTA$2,500.

Check Apartment 5 on Santiago Avenue

Push Square shows another approach in Rockridge. From Bump & Grind Coffee, go west along Santiago Avenue and check Apartment 5 for pipes beside the door. Compare this stop with the Central Rockridge marker in your own save. The two published runs don’t establish whether it credits a separate lab.

Finish Spin On This and claim the trophy

  1. After clearing enough labs, look for Shut Down the Labs outside Grayson in Lummox County. Bring armor and a longarm before approaching the diner. The scene can move you into Tyrone and Dook’s car as soon as you arrive.
  2. Drive east to the motel. While Tyrone talks to the clerk, take the stairs on the left and look for the guarded green door. Inspect the wooden hatch in the floor. If you kept quiet, return to Tyrone when prompted. If the clerk went down during the fight, take the key from the clerk upstairs and use it on the hatch.
  3. Follow Tyrone below, clear the room and use the mission prompt on both marked vats on the upper level. Watch the entry stairs for the next wave. When Tyrone goes down to the pipe, regroup at his marker.
  4. Follow Rocky through the pipe and save the fight for outside. After the exterior objective clears, return to Tyrone. Check the Spin On This! trophy or achievement and the reported GTA$10,000 finale payout.

The exact number of labs needed to unlock the finale is still unverified. If the diner marker is missing, clear an active lab and read the next text. For selling stolen goods instead, see Tyrone’s fence in La Perle.
