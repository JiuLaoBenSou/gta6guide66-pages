GTA 6 on Xbox Series X shuts off or crashes to Home

Check the Xbox power light and whether Home responds. Follow separate steps for a Series X shutdown, restart, or GTA 6 crash to Home.

Check the power light and Xbox Home

If the power light goes out or the Xbox startup animation returns by itself, follow the shutdown steps. If you land on Xbox Home and the Xbox button still opens the guide, follow the game crash steps. A black or green TV screen on its own does not identify the problem.

The Xbox shuts off or restarts

  1. With the console off, check that it sits securely in an open space and that nothing covers the vents. If you saw a temperature warning, let the console cool before restarting.
  2. If it shuts off again, fails during startup, or has done this in other games, stop trying to reproduce the fault and contact Xbox Support. Tell them whether the light went out, whether you saw a warning, and whether the console can reach Home.

GTA VI closes and Home still works

  1. If you can reach Story Mode free roam, open Pause → Game → Save Game and save in a new slot when the option is available.
  2. On Home, highlight GTA VI, press the controller’s Menu button, and choose Quit if shown. Check My games & apps → Manage → Updates and Settings → System → Updates. Restart the console.
  3. If only GTA VI exits again, send Rockstar the game version if visible, the last mission or action, the exact error code and whether Home still responds.

Launch reports describe both a complete shutdown after a brief green screen and separate crashes to Home. A shared cause and a patch that fixes both remain unconfirmed.
