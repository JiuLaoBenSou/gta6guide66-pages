Where to get a parachute

Get a free parachute on the Badger tower in Key Lento, or buy one at an Ammu-Nation that sells clothing.

Free parachute at the Badger tower

  1. In Key Lento, use Nifty Threads Islandwear as the landmark for the fenced Badger facility. Cross the gate and head to the tower behind the low building.
  2. Take the stairs and ladders to the control room. The SM-69 is on the outside balcony. For the parachute, climb the final ladder and look at the rail on the top platform.
  3. Check for the parachute pickup prompt before jumping. Other free boxes appear on some Vice City rooftops. A filmed guide also lists a parachute on the tallest peak in Mount Kalaga National Park.

Buy and check the gear

  1. Find a gun store with clothing stock and look for the parachute rack. The price can vary. Use the price shown at the rack.
  2. Before boarding, confirm you have the parachute. An aircraft pickup alone does not make the parachute available in every recorded flight.
  3. In the air, use the on-screen deployment prompt. After using the chute, check whether it needs to be added back to your inventory at the store.

For an early flight destination, see the Southwind helicopter route.
