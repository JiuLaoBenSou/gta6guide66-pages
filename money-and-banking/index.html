How to store and protect cash in GTA 6

Before BuckMe, use the floorboard stash in Jason’s Key Lento bedroom for pocket cash. After Buck Me, use the phone app to find an ATM.

Store cash under Jason’s bedroom floor

Go to the bedroom at Jason’s Key Lento house and look down at the floorboards until the open prompt appears. Open the stash and choose the amount to leave or take. This works with the cash on the active character before the bank account is available. Putting some aside keeps it out of the pocket balance you can lose if you die or are arrested in the game.

A loot bag is a carried item picked up at a job or activity. Some bags contain goods that go to a fence instead of an ATM. Use the bag’s inventory name and current objective to decide where it goes. If Jason drops it at the door or the stash has no option for it, check the in-game item prompt before treating it as pocket cash. There is no verified rule that every bag with cash can be moved directly into the floorboard stash.

Unlock BuckMe and make a deposit

  1. Complete Buck Me, the early mission that opens the account. Launch guides place the bank visit in Key Lento after Coming Home.
  2. Open the app’s ATM Finder to set a waypoint to a nearby machine. At the ATM use Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox.
  3. Choose Deposit or Withdraw. Before confirming, check the amount shown on the machine and use its on-screen adjustment prompts. Checking and pocket cash each have limits that affect the amount you can move.

Keep income separate from Family Savings

Checking is the account you use for ordinary spending. Family Savings is the shared milestone fund. Transfers to that fund are treated as one-way in the launch guide, so leave yourself cash or checking funds for purchases.

If your immediate goal is early spending money, see the Chapter 1 route to your first GTA$3,000. Check the destination for bags and other loot so you can turn in items you already have.

Chapter 1 spoiler · What happens to your money?

GTABase reports that Tourist Trap starts the chapter’s final run of missions. In its account, pocket cash, checking funds, and the floorboard stash are lost by the start of Chapter 2, while Family Savings up to GTA$5,000 carries through.

Keep a separate save before starting Tourist Trap and confirm the account category in the phone before moving money. The GTA 6 Guides team has yet to reproduce the exact transition rules on a console.
