GTA 6 Randy’s Wish List car locations

Start with the Zorrusso in Chapter 3 to unlock the Key Cloner and Randy’s board. Use the locations below to find the 15 requests listed after that story delivery, then check for later calls.

Unlock the Wish ListBase requestsStarfish Island garageLater jobs

Start with the Zorrusso and unlock the Key Cloner

  1. Complete Crosstown Traffic and choose Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS. Find the walled parking area at Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach. Check its east side at ground level.
  2. When Randy calls, install the Key Cloner from the phone prompt. At the vehicle, select Clone Key and hold the stick where the in-game meter turns pink until it fills.
  3. Drive to Leonida Auto World and follow Randy to the board after the handoff. Use Map → Robberies → Randy’s Wish List. Select an entry and read its location; ask Randy for a hint if your card still says Unknown Location.

The story car and 15 base requests

For a car you find away from a mission marker, scan it with WAiNK and look for the Wish List prompt. If a particular parking spot is empty, follow Randy’s current tip or look for the same model nearby. An active Advanced tracker can alert the in-game police when the car slows down.

Three cars in the same Starfish Island garage

From Waxman Island, follow the road to the northeast tip of Starfish Island. Find the waterfront house beside the oval turnaround. The green-walled garage is next to its driveway.

The captured run shows a dark Vitello, a red Itali RSX and a boxy Dubsta. Scan the exact car before taking it. The two sports cars showed Advanced trackers, while the Dubsta showed Standard. The source did not test the three deliveries from this garage.

Two later jobs to check after the base requests

¡Dale Mamba! · Mamba GT

When Randy adds this job after some deliveries, go to the Leaf Links mansion. The car is outside behind the right-hand building. Use the game’s Snap Gun interaction at the main house by the pool. The original key is in a bowl behind the sofa. After delivering it, wait for Randy’s follow-up call before trying Advanced-tracker registration at Pay ’n’ Spray.

Thrax Mansion · Truffade Thrax

When Randy calls about the final car, take the ferry from Waxman Island to Dalton Island. Look in the rear car park by the Dalton Island Club. Follow the mission’s ferry escape and final Bayside Drive Garage marker. Check for Supply & Demand after the final handoff.

Published lists differ on the total. The captured base menu shows 15 cars after the Zorrusso, and the Mamba and Thrax appear in later jobs. Other reports count 22 or mention additional models. Their status on the required list is still unverified; use your game’s live request list before treating an extra car as required.
