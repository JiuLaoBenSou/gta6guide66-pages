GTA 6 arcade and home console games

Start each of the four games for Story Mode 100%. Finish all levels of any one game for the Stick Fiddler trophy.

Three games on the home console

  1. At home, interact with the controller in front of the television to open JoySystem.
  2. Start Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III and Time Jackers Rewind. The story introduces the first two during Feng Shui. Check Time Jackers separately.
  3. If the controller prompt is unavailable in Chapter 2, try a home console again in Chapter 3 or during post-story free roam.

Read the full Tenebrae Lux walkthrough

Bang For Your Buck in Port Gellhorn

Find Bargain World in Port Gellhorn. Check outside the clothing store for the cabinet with the Bang For Your Buck prompt. Other cabinets exist around Leonida, so confirm the game name before starting.

Check the trophy and 100% counters

Use Progress → Activities → Games to check which titles you have played. You can exit after each game begins to fill that list. To get Stick Fiddler, pick one game and clear its final level.
