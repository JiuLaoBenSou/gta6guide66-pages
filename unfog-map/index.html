Uncover the map and find an aircraft

Use an aircraft in free roam to uncover several areas in one trip. Check the map between passes so you don’t miss narrow dark sections.

Find a crop duster in Chapter 1

  1. Leave Key Lento toward the swamps in Mariana County. Look for the short runway with a hangar and check outside it for a crop duster.
  2. If the first strip is empty, check the second Mariana County airstrip.

    Use the Kelly County strip to swap planes after flying through the southern regions.

  3. Press Down on the D-pad to bring up the fuel gauge if it is enabled in the HUD settings. When the gauge turns yellow, plan your next landing before continuing. Make a manual save between regions.

For a helicopter or seaplane, follow the Southwind boatworks route.

Reveal the dark parts in passes

  1. Fly over a visible dark section of the map after the story releases control.
  2. Open the map and check how much filled in. Pick a new landmark beyond the remaining dark patch.
  3. Check the terrain and leave enough landing space for the aircraft you chose.

Get a parachute before flying if you may need to jump out. Use the flight stunt guide to find named bridge crossings, knife-flight gaps and BASE jump starting points.
