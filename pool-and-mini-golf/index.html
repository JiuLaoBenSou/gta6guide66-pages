Pool and mini golf

Win against five different pool opponents and finish rounds at two mini-golf courses for Story Mode 100%.

Pool opponentsMini golf courses

Beat any five pool opponents

  1. Stand by a player at the table and select Play Pool. At The Rusty Anchor, the entry stake was GTA$5 in the documented run.
  2. Use the cue-ball view to line up. On PS5, hold R2 to build power and let go to take the shot. Clear your assigned group first and leave the 8-ball for last.
  3. After the win, open Progress → Activities → Sports → Pool. Pick a different opponent for your next win. Solo tables are useful for practice.
  • The Rusty Anchor · Key Lento. Enter the main bar and go left toward the pool table. Approach the player with the cue.
  • Skeeter’s · Hamlet. On the south side of Hamlet, check the patio behind the bar.
  • Zotico’s · Balsero. Find the bar on Balsero’s west edge. Look along the east wall for the table.
  • Delights · Port Gellhorn. Walk toward the central area inside the club. If the club is closed after Chapter 2, return when it reopens.
  • Kowaachobee Cafe & Bar · Buckskin Swamp. Pull off at the roadside cafe and look on the west side of the building.
  • The Thirsty Wolf · Belville. Find the bar northwest of town. The table is on the north side.
  • Jacob’s Tavern · Millport. Head to the bar near the eastern Pay ’n’ Spray and check its north side.

Finish rounds at two mini-golf courses

Track the course card in the map’s Sports filter. Complete a round and check that course’s entry under Progress → Activities → Sports. Leaf Links and Mount Hailes have separate full-size golf entries.

  1. King Gator Adventure Golf · Buckskin Swamp. Go to the gator-themed course off the highway. Choose 9 or 18 holes from the course prompt.
  2. Frontier Adventure Golf · Waning Sands. Look by the looped track. The course offers 9 or 18 holes.
  3. Dino Golf Challenge · Frogswater. Pull off at the old roadside dinosaur course. The course card asks you to play all 18 holes and lets you tackle them in any order.

Find basketball hoops for Story Mode 100%.
