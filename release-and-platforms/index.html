GTA 6 platforms, release date and editions

Rockstar launched GTA 6 on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One players can check Xbox Cloud Gaming for streaming access.

Where can you play?

  • Native console install. Use a PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X, or Xbox Series S. Confirm the correct edition on your console’s store.
  • Xbox One streaming. Xbox announced cloud play for Xbox One in supported markets. GTA 6 is a separate purchase. Your streaming entitlement, device and region determine availability. In some markets Xbox also sells cloud playtime.
  • PC version and requirements. The current Rockstar launch announcement lists only the native console versions. Check the official site for future PC details, including the release date, exclusivity window and system requirements.

What do the editions cost?

The U.S. store listings checked for this guide show $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate. Both include the base game. Ultimate adds extra items that unlock across the story. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade through PlayStation or Microsoft after redeeming the base game if needed. Use your regional store for the live price, taxes and item details.

Where are the official game and support pages?

Rockstar’s game page links to trailers, editions and the official people and places guide. For a download, storage or startup problem, use the support path for your console.
