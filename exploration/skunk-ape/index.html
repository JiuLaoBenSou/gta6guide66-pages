Find the Skunk Ape and finish Kirk’s quest

For Kirk’s mission, start with a Skunk Ape flyer and call him. For the reported free-roam sighting, check the swamp road south of Hamlet. The two routes have different triggers.

Follow the reported swamp sighting

Start on the wooded road by Cottonmouth Lake, south of Hamlet. Check the eastern bank near the road crossing. If you see a figure crouched over a deer carcass, slow down and get out. Press both sticks to enter Focus when you hear the growl, and follow the sound ripples on foot into the trees. Look for the effigy with green mist. A daytime sighting is documented, while the repeat conditions remain unconfirmed.

Reveal what to do at the green mist

Stay close until your character passes out. The encounter plays a short scene, then returns you to free roam nearby.

Start Skunk Apeology 101

From Chapter 1, enter the Rusty Anchor in north Key Lento, turn right and inspect the flyer on the noticeboard behind the front door. Another flyer is up at Skeeter’s in Hamlet. Call Kirk from Contacts. His voicemail directs you toward Thorpe Adventures in Pelican Valley after dark. Either character can start this stage.

Follow the drones to Kirk’s truck

Track the KK marker near Pelican River. The recorded meeting took place around 10 p.m. in-game. Follow the drone lights to Kirk’s truck. Talk to him, hit the truck with the stick he gives you and follow him into the woods after the response.

Equip the flashlight from the item wheel. In the pink search circle, use Focus to follow the white sound ripples. The feeding site is close to the center and the claw marks are on a tree just east of it. Follow the chase to the next effigy and inspect it. If the giant boar appears, run toward the truck marker. The recorded run continued from the truck even when the boar caught up.

Inspect three trees for Kirk

After Search for the Skunk Ape, wait for Kirk’s group message. One recorded run got it after about half an in-game day. Track Search for Tree Effigies and use a boat between banks. Any three of the nine effigies count. Near a trunk, use Focus to spot the effigy, then follow the study prompt with L2 or LT. Check the group chat after each find.

  • Central crossing. From the open patch beside Kirk’s marker, cross north to the little wooded island.
  • Thorpe Adventures. Find the property by Stillwater Path. Search the opposite bank directly to its south.
  • South channel. Where the small islands meet the broader channel, check just inside the southern edge of the marked search area.

After three inspections, Kirk’s reply confirms you can leave.

Start Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove

  1. Wait for Kirk’s next text, then track the KK marker southeast of Cottonmouth Lake at night. Recorded runs show both Jason and Lucia. Take the rifle from Kirk before you board the helicopter.
  2. Find the figure in the searchlight. Click the right stick to use the scope and land several tranquilizer hits until Kirk calls for a landing.
  3. After landing, step onto the dock by the overturned boat. Turn right toward the barrels under the orange light. Enter the shack beside the barrels and follow the interaction prompt at Mr. Mangrove.

Find all three fly swarms

  1. As the frog, use Focus at the shack door. Hop to the first carcass just outside and use the eat prompt until the swarm clears.
  2. Go around the right side of the alligator pen. Continue past the firepit and picnic table to the second swarm under the dock light.
  3. Keep right past the broken planks and cross the short bridge. Head toward the yellow light on the left near the airboat to find the last swarm.
  4. When the camera changes and you become a fly, follow the helicopter light across the water. Approach Kirk and Noah to finish that sequence and return to the shack.

Ending choices and rewards

Kirk gives you the Mister Mangrove Laird 350 during the helicopter stage. You keep it in your collection after each of the documented endings.

Reveal the three ending choices
  • Side with Kirk. Shoot Mr. Mangrove and check BuckMe after the scene. IGN’s run recorded GTA$10,000. Another published run showed GTA$5,000.
  • Side with Mr. Mangrove. Shoot Kirk and let the scene finish. Mr. Mangrove carries him away. IGN’s run paid no cash for this ending.
  • Wait without firing. Let the standoff play out to see the third documented ending.
