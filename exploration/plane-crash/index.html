Plane crash random encounter

During free roam, watch for a low aircraft trailing black smoke around the swamps on the road to Hamlet. Follow the smoke to the wreck while the encounter is active.

Follow the plane and the smoke

One published run saw the plane while driving toward Hamlet from the Keys. When you hear the failing engine, use Focus to help locate the sound. Follow the smoke off the road and look for the wreck in the marsh. A fixed spawn point and a repeat schedule remain unverified.

Check for the pilot and the cargo

  1. Check the crash area for a rival group that may arrive for the cargo. Follow the in-game prompts and check the ground around the fuselage once the area is clear.
  2. Look for a downed pilot nearby. If he is alive and has a Talk prompt, speak to him before leaving. GameWith recorded a message for Jimmy at a hangar in Ambrosia and a +1% Focus popup. A different run found cash and a flare gun by the pilot.
  3. Collect the accessible packages around the wreck and look for an available buyer who accepts narcotics. AlcastHQ recorded three bags and sold them to Brian. Check the buyer’s offer in your game for the amount.

Sources
