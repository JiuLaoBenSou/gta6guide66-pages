Find the radio tower egg and other hidden references

The literal painted egg is on the Bluegrass radio mast by Vice City International Airport. The same launch footage also shows a GTA V callback at Jason’s house, Trinity Apartments and more discoveries around Leonida.

Climb the Bluegrass radio mast

  1. Find Bluegrass by Vice City International Airport. Look just below the canal for the rectangular field roads. The approach branches off their eastern edge.
  2. Follow the dirt spur to the fenced utility lot at the foot of the tall red-and-white mast. Cross the fence, take the stairs and climb the ladder sections inside the mast.
  3. At the upper platform, look by the rail for the nest and the Easter Egg prompt. Allow several minutes for the climb. The platform with Base Jump is above the nest.
Reveal how to start the scene

Take an egg, aim away from the platform and throw it over the rail. On PlayStation the filmed prompts show L2 to aim and R2 to throw. The scene shows the egg hitting a car windshield.

See Los Santos on the boat’s GPS

At Jason’s first house in Key Lento, look for the white-and-blue boat propped up in the yard. Climb aboard and look at the small screen near the wheel. Bring the binoculars closer to the screen or zoom in with the phone camera. The display shows the GTA V map. The footage supports a reference to Michael’s stolen boat, with the ownership connection left implicit in the game.

Find Trinity Apartments and the boat in Vice Beach

Go to Washington Island near Vice Beach Top Fun Drones. Look for the Trinity Apartments sign on the waterfront block. The door shown in the footage stays closed. Go up to the roof for the props by the circular roof structure and check the dock for the Lure Predator L35P, the boat resembling Dexter’s Slice of Life.

Trigger the UFO northwest of Starlet Motel

  1. Go alone to the road northwest of Starlet Motel in Kelly County around 2:00 a.m. in-game. The encounter has been seen shortly before and after that time.
  2. Look for a car with blinking lights by the road. If it is absent, drive away and return. When it appears, take the path near it into the woods and walk toward the blue light.
  3. Speak to the woman and offer to help. Return to the roadside car to finish the encounter and unlock The Truth Is Out There.
Continue the UFO chain through the dream and lab

Get the door code from the dream

After the abduction and Chapter 2’s Coffee Break, sleep in a bed with the character who met the UFO. If no dream starts, sleep again with that character. In the white room, turn around for the dark passage. Crouch and keep left through the eggs. Interact with Earth in the model solar system and follow the passage that opens.

At the keypad, count the four clusters of lines above it from left to right. Each count gives one digit. Keep that number for the lab door. The photographed runs show different codes, so use your own clue.

Open the three rooms

Activate the console at its lit button. Number the four buttons from left to right. Press 4 → 3 → 4 → 3 → 1. Collect the milk from the left room and put it into the machine in the right room. Skip the middle room and let the dream end.

Find the lab in Grassrivers

Look for the fenced site east of Galette Wetlands, south of Hamlet. Clear the locks on the front gate and take the right-hand path to the building. The keypad down the stairs takes the number from your dream. Ride the elevator down and repeat the four-button sequence and the milk transfer. When you free the alien, take it straight to the elevator to escape the gas. Outside, the UFO rescue scene awards increases to maximum health, stamina and Focus.

Find the haunted Marbelle Hotel and open its safe

  1. Go to NW 8th Street in southern Crosstown, west of Panos Plaza and close to Nine1Nine. Match the boarded-up frontage and the For Sale sign. Take the path around the building and use the rear entrance.
  2. Go upstairs. When the door opens by itself, cross the threshold and wait for the lights and red markings to finish. Follow the stairs to the roof and read the old note for the full six-digit code.
  3. On the way down, check the lit cabinet opposite the stairs for the Ratface card from ED Comics Universe. Back on the ground floor, turn left from the stairs and enter 955214 at the safe. Collect the cash and watch to clear the fog. This can also unlock The Truth Is Out There if you have not earned it from the UFO.

Find the Pet Cemetery near Grayson

Follow SR-73 south of Grayson in Lummox County. Leave the cracked paved road on foot and follow the narrow dirt track into the woods. Look for a clearing with low graves ringed by piles of stones. The scan was recorded in daylight.

Point your phone’s WAiNK scanner at the graves. Look for Pet Cemetery on the result card, then check Curiosities in the Progress menu.

Get a ride to Belpoint with Cool Dog

  1. In free roam, head into open water. Try Gambit Bay north of Key Lento, where a player filmed the encounter, or the Gulf west of Vice City, where a separate run reached the same beach.
  2. Get out of the boat and swim. Turn toward the barking and look for a dog in goggles at the controls of a jet ski. Use the ride prompt and stay aboard for the trip to the dog beach on Belpoint.
  3. On the beach, you can look for the Dog Island wallet. The ride has been recorded with Jason and Lucia. If the dog is absent, try another stretch of open water. The reported runs give no reliable timer.

Survive the Circles of Hell encounter

Look for the carved throne at a mountain campsite in western North Kelly County. Once the encounter starts, extinguish the four blue-green torches by defeating enemies, then attack the boss while its health bar is red. See the campsite map, trigger and Hellspawn Comshot 12 pickup for the full route.

Recognize the Mount Chiliad camper

Look around grid C5 on the printed Leonida map. Use MacAlister Gold Mine as the western landmark and US-628 as the eastern landmark. In the recording, the pickup approaches a left bend as the minimap changes to Moreau. Look for a wide gravel turnout on the water side of the road, with a left-curve chevron beyond the camper.

Look for a left-bend chevron beside a wide gravel turnout. The turnout is on your right on that approach, with open water below and a rock wall across the pavement. The camper has a large painted eye and handwritten lettering along the driver’s side. The sighting was filmed in daylight.

Where the camper’s QR code leads

The QR sticker beside the eye encodes rsg.ms/qr1. On November 21, 2026, it redirected to Rockstar’s fictional SEIZED page outside the game. An in-game unlock from scanning it remains unverified.

Use the Grady’s Family Store map pin on Rockledge Road as a starting landmark and check the broad bend to the west. The pin marks the store. Compare the turnout with the footage to identify the camper. The exact road segment and any repeatable in-game appearance time still need confirmation.

If you’re at the broken bridge, look in the water for Wyman’s Mamba GT wreck. The cream-and-red mobile home on the Moreau River’s south bank is the spot for the two pistol variants.

Open the Mount Kalaga firewatch shed

  1. Find the steel firewatch tower near the summit in Mount Kalaga National Park. Climb to the lookout and read the note on the board for the shed code.
  2. Go back down and use the keypad on the wooden shed near the stairs. The filmed code is 69185. If your keypad rejects it, check the in-game note again.
  3. Inside, look at the logbook on the table and turn the page for the sketch. A follow-up objective has yet to be documented.

Scan the Monument of Leonidan Supremacy

Drive north from Port Gellhorn on SR-73. In northern Manifest Canyon, take the short side path west of the road to the globe-topped monument. Use WAiNK to register it under Sculptures. If the controller buzzes and you hear a beep, check for the scan alert. The player who first reported the beeping later identified it as the WAiNK notification.

After the scan, inspect the plaque at the base and the symbols on the horse pedestals. Players reported that a lightning strike set the globe alight during a storm. A follow-up objective and any connection to the Moreau camper remain unverified.

Find the pineapple bus stop on Cicada Avenue

Look along Cicada Ave in Southside, Vice City, near the highway interchange for a yellow pineapple-shaped shelter with green leaves on its roof. The utility pole and green verge help match the footage. The shape resembles SpongeBob’s house.

The same clip also shows the RX Men comic at the 247 in Key Lento. Look in the magazine rack for the cover labeled Issue 13.

Check the hidden garage in Belville

  1. Find the northern row of homes in Belville, Leonard County, west of Andy’s house. On the middle stretch of the row, approach the second house from the west and check the garage interaction.
  2. Look for the Illuminaughty Purple card pinned to the board on your right and the Foiled magazine on the bed. Inspect the notes in the garage for the hidden story.
  3. Check below the bed for a reported meteorite chunk. Verify an item prompt before counting it. The video’s later trunk shot is a different view from the garage’s pickup.

Find Michael’s ringtone and the Race & Chase joke

On your in-game phone, open Settings → Ringtone → Wind Chimes for the tune heard on Michael’s phone in GTA V. In the social feed, look for posts joking that Barnyard Blue arrived before Race & Chase 7. That post may rotate out of the feed.

Scan the Oceanarium’s Mermaid Display

At the Catalan Key docks, go around the Vice City Oceanarium to its loading area. Enter the warehouse and check the room at the back. Aim WAiNK at the tank with the mermaid and humanatee, then look for Mermaid Display in Curiosities.

For the protest scene, check the Oceanarium’s street entrance. The launch clip shows people on the sign above a ladder. A fixed time or chapter requirement has yet to be verified.

Join Dodos Rising and complete Sisters of the Sea

  1. As Jason, finish Brian’s early rent job and the early Mandy objectives. After sleeping at home, check the map for Check Out the Protest at the Vice City Oceanarium. If the purple question mark is absent the next day, check again after more in-game time passes.
  2. At the entrance, find Rosebud, the green-haired woman photographing someone in an orca costume. Agree to help and give her your number. Read her text when it arrives and track Sisters of the Sea.
  3. Follow Rosebud inside. When the crystal drops into the manatee pool, dive to the bottom and press both sticks to use Focus. Swim to the white glow, pick up the crystal and return to Rosebud.
  4. Follow her back out and take the orca transport truck. Drive to the coast marker, park in the marked spot and interact with the back of the truck.
Reveal Rosebud’s ending and the mission reward

The orca attacks Rosebud after she enters the water. The scripted scene completes the mission without a cash payout. If police are still on you, leave the truck and look for a faster vehicle on the highway.

Play the JoySystem games and find the arcade cabinet

Use the controller next to the TV at home to open the JoySystem. Pick Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III or Time Jackers Rewind. It is available in Chapter 1 and returns from Chapter 3. Find Bang For Your Buck at an arcade cabinet, including outside Bargain World in Port Gellhorn. Start each game once for the 100% checklist. Finish all levels in one for Stick Fiddler.

For the swamp scene in the same footage, follow the Skunk Ape free-roam route. For the laboratory sequence, use the crab outpost steps. For the airfield detour, see the Mile Zero Air Station guide.
