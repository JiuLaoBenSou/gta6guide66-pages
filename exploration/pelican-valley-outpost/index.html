Solve the Pelican Valley crab outpost

Find the stilt buildings in the southwest Pelican Valley marshes, southwest of Hamlet and south of Watson Bay. The central house has the computer, the detached lab has the specimen list and the dock leads to the locked boathouse.

Restore powerCrab encounterBoathouse

Turn on the lights and read the computer

Start outside the main house. Follow the exterior wall to the grey breaker box by the coolers, then restore power. Inside, enter the bedroom through the right-hand rooms. Inspect the writing on the floor near the torn rug before returning to the terminal.

Reveal the computer password

Choose CLEAVES. Read the journal and the green safe clue before closing the computer.

What happens when you leave the terminal?

Finish reading the journal, then exit the terminal. Launch footage shows a crab encounter in the room. Follow the game’s on-screen struggle prompt to regain control, then walk to the detached lab.

Open the safe and the dock gate

Find the clipboard on the lab table. The terminal’s Double Success clue means you need to double the ID of the specimen marked Success. Use the result in the main-house safe. Read the emergency note inside before going down the pier to the boathouse.

Reveal both lock codes and the reward

Successful specimen 0036 gives safe code 0072. Keep both leading zeroes. The emergency note gives boathouse code 0212. Look inside for the Crabshack VF5 SMG and the stash bag. The reported GTA$10,000 is the face value of marked bills in the bag. The safe contains a separate cash take, and the amount you receive for fencing the marked bills can differ.

For the figure in the woods shown in the other half of the 3DM guide, follow the Pelican Valley Skunk Ape sighting.
