GTA 6 nightclub locations

If the name you saw was “919,” look for NINE1NINE next door to The Vault. Delights and Jack of Hearts are at separate locations.

NINE1NINE · Downtown Vice City

Head into Downtown from the Route 97 side. Locate The Vault near the large west-side interchange, then go to the neighboring entrance with NINE1NINE on the sign. Look for the name on the building before setting your waypoint.

Delights · Port Gellhorn

Look for the Delights sign above the red double doors in Port Gellhorn. If a mission sends you there, use its marked entrance. If you are visiting in free roam and the club is closed after Chapter 2, return once it reopens later in the story.

Jack of Hearts · Crosstown

Head north of Downtown into Crosstown. If the bouncer blocks entry, advance the Vice City story and follow the objective that introduces the club. The upstairs recording booth is also a stop on The Sniper of La Perle playlist route.

Get there by taxi

Set a waypoint near the entrance, then call Paradise Taxi from your phone contacts or use RydeMe once you have it. In the back seat, hold L2 or LT to talk to the driver. Press Triangle or Y to skip the drive for an extra GTA$100. The 3DM-linked clip demonstrates this shortcut on the way to NINE1NINE.
