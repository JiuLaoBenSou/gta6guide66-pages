Find the float helicopter in Pelican Valley

Look for a white helicopter on dark floats at the Pelican Valley stilt-house docks. The original footage shows the boat approach, a takeoff from the water and the matching in-game map.

Check the Pelican Valley water landing

  1. Open the map over Pelican Valley. Match the stilt-house cluster on the northeast edge of the large marsh island, beside the narrow channel that bends south.
  2. Approach by boat and stop short of the dock. The footage shows a white cabin, one main rotor and two dark floats. Deal with any hostile NPCs before entering the water.
  3. Swim alongside one of the floats and use the vehicle entry prompt. The recorded flight takes off from the water by the dock.

Check the aircraft’s model

The compact rotorcraft on floats resembles the Nagasaki Sea Sparrow listed in PC Gamer’s in-game cheat menu. The dock footage does not show a readable helicopter name. To check your own find from the dock, hold D-pad Up to open WAiNK and use R1 or RB to scan the aircraft.

Brian’s Dodo is the fixed-wing seaplane used in Gone Fishing. If the float helicopter is missing, the repeat spawn conditions remain unconfirmed. Use the early Southwind helicopter route if you only need to get airborne.
