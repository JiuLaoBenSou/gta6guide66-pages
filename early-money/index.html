Earn and keep early cash

Collect pay the game has already credited, check what a fence buys, and choose where to keep cash before the Chapter 1 transition.

First GTA$3,000Shirley’s rumHidden findsConvenience-store routeNova Auto Junkyard safeChapter 1 payoutsStore your cash

Collect Chapter 1 payouts

  1. Finish an available job such as Lab Fees, or take an early side activity you can complete at your current marker.
  2. When a contact says they left your cut, check the mailbox by the entrance gate at Jason’s Key Lento home. Job completion and the payment notification can arrive at different times.
  3. If you picked up goods instead of cash, check the fence marker. Bring the goods to the requested drop-off and wait for the payment message. With Brian, learn the Lock Up code and use the correct delivery location.

Find cash behind Three Sheets Liquor

  1. Find the gap in the fencing down the side alley at Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento.
  2. Once you can approach the cages, interact with the red macaw.
  3. Check the vent panel by the generator and the small safe in the building for separate cash pickups.

For other shop or RV safes, check the current location for its own clue. If you find a collectible card, confirm its name and check the fence or pawn price before deciding whether to keep it.

On a keypad, use the visible Notes or the clues from the current location. A number from an online farming clip may belong to a different safe.

Find hidden cash, pickups and oddities

Start with the two spots at Jason’s Key Lento house. The bedroom floorboard is where Jason keeps cash. The mailbox by the front gate is where you collect separate job payments. For another cash stop nearby, check the parrot and back-room safe at Three Sheets Liquor.

  • Dog Island wallet. Swim in open water off western Key Lento. If a dog in goggles pulls up on a white jet ski, use the ride prompt. Stay aboard until you reach the island with other dogs. Follow the dogs along the sand and check the wallet pickup. The encounter and payout can vary.
  • Cards and sunken coins. For a named card, use the card pickup guide. For the detector and the first two Gold Doubloons caches, follow Kenton’s Island Dreams dive.
  • Fishing Shacks and Mile Zero. Check the Fishing Shacks weapon pickup if the operation has appeared. The Mile Zero military vehicles are a separate detour with a wanted risk.
  • Fountain mansion garage. Find Starfish Island above the word County in the Vice-Dale County map label. On its east side, look for the driveway with the large courtyard fountain. Check the garage for sports cars and a Dubsta SUV. Use WAiNK to check each car’s entry method and tracker.
Reveal the tower and meteorite finds
  • For the nest above Bluegrass, follow the radio mast route.
  • On Mount Kalaga, look near Hillside Campsite southwest of Dawson Dam. Watch for purple haze around a shallow crater and use the Meteorite pickup prompt. Follow the meteorite routes for Belville, Selkie Hole and the offshore islands for more pickups.

Try the convenience store’s back entrance

  1. In GTA 6 free roam, circle the store to see whether the rear door offers an interaction. The recorded route starts at a red shutter beside a Loading Area sign and continues through the storage rooms.
  2. Check the manager’s office for the case on the desk. Use the displayed game prompt. If you find a keypad on a different shop run, check the local safe clue or the robbery safe guide.
  3. After taking the loot, leave by the available exit. If you see a wanted alert, follow the search-zone guide. A low-profile entrance can still end with a police response.

Work toward your first GTA$3,000

  1. If you have Shirley’s errand active, collect her bottle before starting Strange Noises at Three Sheets.
  2. For cash, check the parrot stash. The back yard is accessible in free roam even if you skip Shirley’s errand. A filmed run collected just over GTA$2,000 across the vent, safe and loose cash. The total can vary.
  3. Pick up credited job pay from Jason’s mailbox, or sell the goods from Nova Auto Junkyard to a fence if the route is open. Count only the cash your save actually received toward GTA$3,000.

Get Shirley’s Rum before Three Sheets closes

  1. After collecting Mandy’s rent in Pipework, head back toward Jason’s Key Lento house along Peach Palm Drive. When you hear Shirley call, click both sticks for Focus. Approach the highlighted neighbor and accept the favor.
  2. Follow the marker to Three Sheets Liquor, east of Donnie’s trailer. Speak to the clerk and choose Shirley’s rum. Pick the Salty Squid bottle up from the counter before starting the separate Strange Noises operation, which can temporarily close the store.
  3. Follow the SB marker back to Shirley and hand her the bottle. The favor counts as an Act of Kindness for the Criminal Profile. Complete it before the Chapter 1 departure sequence.

Find the Nova Auto Junkyard safe in Chapter 1

  1. Track the M7 operation at Nova Auto Junkyard in Hamlet. Look for the garage with the connected side room.
  2. Check the clue on the wall by the safe. The walkthrough shows 7777. Take the cash and check the nearby worktables for the separate goods.
  3. Use the current fence or drop-off marker to sell those goods. A successful delivery and the mailbox payment are separate events.

Check which routes repeat

After a junkyard run, check the operation marker again during free roam. If it displays Gang temporarily disbanded, leave and come back after the activity reopens. Compare the new safe and table contents before assuming the same payout. The opening cash-loop clip begins once Jason has a vehicle and can leave the safehouse. It does not establish an unlimited payout across builds.

For the metro route, the player clip shows a small in-game payout after the train departs. Check the wanted indicator in your own save, and use a job or a fence payout if the loop does not repeat.

Keep spending money separate

Before the bank unlock, aim down in Jason’s Key Lento bedroom to find the floor stash. Confirm the deposit or withdrawal prompt before you leave. Follow the bank and Family Savings options once BuckMe is available. When the chapter is about to change, see the guide to what stays accessible and make a separate manual save.
