Earn and keep early cash

Collect pay the game has already credited, check what a fence buys, and choose where to keep cash before the Chapter 1 transition.

First GTA$3,000Nova Auto Junkyard safeChapter 1 payoutsStore your cash

Collect Chapter 1 payouts

  1. Finish an available job such as Lab Fees, or take an early side activity you can complete at your current marker.
  2. When a contact says they left your cut, check the mailbox by the entrance gate at Jason’s Key Lento home. Job completion and the payment notification can arrive at different times.
  3. If you picked up goods instead of cash, check the fence marker. Bring the goods to the requested drop-off and wait for the payment message. With Brian, learn the Lock Up code and use the correct delivery location.

Find cash behind Three Sheets Liquor

Find the gap in the fencing down the side alley at Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento. Once you can approach the cages, interact with the red macaw. Check the vent panel by the generator and the small safe in the building for separate cash pickups. See GTA BOOM’s fence, macaw and stash screenshots.

For other shop or RV safes, check the current location for its own clue. If you find a collectible card, confirm its name and check the fence or pawn price before deciding whether to keep it.

On a keypad, use the visible Notes or the clues from the current location. A number from an online farming clip may belong to a different safe.

Work toward your first GTA$3,000

  1. As Jason in Chapter 1, listen for Shirley outside the house after the opening rent jobs. Track Get Shirley’s Rum and ask the Three Sheets clerk for the bottle. Deliver it before leaving Chapter 1.
  2. For the separate cash, check the parrot stash. A filmed run collected just over GTA$2,000 across the vent, safe and loose cash. The total can vary.
  3. Pick up credited job pay from Jason’s mailbox, or sell the goods from Nova Auto Junkyard to a fence if the route is open. Count only the cash your save actually received toward GTA$3,000.

Find the Nova Auto Junkyard safe in Chapter 1

  1. Track the M7 operation at Nova Auto Junkyard in Hamlet. Look for the garage with the connected side room.
  2. Check the clue on the wall by the safe. The walkthrough shows 7777. Take the cash and check the nearby worktables for the separate goods.
  3. Use the current fence or drop-off marker to sell those goods. A successful delivery and the mailbox payment are separate events.

See the original safe and garage screenshots.

Find the Dog Island wallet

Swim in open water off western Key Lento. If a dog in goggles pulls up on a white jet ski, use the ride prompt. Stay aboard until you reach the island with other dogs. Follow the dogs along the sand and check the wallet pickup. The encounter and payout can vary. See the ride and beach pickup in the source clip.

Check which routes repeat

After a junkyard run, check the operation marker again during free roam. If it displays Gang temporarily disbanded, leave and come back after the activity reopens. Compare the new safe and table contents before assuming the same payout. The opening cash-loop clip begins once Jason has a vehicle and can leave the safehouse. It does not establish an unlimited payout across builds.

For the metro route, the player clip shows a small in-game payout after the train departs. Check the wanted indicator in your own save, and use a job or a fence payout if the loop does not repeat. Early cash-loop recording · Metro recording.

Keep spending money separate

Before the bank unlock, aim down in Jason’s Key Lento bedroom to find the floor stash. Confirm the deposit or withdrawal prompt before you leave. Follow the bank and Family Savings options once BuckMe is available. When the chapter is about to change, see the guide to what stays accessible and make a separate manual save.
