GTA 6 Moses vehicle drop-offs

The white Crawler in Daily Driver is the first of seven Moses deliveries. Finish it in Chapter 2, then check Moses’s texts and the Robberies map filter for the six follow-up requests.

Unlock the requestsSeven vehiclesEmperor Sultan

Unlock the drop-offs

  1. Finish Moses in Chapter 2 to get the Slim Jim.
  2. Read Moses’s message and select Daily Driver as Lucia. Deliver the white Crawler to his Port Gellhorn garage. Afterward, check for the garage and follow-up texts.
  3. Use Map → Robberies → Moses Drop-Offs to see which request is active. Finish marked requests and check new messages if a later car has no marker yet.

Find the seven requested vehicles

  • ’82 Canis Crawler

    Trader’s Union on Gellhorn Avenue, Port Gellhorn. Start Daily Driver as Lucia. Approach the yard by the north side near the water tower and look for the white Crawler. Follow the Slim Jim prompt before taking it back to Moses.

  • Declasse Yosemite 1500

    Bastion Street off Gellhorn Avenue, southwest Port Gellhorn. Look along the driveways for the pickup on a tow truck. If the truck starts to move, follow the in-game target. Wait for it to stop before using the release interaction at the back.

  • BF Bandito

    Kelly County’s Buckskin Swamp trails. Watch the off-road group in the highlighted area. The Bandito is the small silver single-seater. Check the scan label before following it off the road.

  • Annis Hellion

    Orchard Avenue, southern Port Gellhorn. Check the driveway at the center of the search area. The house has a dog in the yard. The car is parked outside.

  • Karin Futo

    Postal warehouse, southern tip of Draper Island. Find the warehouse door on the north side. The Futo is in the garage at its west end. Use the button beside the shutter to make a clear exit before driving away.

  • Western Nightblade

    Final Chapter motorcycle clubhouse in Ambrosia. Head to the central garage building and look for the orange motorcycle on the ground floor. If the marker stays locked, finish Moses’s earlier requests and check his messages.

  • ’74 Declasse Vamos

    Shed in the marked property northeast of Grayson, off Rockledge Road. Search inside the main trailer for the road signs. The filmed combination is 2727. Enter it at the keypad on the small shed east of the house, then use the button beside the shutter to drive out.

Claim the Emperor Sultan

After seven credited deliveries, look for Collect Moses’s Gift and pick up the Emperor Sultan. The gift car is free. The recorded registration prompt charged GTA$1,600 to keep it. Check the garage entry after registering.

For ordinary sales, check Moses’s fence and your car’s tracker status. For Chapter 3’s second contact, see Randy’s Wish List.
