How to scan 200 vehicles in GTA 6

Scan any 200 of the game’s 277 base vehicles for 100%. Start with the impound lot, then use the class counters to plan where to look next.

Scan controlsEarly scan routeClass totals96 names to look up

Scan with WAiNK and check the result

  1. Follow Unwanted Attention in Chapter 1 until Cal introduces the WAiNK app.
  2. On foot, hold D-pad Up and point the phone at a vehicle. Hold R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to run the scan.
  3. Read the make and model on the scan card. Check Progress → Vehicles and the base-model class total. A custom or named version can appear separately in Standout, which has its own total.

Start at the Hamlet impound

During Unwanted Attention, pause at the first WAiNK prompt and scan some of the parked cars before following the Phoenix objective. The filmed run shows an Albany ’85 Emperor, Declasse ’78 Tulip M-100, Dinka ’85 Blista, Bravado ’95 Bison 900 and Imponte ’77 Phoenix in the lot. Watch the class counter as each new capture registers.

After finding Brian’s garage off Palmetto, look down the alley for the Karin ’84 Rebel. That scan gives you a pickup-class example. R3 on the captured PS5 layout opens the second page of the card.

Branch out by class. Search different parking areas for cars, docks for watercraft and airfields for aircraft. In Southwind, gameplay captures place a Mammoth Dodo and Buckingham Maverick by the same waterfront lot. Later, Moses’s drop-offs and Randy’s Wish List take you to different models. Check your game counter after scans made during a task.
