How to repair Jason’s house in GTA 6

You can make four optional repairs at Jason’s Key Lento house in Chapter 1. If the railing prompt is missing, go downstairs to the workout area and approach from below.

RailingFront stepsLeaky roofBedroom walls

Fix the railing from the workout area

  1. Go out the back door and down from the porch toward the weights. Find the broken railing beside the silver ladder on the lower side.
  2. Hold Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox to start. Press R2 or RT when prompted to fasten the slats and finish.

Fix the front steps

Walk around to the front entrance and look beside the steps for the stacked boards. Interact with the lumber to start the repair and use the prompts until the game confirms it.

Patch the leaky roof

Climb the yellow ladder near the front door. On the roof, walk to the blue tarp and interact with the work area.

Repair and paint the bedroom walls

Go inside to the bedroom. Use the drywall supplies, then follow the prompts around the paint materials to finish the walls.

Can you fix the house after picking up Lucia?

Yes. You can still finish the repairs after Lucia comes home. Treat them as optional relationship tasks. If the railing prompt fails to appear, check that you are by the weights below the porch and not on the upper deck next to the back door.
