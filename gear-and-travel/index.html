Recover your gear and get around in GTA 6

If a purchased weapon disappears from your loadout, check your personal vehicle’s trunk and the Ammu-Nation locker before buying it again.

Holster or recover a weapon

On the recorded Default PS5 layout, a tap of L1 holsters and a hold opens the loadout. You can also open the wheel and select Unarmed. Use your own On Foot chart if you switched schemes, and check the separate Holster in Vehicle setting if the issue only happens in a car.

Open the trunk of your personal vehicle with Triangle/Y. Open the loadout when prompted, select the weapon slot, and cycle through stored gear with left or right on the D-pad. If it is absent there, use a weapons locker at Ammu-Nation and check your owned weapons. If it is listed at the locker but still fails to appear in the trunk, use the locker to retrieve it and record the vehicle and weapon if the issue repeats.

Find masks and the Cut-Off Tool

Some missions supply a mask. For an equipable mask in free roam, check the Items tab and the clothing options at an Ammu-Nation that stocks masks. The equipment guide places the Cut-Off Tool unlock in Chapter 3 during Meet the Heders. Check Progress → Equipment and the container’s prompt before looking for it in stores.

Take a taxi, rideshare or bus

  • Early taxi. After Gone Fishing, open the phone and select Contacts → All Contacts → Paradise Taxi. Wait somewhere a car can reach. Set your map destination, enter as a passenger, and use the driver interaction to ride or pay the extra skip fee shown.
  • RydeMe. Once it is available, request the ride from the phone app. If no driver is available, call Paradise Taxi.
  • Bus. The documented system uses stops and a schedule. Check the stop for the next service, wait there, and use the boarding prompt when it arrives. If there’s no available service or prompt, use a passenger taxi as a fallback. A repeatable paid taxi-driver activity has yet to be confirmed by the checked launch guides.

Other early exploration questions

For an early aircraft, use the Southwind route. If a basketball is missing, check where that hoop’s ball usually sits and look in Items if you already picked it up. Open the basketball controls and location guide. For a highlighted generator, follow the current activity prompt. Check your garage’s customization options before spending money on Jason’s truck. A full revert to an earlier look remains unconfirmed.
