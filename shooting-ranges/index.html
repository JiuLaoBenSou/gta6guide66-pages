GTA 6 shooting range gold medals

Earn 18 gold medals at Griffin and 24 at Rodgers Ranch. All 42 count toward both The Shootist and Story Mode 100%.

Find the ranges and buy the waiver

  1. Go to Griffin Shooting Range by Balsero, northwest of central Vice City, or Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range northeast of Port Gellhorn.
  2. Buy the site’s $5 waiver from its clerk to reveal the course markers. At the start table, check the level, the weapon class and the entry cost. A recorded menu shows a $10 entry fee per attempt, with an extra rental if needed.
  3. Pick up any rented weapon before crossing the start. Finish the available difficulty to open the next one. Check the medals in Progress → Activities → Shooting Ranges.

Griffin Shooting Range · 18 golds

Griffin’s six courses are timed. Bullseyes subtract time, but keep moving if a perfect shot would slow the run down. Lucia is useful on stages with lots of running between targets.

  • 3-Point Range
    Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
    Clear each rack before moving on. Pick up the pistol, shotgun and rifle at the successive stations. An easy hit is worth taking when lining up the center would cost more time.
  • Hook Range
    Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
    For the spinning group of five small targets, take the top one first and move around clockwise. Save Focus for the last quick targets if you are losing time there.
  • Loop Range
    Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
    Keep moving from station to station. Save Focus for the small red targets that rotate out of line.
  • Maze Range
    Intermediate, Advanced, Master
    Follow the marked shooting stations around the long course and check the target counter before leaving each one.
  • Compound Range
    Intermediate, Advanced, Master
    Keep an eye on the minimap as you move indoors. Pink marks the next weapon. Red helps you find targets around corners.
  • Villa Range
    Intermediate, Advanced, Master
    Take a full loop through the houses. On later difficulties, make a point of entering the small buildings along the sides so nearby targets appear.

Rodgers Ranch · 24 golds

Each of the six courses has Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Master levels. Market is timed. On the scored courses, keep the hit streak alive to build the multiplier.

  • Market Range
    Pistol on all four levels
    This one is timed. Clear the fruit-stand cutouts promptly. Take the center hit when the target is already lined up.
  • Zombie Range
    SMG · Shotgun · Assault rifle · Shotgun
    Use short bursts into the cutouts with the SMG to build the streak without firing through a target that has already dropped. Try head hits with the shotgun.
  • Hunting Range
    Pistol · Shotgun · Tactical rifle · Pistol
    For Master, take quick head hits on small targets. On the bear cutouts, land several body hits before the final weak-point shot to build the multiplier.
  • Saloon Range
    Revolver · Tactical rifle · Revolver · Tactical rifle
    Build your combo on the early targets with body hits before the final hit. Prioritize a quick hit on targets that vanish early.
  • Cops & Robbers Range
    Sniper rifle on all four levels
    Use the gaps between waves to reload. Skip the police cutouts on rounds that mix them into the targets.
  • Alien Range
    Pistol/revolver on Beginner, Intermediate and Master · SMG/assault rifle on Advanced
    Build the score with a few hits to each cutout before the knockdown. Check the board for the weapon class before paying.

What the medals unlock

At 25 golds you unlock the No Reload cheat. At 42 you also earn The Shootist. The recorded final gold shows a gun wrap notification and an increase in max Focus.
