About GTA 6 Guides

GTA 6 Guides is an independent fan site run by Foster. Its guides are free to read so players can look up answers while playing Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. The site is unaffiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

We use Google AdSense to help support the cost of running the site.

How the guides were prepared

AI assisted with collecting, comparing and drafting from launch sources. Each guide links to the source coverage. Information may contain errors, omissions or version differences. The site has yet to reproduce these steps on a console.

These guides contain mission names and objectives. They avoid story-ending details.

Use Contact to report incorrect instructions, broken links, or source credits that need updating.
