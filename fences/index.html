All seven GTA 6 fences

Use your in-game map to check which fence is open, what they buy and their current rates. Yasmani is a high-rate option for Marked Bills and jewelry, while Tyrone pays well for narcotics.

Sell a bag of stolen goods

  1. Keep the bag with you or put it in the trunk. Check the bag’s contents before choosing a buyer. Take cash from Bank Jobs or Payday Loans marked Marked Bills to a fence for processing.
  2. On the pause map, highlight a green contact marker and read their rates by item type. Go to the drop-off point and use the prompt with the bag.
  3. Check the payment channel shown by the game. Early Chapter 1 cash deliveries can arrive in the mailbox, while later payments can go to BuckMe.

All fence locations and unlocks

Chapter 1 · Leonida Keys

  • Brian’s Lockup

    South Key Lento. Complete Lab Fees, then use this drop-off during Chapter 1. If the game tells you to wait for a cash delivery, check the mailbox at Jason’s house.

  • Donnie’s Trailer

    South Key Lento. Donnie is an early option for narcotics. The original drop-off disappears after Swamped.

Chapter 2 · Port Gellhorn

  • Moses

    After Take a Vehicle to Moses, scan the car’s tracker status with WAiNK. Moses handles untracked vehicles at his garage.

  • Dunk’s Trailer

    Unlocks through Slam Dunk (Find Painkillers). A general drop-off for narcotics, jewelry and Marked Bills. The story can temporarily remove the marker.

Chapter 3 · Vice City

  • Tyrone

    La Perle. Compare his map rate with Yasmani’s when you have a bag of narcotics. Tyrone offers a high rate for this category.

  • Yasmani

    Tropik Haze in Little Cuba. A high-rate option for jewelry and Marked Bills. Unlock his store through the Chapter 3 story, then use the drop-off point.

  • Randy

    Balsero. Finish Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS (Steal the Zorrusso for Randy). Check which high-end vehicles with Advanced Trackers Randy requests before bringing one.

Use a garage or send your partner

After unlocking Yasmani in Chapter 3, check your garage for the option to drop off the bag and select a buyer. If Jason and Lucia are together, put the bag in the personal car’s trunk and look for Fence Goods in the partner menu.

For a vehicle sale, check the tracker guide. For a specific target, follow Moses’s drop-offs or Randy’s Wish List. For ordinary spending money and ATMs, see cash, BuckMe and banking.
