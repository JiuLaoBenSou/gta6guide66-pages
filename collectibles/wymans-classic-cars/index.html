Wyman’s classic car collection

Claim the Chapter 2 Dominator Buggy to start Wyman’s Ultimate Edition quest. Four restorations take 15 scans across Leonida. The ’72 Deviant comes from Wyman’s final call.

UnlockTransgressorRiataMamba GTReynolds Kit CarDeviant

Unlock Find Cars for Wyman

  1. After Thrillbillies, look for the purple Dominator Buggy objective. Follow it to the garage at Paradise RV Park in the Watson Bay area and collect the buggy.
  2. After Wyman texts, open Map → Exploration → Find Cars for Wyman. Track the model he currently wants. On foot, hold D-pad Up to open WAiNK. Aim at the wreck and scan with R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox.
  3. After the last scan for a model, check BuckMe for Wyman’s GTA$1,500 rebuild request. Pay it, then use the W marker to collect the finished car. Store it in a garage or register it at a Pay ’n’ Spray.

’67 Transgressor · Port Gellhorn

  1. Tanglewood Lane. Start at Sarena’s Bargain Store and head north into the trailer park. Another approach is the circular drive at McCarthy’s Fresh Fruit & Veg off US-9. Go around the right of McCarthy’s, then look beyond the trailer and the pig for the wreck on a shaded concrete slab.
  2. Gellhorn Avenue. Northwest of Wasteland Off-Road, look for the blue house with sunflowers in its yard. If starting from Brooks Boatworks, follow the avenue northeast past the tennis courts. Walk down the right side of the house and look in the open shed beyond the pickup.
  3. South of Dirt Merchants. Take the dirt road beside the shop and stay on it to the small trailer park. Find the wooden building with an RV parked out front. The wreck is behind the building by the broken back fence. An active Dunk robbery tip may offer another landmark nearby.

The next three routes use published community locations. Confirm each wreck against the active Wyman objective before leaving.

’70 Riata · Three stops in the south

  1. Key Lento. Start at the police impound by Sunbelt Highway. Cross to the raised house on the water side and look beside the jet-ski trailer under the house.
  2. Southwind. From the Xero station, take the side road to the water. Check the open carport at the end of the road.
  3. Shoeless Keys. From Hamlet, head south to Old Fort Road. Check the shelter behind the disused filling station.

’64 Mamba GT · Two garages and two water stops

  1. North of Leonida State Prison. Use the Gruppe Sechs facility as the junction landmark. Go uphill on the road across from it and check the house’s detached garage.
  2. Ambrosia. Find the Xero station under the rainbow Tiny Frank billboard. Go past the tractor yard to the lane behind the shops and look for a small garage next to a green trailer.
  3. Thrillbilly Mud Club. Enter under the raceway sign and take the left branch onto the dirt loop. Look in the pond on the left from the track.
  4. North Moreau. Find the broken bridge near the east side of Bonanza. Check the water below the bridge debris.

Reynolds Kit Car · Five wrecks across Leonida

  1. Salton. Look west of the green parking structure by 7th Avenue Garage for the orange-and-blue Willie’s Service Center. If the bay is locked, use the side entrance interaction.
  2. Casanova Island. Follow the northern road to the vacant lot. Check the gated driveway of the property immediately to its left.
  3. Wild Hog Point. From the end of the airstrip west of Port Gellhorn, follow the sandy road to the beach containers. Look for the single orange container and bring the in-game angle grinder to open it.
  4. Watson Bay. Follow Seabell Avenue to Donnie’s Auto Repair, across from the motel by the water. Go behind the workshop.
  5. Capri. On the road toward the northwest point, look across from the construction site. Find the villa with fallen cones and bins out front, then follow its drive to the wood-trimmed garage.

’72 Deviant · Wait for Wyman’s final call

Collect all four restored cars and let in-game time pass. When Wyman calls, check BuckMe and pay the final request if it appears. Go to the W marker and interact with the barn door to start the handoff. Check Progress → Collections → Exploration → Wyman’s Cars for the five-car total. The optional tasks can count toward Story Mode 100%.

Wyman is still working on a car at Starlet Motel

If you see Car Trouble? while playing as Lucia, that interaction is a separate motel tune-up. On PS5, hold L2 on Wyman and choose it with Cross. In the driver’s seat, use R2 to start the engine, then aim for the pink part of the gauge during each round. Follow his cue to hold the throttle in the final round.
