Find Thelma, Marquise Bernard and Perla

Start in Key Lento for Thelma at the Badger tower and Marquise Bernard at Kenton’s shop. A nearby rooftop box gave Perla in one recording. For the standard card, also check the reported fixed location at the Vice City rail yard.

Thelma · Badger R&D Tower

Go to Sunset Drive in Key Lento and find the Badger R&D Tower near the Nifty Threads Islandwear marker, between the highway and the water. Cross the chain-link gate, then enter through the gray door at the tower base. Take the ladder up to the control room and look for the Thelma — Standard card on the desk between the green control panels and the small TV.

The balcony next to the control room also has a free SM-69 Sniper propped against the railing.

Marquise Bernard · Island Dreams

On the east waterfront of Key Lento, find the Island Dreams sign at Kenton’s shop. Follow the wooden walkway past the front entrance to the deck along the yellow building. Look for a blue bench and an air compressor. Use the Collectible Card prompt at the barrel beside them. The pickup screen identifies Marquise Bernard — Standard from Los Santos Panic.

Perla · rail yard and Key Lento rooftop box

For Perla — Standard, find the elevated control hut over the tracks at the north end of the Balsero rail yard. On the yellow-railed deck, check the gray electrical cabinet against the hut’s outer wall. Some community maps file this stop under Belville, so match the tracks and hut in the in-game captures.

While you are at the Badger R&D Tower in Key Lento, you can also check the yellow box on the roof of the low Badger building in the same fenced lot. Go around to the exterior steps and climb the wall ladder, then check beside the raised rooftop structure. The filmed run found Perla here. Yellow containers can give different cards, so a different pull is normal.

Check Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards to confirm the card and its grade.

Three more early cards

  • Danny McLean. From Thelma’s control room, go onto the Badger tower balcony. Check the yellow phone box next to the rifle.
  • Santiago Lopez. Look in the mobile homes behind Jason’s house in Key Lento. The photographed pickup is in a bedroom beside a box of ammo.
  • Leroy Rook (Silver). Check the safe in the back room of the Hamlet warehouse. GTA6Heists recorded the pickup and a GTA$6,000 sale at Hamlet Pawn. Check your Collections entry before selling.

How many card sets do you need?

The game has 18 sets with 480 unique cards. Complete three sets for Don’t Touch My Deck or 15 sets for Story Mode 100%. Open Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards to see what each set still needs. Compare your closest sets and look for those cards first.

A duplicate of a card you already registered adds no set progress. Where a card has color variants, each distinct variant has its own entry. Once a pickup has registered, you can sell the physical card without losing its collection entry.
