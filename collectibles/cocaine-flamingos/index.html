Cocaine Flamingos

Start on southeast Belpoint for six ornaments close together. This guide covers 23 reported stops, including the Goose Key shoreline. Story Mode 100% takes any 100 of the 125.

How to tell which flamingos count

Look for the pink lawn ornament with a black-and-white bill. Shoot the pink body and check that a package drops. Leave the black-and-yellow-billed decorations alone. The counter advances when the ornament breaks, so you can move on without picking up the package.

Belpoint route · six targets

  1. Start at the southeast docks. Walk out onto the wooden pier.
  2. Walk up to the larger white house. Check the open ground in front of the building.
  3. Continue to the smaller white house. Walk around its corner.
  4. Head toward the beach. Look along the floor inside the shipping container.
  5. Step out onto the beach. Check the sand by the fire and the overturned chair.
  6. Look out over the shallows for the yellow container. Check the top.

Roadside stops around the Keys and Vice City · eight targets

  • East Watson Bay motel. Follow the road west from Hamlet to the loop just inside Watson Bay. Check behind the building where the corrugated extension meets the brick wall. The ornament is among the boxes and rubbish under a leaning sign.
  • North Watson Bay. On the west side of town, use the house with the Delights Dancers Chip card as your landmark. Check behind the house next door.
  • Central Key Lento. Check the unusual house’s front garden from the road.
  • Central Capri. Go to the street grid south of Tortoise Lake and check the Halloween House garden.
  • West of Volcano Coffee Co. Still in Capri, find the nearby house west of the cafe and look in its front garden.
  • La Costa, southwest Capri. Find Hair By Clair, go two blocks southwest and check outside La Costa.
  • Abismo Key. Follow the southeastern shoreline near the crossing toward Key Lento.
  • La Perle, NW 9th Avenue. Go northwest from Tyrone’s apartment. Enter the ground-level courtyard of the C-shaped building and look between the two doors beside the large wooden crate.

Take a boat to the outer islands · eight targets

  1. Riquezas Keys. Land on the large southwestern island and check inside the building on the island with the radome.
  2. Cottonmouth Lake. Go south of the lake to the island with the broadest sandbar. Sweep its western shore. The trailer holding the Laki Maivia card safe is another landmark on the same island.
  3. Northern Featherstone island. Make three landings or walk the coast. Check the northern tip first, then the southeast shore and the south shore for one ornament each.
  4. Wreck of the Meerwunder. Go to the north beach of the southeastern Featherstone island. Sweep along the sand by the wreck for three more.

Find the flamingos along the Goose Key shoreline

  1. Open the map at Mile Zero Air Station. Find the long bridge east of the curved road junction. Set a waypoint on the bend in the shoreline just south of the bridge’s western end.
  2. Follow the shore below the road. Look for a yellow excavator and a low stone wall above the rocks. Check the shallow water and the gaps beside the boulders for the pink ornaments. Break the black-and-white-billed target and check your collection counter.

The footage shows two package pickups at this spot. The guide counts the shoreline as one search stop.

Four more stops in Key Lento and Port Gellhorn

  1. Key Lento roadside. Walk onto the grass to the left of the hydrant and check between the two palm trunks. Open map pin
  2. Key Lento beach. Follow the sand between the mangroves and the water. Use Focus beside the seaweed to distinguish the target from the rest of the flock. Open map pin
  3. Key Lento painted porch. Find the multicolored railing and the large pink inflatable. Check the ground at the base of the railing. Open map pin
  4. Port Gellhorn. Find the small concrete building with a barred door. Check under the window immediately to the right of that door. Open map pin

Six more flamingo stops across Leonida

  1. Belpoint: climb the lighthouse ladder and check the upper platform beside the light. Map pin
  2. Capri: look in the garden by the entrance to the hotel in the southwest of town. Map pin
  3. Rockridge: enter the shop block at the marker and search the stockroom. Map pin
  4. West of Port Gellhorn: on the rubbish island, check the top of the mound just below the old refrigerator. Map pin
  5. Buckskin Swamp: check the water in the marked channel for a floating ornament. Map pin
  6. South of Waning Sands: open the coastal property’s garage and look behind the parked car. Map pin

Check the count after each cluster

Open Progress → Collections → Exploration → Cocaine Flamingos after breaking your first one. A full Belpoint sweep adds six if you have never broken those targets. If the count stops short, check the bill color and revisit the last cluster.

Destroying 50 unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo cheat. Story Mode 100% still requires 100. The collection menu shows all 125 possible targets.
