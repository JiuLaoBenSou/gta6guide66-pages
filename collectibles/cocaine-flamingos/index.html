Cocaine Flamingos

Start on the southeast side of Belpoint for six close-together targets. For Story Mode 100%, clear any 100 of the 125 flamingos.

How to tell which flamingos count

Look for the pink lawn ornament with a black-and-white bill. Shoot the pink body and check that a package drops. Leave the black-and-yellow-billed decorations alone. The counter advances when the ornament breaks, so you can move on without picking up the package.

Belpoint route · six targets

  1. Start at the southeast docks and check the dock area for the first ornament.
  2. Walk toward the white houses. Look in front of the larger house, then sweep around the corner of the smaller house. There is a target at each building.
  3. Head for the burning container on the beach. Check inside it, beside the overturned camping chair near the fire, and on top of the yellow container sitting in the water.

Two more in Watson Bay and La Perle

  • Watson Bay. Go to the motel at the eastern edge of the area. Look near the trash at the southwest corner of the building.
  • La Perle. Use Tyrone’s apartment as a landmark. Check the ground-level courtyard of the C-shaped building to its northwest.

Check the count

Open Progress → Collections → Exploration → Cocaine Flamingos after breaking your first one. Compare the count before and after a stop. Belpoint should add six if you are starting there for the first time.
