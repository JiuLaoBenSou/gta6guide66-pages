Underwater discoveries

Start at Island Dreams in Key Lento. The route covers the story wreck and all seven treasure-set areas. The 100% checklist asks for five completed treasure sets and 33 Underwater Discoveries, tracked separately.

Find the first Gold Doubloons caches

  1. Start Island Dreams and take the boat to the objective. Put on the dive gear before following the group down through the coral trench. On PS5, press R1 to dive or Cross to surface. Swim close to the dark hull until the Underwater Discoveries notification appears.
  2. Bring up the metal detector from the weapon wheel. At the wreck, aim it down and sweep across the sand next to the debris. Follow the signal until the game offers the search prompt.
  3. Find the first gold marker by the broken pots. Use Search and finish the on-screen input.
  4. Swim north along the edge of the coral ridge to find the second gold marker. Search it the same way.
  5. For the third cache, aim the detector at the sand beside the large rounded rock. Dive until the gold marker is in front of you, then search it. The objective should switch to checking on Dobbs.
  6. Find Dobbs at the metal grate on top of the wreck. Hold both triggers and tap Cross / A when prompted. The grate opens the way into the hull for a fourth, optional cache. You can return to Del Ocio for it later.

Clear Kenton’s first three wrecks

If you just finished Island Dreams and see no purple markers, play through Feng Shui as Jason. Answer Kenton’s follow-up and check Map → Exploration → Search the Dive Sites. The first message names Del Ocio, La Noche and Ética Laxa.

Take the Huntress from the Island Dreams dock or use Jason’s boat once Kenton says he has left scuba gear on it. Use the Scuba Gear prompt at the stern to equip the metal detector. On PS5, hold D-pad Left and use the anchor option under Vehicle Properties before diving.

Read each purple marker’s ship name and item count before setting the waypoint. Underwater, face the strongest detector signal and follow the gold marker when it appears. At a stuck chest, hold both triggers and repeatedly tap Cross / A when prompted. Open the chest after pulling it free.

Nuestra Señora del Ocio · 10 Gold Doubloons

From Kenton’s dock, head northeast past the small island. Pick the same wreck as the story mission. For coins you missed, sweep inside the hull opening, around the pottery north of the bow, and along the coral on the north and northeast side. If the count is still short, check the sand east of the hull by the low rocks. Stop once the map shows 10/10.

For the cache inside the hull, zoom in on the pause map. Use the rounded bow to orient yourself and sweep just behind it.

Nuestra Señora de la Noche · 7 treasures

Take the circle close to Key Lento’s south coast. Start at the bow and work around the hull, then widen the loop to cover the loose debris and the chest:

  • Bow and north side. Sweep the rubble at the bow for the Serpentine Statue. Check the exposed boards near the north bow for the Pewter Gauntlet, then look tight against the north hull for the Ivory Saint.
  • Stern and west side. Look by the barrel at the stern for the Purple Gemstone. From there, search to the west for the chest holding the Stained Doubloon.
  • South side. Look among the netting and splintered timbers near the mast for the Dragon Pipe. Follow the detached debris out to the anchor and barrels for the Antique Candlestick.

Nuestra Señora de la Ética Laxa · 5 treasures

Use the farther south circle off Abismo Key. Search the opening through the middle of the ship for the Golden Pendant among the timbers and the Golden Chalice beside the crate. Work along the broken hull for the Ceremonial Spoon and look under the purple sea fan for the Ancient Snuff Box. Swim through the middle opening toward the reef for the Battered Doubloon chest.

Unlock the next three offshore wrecks

Finish the wrecks in your current batch and return the finds to Kenton. If the next markers are missing, let in-game time pass and check Jason’s messages again. Kamigame’s run got the next batch after about three in-game days. Sleeping can advance the clock. Check the map before leaving, since Cinco Torres is already marked earlier in some runs.

Cinco Torres and Lujuriosa are around the same island chain. Pair them on one trip when both are available.

Nuestra Señora Lujuriosa · 6 treasures

Start southwest of the hooked island, between the close-set islands off southeast Key Lento. Dive toward the ribs on the west side of the search zone.

  • North loop. Check the front mast for the Golden Statuette, the nearby bow rocks for the Ceremonial Knife, and the stones to the northeast for the Golden Llama.
  • South loop. Follow the forward hull south to the rocks for the Jade-Lobe Tankard. Check the southeast stern for the Ceremonial Bowl.
  • Outer sweep. Follow the detector out from the hull to coral for the Shiny Doubloon. The route reports differ on the last approach, so verify the signal outside the visible wreckage.

Urca de Cinco Torres · 6 treasures

Dive east of the hooked island. The upright ribs in shallow water make a good landmark for each pass:

  • South. Sweep the seagrass for the Ancient Ceremonial Mask and check the sand halfway down the wreck for the Rusty Doubloon. Continue southeast to the pottery for the Sacred Necklace.
  • Both ends and the reef. Check the west bow for the Jade-Eye Cup, the coral at the east end for the Golden Glove, and the coral-covered rock on the north side for the Ceremonial Plate.

Urca de Santa Sierra · 6 treasures

Take the outer circle east of Key Lento and identify the red sail lying along the hull. Search the wreck first, then the coral and wreck fragments:

  • West side. Check the planks beside the sail for the Dividing Compass. Find the Santa Sierra Bell in the sand northwest of the sails and the Brass Spyglass beside the southwest bow.
  • North end. Look under what remains of the stern for the Ornate Pocket Watch. Continue north to the sand beside the coral-covered rock for the Worn Doubloon chest. Swim around the rock to reach the prompt.
  • East side. Sweep the sand around the detached wreck fragment and nearby rocks for the Silver Hip Flask.

Find the Belpoint and Bobcat treasure sets

Belpoint Strait · five treasures

Set a waypoint at the marked spot southeast of Belpoint’s tip, just west of Bobcat Bight. Anchor above the marker. Put on the boat’s scuba gear with Triangle / Y, then hold R1 / RB to reach the seabed before sweeping.

Set contents. Ancient Bracelet, Ancient Ingot, Avian Plate, Golden Fish and Lover’s Grail.

Bobcat Dive Site · six treasures

Head west off Bobcat Key’s southern bend, northwest of Watson Bay. Set the waypoint at the offshore cursor shown below. Work along the reef edge first, then turn across the open sand and check any faster beeps. When the detector goes quiet, stop swimming and pan until you pick the signal up again.

Set contents. Astrolabe, Captain’s Whistle, Ceramic Pirate, Ivory Sundial, Naval Hourglass and Pirate’s Buckle.

Check the site card and Progress → Collections → Kenton’s Treasures after each dive. The individual pickup positions within these two search areas still need confirmation.

Check both counters and claim your rewards

  1. Treasure sets. Take finds back to Kenton’s counter at Island Dreams. Aim at him to see the Treasures sale prompt. He can send a larger BuckMe payment after you sell him the last item in a set. Check Progress → Collections → Kenton’s Treasures for the seven sets and the five-set 100% target.
  2. Underwater Discoveries. Check Progress → Collections → Exploration → Underwater Discoveries for the separate 33-discovery target. The six documented wrecks are Del Ocio, La Noche, Ética Laxa, Lujuriosa, Cinco Torres and Santa Sierra. Belpoint Strait and Bobcat count toward the treasure sets. Their open-water dives do not register as wreck discoveries. Locations for the other 27 discoveries remain unverified.
  3. Exploration challenges. Under Progress → Challenges → Exploration, Underwater Discoveries asks for 25 sites, Underwater Treasures asks for 85 finds, and Black Hole Wrecks asks for all 11 wrecks. Use the Collections counter for the 33 discoveries and the separate Kenton set count. The 11 Black Hole Wreck locations and their overlap with the 33 discoveries are still unverified.

Get Kenton’s finale to appear

  1. Finish the purple sites Kenton has already sent and sell the finds to him at Island Dreams. The trophy run saw the Todd scene at the dock after Ética Laxa, La Noche and Cinco Torres were turned in. If your initial group differs, clear the sites showing in your current save first.
  2. After the scene, check Jason’s messages for Lujuriosa and Santa Sierra. Finish both and return their treasures. If the Kenton marker has vanished between chapters, continue the story and check again as Jason.
  3. Complete Maritime Disputes to unlock This Was No Boat Accident. You can use the Rugger Fortune for later dives. Recheck the 100% page for your treasure-set and discovery totals.
