GTA 6 AHOL street art locations

There are 112 painted objects around Vice City. Match the missing number in Geographies of Trash, then use the street and landmark to find the object.

How to scan AHOL art

  1. Approach the painted object on foot and wait for its scan prompt. Look around the edges of the building, especially near stairs, alleys and bins.
  2. Hold D-pad Up to open WAiNK. Center the painted object and follow the scan prompt (R1 on PS5, RB on Xbox).
  3. Open Progress → Collections → AHOL Street Art and check the item number. These scans count toward Geographies of Trash. For the 100 required location scans, use the WAiNK locations guide.
