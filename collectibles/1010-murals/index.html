GTA 6 1010 mural locations

Stand at the five 1010 marks and photograph what each mark points toward. Two shots are taken from the sidewalk and three are taken from higher vantage points.

Start the 1010 photo challenge

  1. Look for the artist 1010 in Snapmatic on the in-game phone. At each location, move onto the viewpoint mark.
  2. Open the phone camera and aim at the subject indicated by the mark. Allow the painted train to enter the frame at the Crosstown stop.
  3. Take the picture and check Progress → Collections → 1010 Murals. If your counter did not move, check your position and whether the required subject was in frame.

Five places to stand and what to photograph

  1. Loophole Architecture · Rockridge
    At Northbrook Drive and Santiago Avenue, get to the Parlor Apartments roof. Stand on its mark. Aim down across the street into the rainbow-colored building.
  2. You Signed the Terms & Conditions · Crosstown
    Use the sidewalk mark along NE 2nd Avenue. Frame the station mural and wait to take the shot until the painted train pulls in.
  3. Extraction Service · Downtown
    Find New Quay Plaza on SE 1st Avenue. Stand at the marked corner of its roof and frame the bright rooftop below.
  4. Compliance Theater · Downtown
    Go to Pike Financial Center on SE 2nd Avenue. Find the sign at the top of the rooftop antenna and aim down at the painted roof from beside it.
  5. Privatized Pains Socialized Gains · Ocean Beach
    Find the Barlow Street sidewalk mark by the public parking garage. Fit the painting that runs up the garage wall into the frame.

The 1010 counter is separate from AHOL street art and the 100 WAiNK locations. IGN’s coverage reports a custom-painted ’95 Grotti Cheetah after all five photos.
