Key Lento collectibles route

Grab the Chapter 1 reads at Jason’s house first, then sweep the northern stops and the east waterfront. Cross to Sunset Drive and finish on Surfside Drive.

Bring up the map for each named stop as you go. The house and Cal’s errand have Chapter 1 timing. Skip those steps if your save has moved on.

Jason’s house and Peach Palm Drive

Make this your first stop while Chapter 1 is active. From the waterfront driveway, check the basketball area below the stairs, then go upstairs for the house reads.

  • Read five titles at home. Open Ashes of Glory beside the bed. The living area has Lethal By Design and Orca A Whole Different Animal. Check by the weights outside for Body Copy and the shed below for Off the Hook. House reading locations
  • Make a basket by Jason’s parking space. On the way out, check the detached garage at the green corner house farther along Peach Palm Drive for another hoop. Mariana County hoop landmarks
  • Take the terrace meditation if Lucia invites Jason while both are home. Keep a separate Chapter 1 save if you want to return to the scene. First meditation trigger and controls
  • If you have left Chapter 1, check alternate reading locations for any titles you missed and start the road sweep at the Rusty Anchor. Alternate reading locations

The Rusty Anchor

Set the next waypoint at the Rusty Anchor in northwest Key Lento. The card, the pool table and Kirk’s notice are in the same building.

  • Check the toilet in the Captain’s Log Room for Aimie (Pink). Key Lento card pickups
  • Enter the main bar for the pool opponent. Across from the bar, look for the arcade cabinet and confirm Bang For Your Buck on its prompt. Pool controls and opponent locations
  • To start Kirk’s quest, turn right just inside the front entrance and inspect the flyer behind the door. Call Kirk from Contacts for the next clue. Kirk’s flyer and first meeting
  • If Cal texts while you are on the way to meet Brian during Swamped, finish Ring the Bell! before continuing to the marina. Giving Cal a ride home unlocks the Rusty Anchor T-Shirt and Cap for purchase later. Cal’s timed encounter

Dolphin Drive by the Corsair Key crossing

Find the party house near the bridge to Corsair Key. Take the outside stairs to the upper entrance.

  • Check the first bedroom on the left for the Pêche magazine and the Novak Laurent Alcazar watch. In the upstairs bathroom, check the toilet for Lionel Taber. Lionel Taber’s upstairs pickup
  • Match the watch table at the bedroom window before leaving the house. Novak Laurent Alcazar pickup
  • On Dolphin Drive, look by the driveway of the house with a fish decoration for the hoop. Check the garage for the ball. Fish-house hoop and ball positions
  • Look near the blue house for the turtle-shaped Book Swap cabinet. Open Killer Prom and confirm that it registered. Killer Prom Book Swap

Island Dreams on the east waterfront

Before crossing the island toward Sunset Drive, set a waypoint at Kenton’s yellow Island Dreams building on the east waterfront.

  • Go past the front door along the deck. Check the barrel beside the blue bench and air compressor for Marquise Bernard (Standard). Island Dreams map and barrel screenshot
  • If Kenton’s Island Dreams objective is active, you can start the equipment and first dive steps from this stop. Kenton’s first dive objectives

Field references. Nerd’s Chalk · the Island Dreams barrel and bench · IGN · Island Dreams and Kenton’s first dive

Badger R&D Tower and the construction lot

Head for the tower on Sunset Drive near Nifty Threads Islandwear. Do the scan before climbing, then cross Sunbelt Highway for the construction lot.

  • Use WAiNK to scan Badger R&D Tower from outside. Badger Tower scan
  • Go through the chain-link gate and enter the gray door at the base. Climb to the control room for Thelma (Standard) on the desk beside the small TV. Tower entry and card screenshot
  • Across Sunbelt Highway, use the rear entrance to Steven Strachan Construction. Check the storage boxes by the green container for Georgia (Purple). Georgia in the construction lot

Field references. Push Square · Key Lento roads, pickup rooms and scan targets · GTA BOOM · tower map and gated entrance

Christmas House and the Pacific Drive waterfront

Continue southwest on Sunset Drive to Laguna Drive, then follow the waterfront. This stretch has two scan targets and a reading stop.

  • Match the decorated façade and the sleigh at Christmas House, then scan the building from the road. Christmas House map and scan target
  • At the waterfront beach house farther along Sunset Drive, take the stairs up. Trigger Happy is at the corner of the dining table. Trigger Happy and other open-world reading
  • On Pacific Drive, find the B.E.N.D.S. dive center. Go around to the shark mural and scan it for Plunge Masters Deep Sea Chalet. Dive-center map and shark mural

Field references. Push Square · Key Lento roads, pickup rooms and scan targets · IGN · WAiNK Curiosities maps and scans · Push Square · Reading-item pickups

Surfside Drive to the southwest tip

Use Mahi Street and Nautilus Lane to get from Sunset Drive to Surfside Drive. Group the resort and whale stops before finishing at the end of the road.

  • At Crastenburg Resort, look behind the resort near Nautilus Lane for the fenced half-court. Check near the arc for the ball. Crastenburg Resort hoop
  • At The Wonder Whale near Lemon Street and Sunbelt Highway, scan the whale sculpture outside. The Wonder Whale scan
  • At the same stop, go round to the northeast side of The Wonder Whale clothing shop and inspect the bench for Wokester (Blue). Wokester’s bench
  • On Surfside Drive, make a basket behind The Red Lotus. Check near the green container and forklift for the ball. The Red Lotus hoop
  • Follow Surfside Drive to the southwest tip. Scan the Fatal Incursion Plaque, the memorial beside the water. Fatal Incursion Plaque scan

Field references. Push Square · Key Lento roads, pickup rooms and scan targets · Push Square · Key Lento hoop locations · Push Square · whale and memorial scan targets · PowerPyx · Wokester in Key Lento

Continue by water

Make the Shoeless Keys run separately. Follow the six bottle stops from the causeway. For dives from Kenton’s dock, use the Island Dreams dive route.
