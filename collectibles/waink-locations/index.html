WAiNK landmark scans

Start with Vice City and the beach tags, then work through the regional routes. Check the WAiNK Locations counter after each sweep.

Get the scan to register

  1. Get out of your vehicle and walk through the area. Listen for the alert and look for the notification near the lower-right corner.
  2. Hold Up on the D-pad to open the scan view. Look around for the WAiNK starburst on the object or surface. With the marker in view, use R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox to scan it.
  3. When the alert goes off but the scan does not appear, step back to see the landmark and check both sides of the structure. For a No Flusher tag, look down at the pavement.

Plan a route to 100 scans

This guide lists 108 reported targets. Start with the city, the beaches and the pavement tags while you are on foot. Pick up the billboards on county drives, then use the Keys and backcountry groups to fill in the count. If you scan all 15 listed tags and four billboards, you need 81 of the 89 other targets. Leave whichever eight are less convenient for your route.

All 35 curiosities below have a close-up map and a field screenshot. Open Map and scan target at any curiosity to match the spot and see what to scan.

Check the total under Progress → Collections → WAiNK Locations after each group. Use the in-game notification and the relevant counter to confirm each scan.

  11. Fleeca Field. Approach the stadium entrance in west Rockridge and aim at its sign.

Fifteen No Flusher Tags in Vice Beach · 15

Cal’s text introduces the pavement markings. Start on foot along Shore Drive. Scan across from The Buckingham, then work toward The Banana Hole and around the nearby side streets.

The route lists 15 mapped markings. PowerPyx’s Progress Database reports 23 in the category. Keep track of the streets you visit because the menu gives a count without listing each tag.

  1. Maritime Court Luxury Apartments. Northwest Ocean Beach. Walk into the cul-de-sac off Harborside Avenue and scan the sidewalk.
  2. Portside Lane. West Ocean Beach. Look down where the lane bends around the blue-and-white apartments.
  3. Luxx Artemis Heights. Southwest Ocean Beach. Scan the pavement by Koko Boulevard at Admiral Causeway.
  4. Positano Towers. South Ocean Beach. Check Shore Drive along the front of the towers.
  5. Hathaway Apartments. Ocean Beach. At Seaview Boulevard and Shore Drive, look down on the pavement across from The Buckingham.
  6. The Banana Hole. East Ocean Beach. Check the Shore Drive and Huevo Street corner east of the bar.
  7. Bean Machine Coffee. Ocean Beach. Follow Huevo Street to the sidewalk outside the coffee shop.
  8. Jollies. Ocean Beach. Scan the pavement along Grape Street at the shop front.
  9. Tootsie Katz Shrimp House. North Ocean Beach. Check the Cabega Street sidewalk at the restaurant.
  10. Fuji Kitchen. North Ocean Beach. Check the pavement at D Street and Atlantic Avenue.
  11. High Tide Hotel. East Washington Beach. Check the hotel sidewalk on the side street off Shorebeach Road.
  12. Mariposa Avenue apartments. North Washington Beach. Look down by the tan-and-teal apartment building.
  13. Sea Scoop Cafe. Southeast Washington Beach. Look at the coastal end of Barlow Street near the cafe.
  14. Wash Haus. Washington Beach. Check the sidewalk out front near Northside Avenue.
  15. Hotel Sapphire. Oldby Island. Check the corner of Oleander Boulevard and SW 4th Street.

Which scans count toward 100%?

The target is 100 entries in Progress → Collections → WAiNK Locations. Landmarks, curiosities, sculptures, Pee-Wee billboards and No Flusher Tags can contribute. AHOL street art and the 1010 mural photos have separate counters.
