Study wildlife and track Legendary Hunter

Use binoculars to record wildlife and Focus to follow animal tracks. Jason can unlock Bret’s Target Decks in Chapter 2. The Legendary Hunter challenge has its own eight unique targets.

Study wildlifeSearch by habitatBret’s first three decksLegendary Hunter

Log a wild animal

  1. Select binoculars from the weapon wheel and center an animal in the view. Press D-pad Up to zoom if it is too far away.
  2. When Study appears, hold R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox until the record registers.
  3. Open Progress → Animals to check the entry. You need any 100 of 255 entries for the 100% checklist. Different breeds of cats and dogs count separately.

Where to look for more wildlife

If you keep finding animals you already studied, move to a different habitat. The wildlife video linked by 3DM gives these broad search areas:

  • Coast and shallow water. Try Key Lento’s beaches, the Keys’ reef margins, Watson Bay or Gambit Bay.
  • Fresh water and forest. Check the wooded paths and water edges around Kalaga Reservoir, Dawson Dam or Lake Leonida.
  • Developed areas. Try city parks and alleys as well as the area around the Leonida zoo for additional sightings.

The video describes wildlife habitats rather than pins for the eight unique animals. Use the live map marker when a hunting job gives you one.

Unlock Bret’s hunts and find the early targets

  1. After Coffee Break in Chapter 2, stay in free roam as Jason until Bret texts. Go to the Veteran Hunted marker alone in Lummox County.
  2. When Bret asks you to track the deer, click both sticks to activate Focus. Follow the white scent trail until it turns yellow. Study the deer through binoculars or a scope, then finish the hunt and deliver it to the processing area at Bret’s cabin.
  3. After each delivery, read Bret’s next text and use Map → Activities → Target Deck to find the purple markers. Check the name of the animal through the scope before shooting. Bret requests specific animals, including some variants.

Check Legendary Hunter separately

Open Progress → Challenges → Survival → Legendary Hunter. The challenge asks you to hunt eight unique animals. The 100% checklist counts studied animals and Bret’s 15 Target Decks separately. It accepts any 70 of the 80 challenges, so you can leave Legendary Hunter for later while working toward 100%.

The complete eight-animal roster and its spawn points remain unverified. Bret’s first three decks cover the targets in the table. Check the challenge entry after a unique hunt before counting it toward Legendary Hunter.
