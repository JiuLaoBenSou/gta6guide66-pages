Find all five Unique Playlist posters

Four scans unlock The Sniper of La Perle and one unlocks The Vault Radio. Hold D-pad Up to bring up WAiNK, frame the poster and confirm the scan.

The Sniper of La Perle

Clear the three La Perle shops together. If the Jack of Hearts upstairs studio is blocked, advance Chapter 3. The filmed route linked by 3DM uses the club access around Encrypted Hard Drive.

  1. Drip Drops. Face the clerk, then check the counter.
  2. TWA Ponya Tattoo. Stop at the table by the entrance.
  3. Aunt Zola’s Soul Food. Find the poster within sight of the register.
  4. Jack of Hearts. Go upstairs to the recording booth on the east side. Scan the poster resting on a crate.

Open the music menu from a vehicle or with headphones and check On Demand → The Sniper of La Perle. If it is missing, return to the posters. The Progress screen reports the playlist as a whole rather than showing which individual poster you missed.

The Vault Radio

Enter The Vault in Downtown Vice City. Scan the red poster by the entrance, then look under Radio → The Vault. To verify both unlocks, open Progress → Collections → Exploration → Unique Playlists.
