Magazines, books and comics

Read the safehouse copies before changing chapters. Start with six titles in Key Lento, then follow the later routes and visit all seven Book Swaps to work toward 30 unique reads.

Chapter 1Chapters 2–5Open-world readingMissed copiesThree extra titlesAll seven Book Swaps29 mapped copies

Six quick reads in Key Lento

  1. Bedroom. Read Ashes of Glory from the table by Jason’s bed.
  2. Living room. Check the table for Lethal By Design and the one by the east window for Orca A Whole Different Animal.
  3. Outside. Look for Body Copy by the weights on the back deck. Go below the house to the tool shed for Off the Hook.
  4. 24/7 store. Swing by 247 – Key Lento and look for RX Men in the magazine rack.

Open and read each item when you find it. The five copies at Jason’s house become inaccessible after Chapter 1. If you are already farther along, check the alternate locations.

Read these during Chapters 2–5

  • Chapter 2, Starlet Motel. Read Help Me, Help You, Help Me to the left of the reception door, by the computer. In the kitchen, look by the radio at the window for Children of Wizard Wang.
  • Chapter 3, Bayside apartment. At 201 Catalan Boulevard, check the table right of the hot tub for Passion B.C. and the table in front of the TV for Two Pricks in the Bush.
  • Chapter 4, Ajax Gym. Use the Trauma Floor access from Blood, Sweat and Gear. Look for Remote Strike on the pool table near the lockers.
  • Chapter 5, Pegassi Residences. In Tequesta Retreat, check the table on Penthouse Level 3 for Lights, Camera, Affirmative Action.

Sweep the open-world copies by area

  • Key Lento. Use the upstairs entrance of the Dolphin Drive party house. Turn left into the first bedroom and check the bed for Pêche Issue 69. At the Sunset Drive beach house, go upstairs and look on the corner of the dining table for Trigger Happy Issue 99.
  • Gellhorn and Port Gellhorn. Look at Alpha Gas northeast of Starlet Motel for The Johnsons Issue 1. Follow Dunk’s Lost Vista marker northwest of the station garage and check a trailer for Dicked Down Deep in Space.
  • Belville. Look on the bed in the garage west of Andy’s house for Foiled.
  • Moreau and Mount Kalaga. Go to the Grayson Motel on Rockledge Road, north of Taco Bomb. The Cowboy’s Prayer Is to Die Young is on the table just inside reception, on your right. For 11:59 Survivalist Culture, climb the ladder at the camouflaged lookout east of Kalaga Reservoir near Dawson Dam.
  • La Perle and Crosstown. The Xero Food Mart magazine rack has Ratface Issue 1 and Special Friends Force Issue 1. At Jack of Hearts, look upstairs in the hall west of the recording booths for High Rollerz Issue 37.
  • Oldby Island. Look on the rack in Ron Stop’n’Snack for ¡BOMBAZO! Issue 2342.

Find alternate copies of seven titles

If you missed a safehouse copy or are passing through another district, try these alternate locations. Read each title once and watch for the collection notification.

  • Off the Hook. On eastern Key Lento, find the cream house on the water along Surfside Drive and check its dining table. In northern Port Gellhorn, a second report puts a copy on the dresser in the living room of a blue-and-white mobile home off Washburn Avenue.
  • Body Copy. In southern Downtown, look on the padded block in the corner of the Zenith gym inside Moon tower on SW 2nd Street. On Oldby Island, another reported copy sits on the ledge in front of the Fuel Stop kiosk at Ajax Gym on Vineyard Drive.
  • Orca A Whole Different Animal. If Jason’s first house is locked, try the tiki bar in southern Southwind. Check the tables for the book.
  • Remote Strike. If you missed the pool table at Ajax Gym, look behind the closed bar on the east side of Southwind.
  • Trigger Happy. On the west side of Port Gellhorn, find the red-and-white mobile home near Gellhorn Avenue. Look on the floral couch in the living room.
  • Foiled, Issue 329. Take a boat along the Pelican River at the west edge of Gullet Marsh. Look around the dock and shed at the small fishing compound on stilts. If you already read the Belville copy, check the counter before counting this one.
  • ¡BOMBAZO!, Issue 2342. South of the Hamlet prison, check the ground floor of the western apartment row. Look on top of the toilet. The magazine rack at the Oldby Island Ron is the other route.

Try these three extra magazine titles

These are community-mapped pickups. After reading, check the in-game total before counting them toward 30. If you already read the same title elsewhere, move on to the next one.

  • Drive Shaft. In Crosstown, head to Dolos Exotic Car Rental on North Vice Avenue and check the table in the rear office. On Vespucci Island, another reported copy is on the garage counter at the large villa on Neptune Circle.
  • Endowed. In southern Waning Sands, check the white villa by the lake. Look low on the left side of the living-room bookcase. A second reported copy sits on a cabinet upstairs in the modern mansion on the northwest side of Vespucci Island.
  • Manly Gaze. The detailed listing points to Farmer Scrumpy’s Red Apple Orchard off SR-73, west of Manifest Canyon. Check the barrel in the small back room. Other maps assign this title to the Port Gellhorn area, so use the orchard as your landmark.

Find all seven Book Swaps

Use the cabinet’s interaction prompt and open the book. Match its title against your checklist before leaving.

  1. The Wizard’s Ruin: Loud Clap of the Night · In Watson Bay, go to Beaumont Avenue and Longship Drive. Check the white house with a flag.
  2. Killer Prom · On Dolphin Drive in northeast Key Lento, look near the road outside the blue house.
  3. Badlands Revenge: Ride Like Hell · In Capri, head to the Halloween house at Fortuna Street and Viola Street.
  4. Thighlander: A Tale of True Scotsmen · In Southside, look beside the red house near Reggie’s Family Soul Food.
  5. Goodnight, Dollface, Goodnight · On Vespucci Island, go to Neptune Circle at Rialto Causeway. The cabinet is beside the villa.
  6. Outback Gas Junkie · At the corner of Mamey Circle in southern Waning Sands, check the verge outside the cream-colored house.
  7. Humanity vs Space · Look along Reservoir Street near the southwest shore of Lake Leonida, across the water from Sweetie Pie Cafe.

29 magazine copies by area

The labels below are map names for physical copies. The numbers help tell them apart by location. Use the reading prompt and the game’s collection counter to see which titles still count on your save.

    Jason’s waterfront house, Key Lento. Go to the upper patio at the back and check the table by the workout area.
  • Off the Hook 1
    At the same house, go down to the little shed and look on its workbench.
  • Off the Hook 2
    On eastern Surfside Drive, enter the cream-colored waterfront house. Look on the dining table.
  • Pêche 2
    Find the Dolphin Drive party house. Take the outside stairs and check the bed in the first bedroom on the left.
  • Trigger Happy 2
    At the white waterfront house on Sunset Drive in southwest Key Lento, look at the corner of the dining table.

    Take a boat to the fishing sheds in western Gullet Marsh. Search the dock by the folding chair.
Kelly County · 5
  • Drive Shaft 1
    Follow the railroad into central Draper Island. Look for the locked yellow container on top of the stack. The reported copy is at the back on a beer pallet.
  • Manly Gaze
    Turn off SR-73 for Farmer Scrumpy’s Red Apple Orchard, west of Manifest Canyon. Search the barrel in the small back room.
  • Off the Hook 3
    Off Washburn Avenue in north Port Gellhorn, enter the blue-and-white trailer and check the living-room dresser.
  • Trigger Happy 3
    Find the red-and-white trailer by Gellhorn Avenue in west Port Gellhorn. The reading copy is on the couch.
  • Pêche 3
    In southeast Buckskin Swamp, find Cougar’s Cave on Cargo Road. Inspect the rack at the back of the closed shop.
Leonard & Lummox counties · 4
  • Foiled 2
    On Grace Avenue in Belville, look for the large yellow house. Check the corner of the bed in the garage.
  • Endowed 2
    Go to the white villa by the lake in south Waning Sands. Look low on the left side of the living-room bookshelves.
  • Trigger Happy 1
    At the logging camp southeast of Lake Tallaloko, check the cabinet against the wall in the lone modular house.

Vice City, Vice Beach & Hamlet · 14

  • ¡Bombazo! 2
    At the red apartment block on Formosa Avenue in Hamlet, check the toilet in the ground-floor unit second from the right.
  • ¡Bombazo! 3
    On Caledonia Boulevard in eastern La Perle, find the white-and-yellow trailer. Look at the living-room couch.
  • Body Copy 2
    Find the Moon tower on SW 2nd Street in Downtown. In the Zenith gym, look on the padded block in the corner.
  • Body Copy 3
    In Ajax Gym on Vineyard Drive, Oldby Island, check the ledge in front of the Fuel Stop kiosk.

    At the large villa on Neptune Circle, south Vespucci Island, search the garage counter.
  • Drive Shaft 3
    At Dolos Exotic Car Rental on North Vice Avenue in Crosstown, check the middle table in the rear office.
  • Endowed 1
    Find the modern mansion in northwest Vespucci Island. Check the cabinet in the top-floor room.
  • Endowed 3
    On the north side of Leaf Links, go behind the big mansion and look at the poolside couch.
  • High Rollerz 1
    Board the cruise ship on the north side of Port Vice City and check the lifeguard counter on the pool deck.
  • High Rollerz 2
    At Lotus Two in Tequesta Retreat, look for the gazebo by the Bayside Drive roundabout. Check the patio table.
  • High Rollerz 3
    Inside Jack of Hearts in Crosstown, go upstairs to the recording studios. Check the table against the wall in the adjoining storage room.
  • 11:59 2
    Find the mansion on the north side of the southern Peacock Estates island. Check the workbench against the back of its garage.
  • Pêche 1
    On Palladia Lane in southern Peacock Estates, find the detached garage full of mannequins. Look on the crate in the middle.

Count unique reads across both menus

In Progress → Collections → Exploration, add the Magazines, Books & Comics count to the Book Swaps count. If you see a familiar title on a different rack, a second copy will add nothing. Style unlocks have a separate tracker.
