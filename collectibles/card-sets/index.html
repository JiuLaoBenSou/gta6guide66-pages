All 18 collectible card sets

Use Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards to see the set and variant you still need. Three full sets unlock Don’t Touch My Deck. Fifteen full sets count toward Story Mode 100%.

Make two stops inside Starlet Motel before Chapter 2 ends

  • Vicki (Delights Dancers). Enter reception, take the door opposite the counter and check the bathroom off the next room. The card is on the floor near the toilet.
  • Victor Maravilla (Los Santos Corkers). Check the refrigerator on the west side of the motel kitchen. Look for the card on the fridge door before leaving the motel.

Both motel rooms become unavailable after Chapter 2. If you missed Vicki, a second location is reported under a chair on the uppermost roof of the Southside apartments at SW 10th Ave and SW 11th St. Check Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards after any pickup.

Key Lento card route

  1. Aimie, Pink (NINE1NINE). In the Rusty Anchor, find the Captain’s Log Room and check the toilet stall. Some finders index this card as Amie.
  2. Lionel Taber (Los Santos Pounders). At the north end of Dolphin Drive, find the party house by the road toward Corsair Key. Take its outside stairs to the upper entrance. Look on the toilet in the bathroom to the left.
  3. Georgia, Purple (NINE1NINE). From the Badger tower area, cross Sunbelt Highway to Steven Strachan Construction. Enter at the rear and look on the storage boxes next to a green container.
  4. Wokester, Blue (The Legends of Ratface). At The Wonder Whale clothing shop, go around to the northeast side and inspect the bench.

Pair these stops with Thelma and the other early pickups.

Port Gellhorn card route

  • Harley (Delights Dancers). Look at the booth seat along the east wall inside Delights. If you miss the club in Chapter 2, check it again after it becomes accessible later.
  • Chrissie, Pink (NINE1NINE). Look for an open trailer on Baywood Road east of Uncle Jack’s Liquor. Inspect the back of the armchair.
  • Kelly, Deep Red (Jack of Hearts). In north Port Gellhorn, follow the dirt track west from Gellhorn Avenue to the pink-and-white trailer. Check the bottom drawer in the bedroom.
  • Lexi Campbell, Gold (Jack of Hearts). Return after obtaining Yasmani’s Snap Gun. At Gellhorn Drive-In, open the painted building beside the movie screen. Use the left-hand door and inspect the desk safe in the equipment room.

Ten more Port Gellhorn pickups

  • Funny Bones, Orange (ED Comics Universe). Climb the water tower on Old Post Road in the north. Check under the chair on the top platform.
  • Fornicator, Pink (ED Comics Universe). From Pitchpole Road on the west side, go down to the sand and look beside the sofa and garden gnome.
  • Swallow, Purple (The Legends of Ratface). Look for the red trailer by the water along Gellhorn Avenue. Inside, check the cassette rack in the living room.
  • General Surgeon, Pink (ED Comics Universe). On Orchard Avenue, look for a green mobile home on a corner lot. Check the side of its refrigerator.
  • Side Effect, Light Blue (ED Comics Universe). Find the red trailer on Crescent Court, northwest of Dunk’s trailer. Check the bedroom dresser along the wall.
  • Bingo Johnson, Standard (Los Santos Pounders). Check the mobile home southeast of La Cascara Motel. The card is on the freezer door of the fridge next to the kitchen sink.
  • Julian Cruz, Bronze (Vice City Flamingos). In a home along the west part of Gellhorn Avenue, check the back room for the shelf over the laundry basket.
  • Christian Freeman, Silver (Liberty City Swingers). At I-404 and Gellhorn Avenue, climb to the highest walkway of the drive-in sign. Look up there before heading inside for Lexi Campbell.
  • Beck R. Heffer, Orange (Vice City Narcos). In Powell’s Construction & Lumber Supply by I-404, check the office corkboard behind the monitor.
  • Kyle Lewicki, Silver (Vice City Narcos). At the Washburn Avenue ballfield, go to the southernmost bleachers and look on the highest row.

Vice City pickups to pair with missions

  • Julian Cruz, Gold (Vice City Flamingos). From Yasmani’s Little Cuba shop, check the building directly south. Look on the board in a small office on the north side of its hallway.
  • Captain Kreuks, Blue (The Legends of Ratface). Go to the unfinished building north of TWA Ponya Tattoo in La Perle and check its top floor.
  • Tracy (Delights Dancers). During Power Play, check the riverside building at the Vice-Dale Water Department on Westbrook Drive in Salton. Take the exterior ladder onto the roof, climb the low wall and step through the open doorway. Look by the folding chair just inside.
  • Ulysses Whitlock, Silver (Vice City Flamingos). At the helipad building east of Grand Majestic Gym in Tequesta, take the elevator to the roof and check the spiral stair’s top landing.
  • Sione Apa, Red (Los Santos Pounders). At Vice Seven Condominiums in Tequesta, enter the gym and check the changing room on its south side.
  • Ichiro Yamada (Liberty City Swingers). During Doggie Bag in Chapter 5, check Port VC Terminal on Gulf Street. Go through the open warehouse shutter and take the open doors to the left. Behind the doors marked Storage, look for the card on the large wood table.

Find nine cards in Belville

North side houses

  • Franklin Turner, Bronze (Los Santos Corkers). Find the row of houses west of Andy’s place on the north edge of town. Check the garage counter in the westernmost house.
  • Malena, Deep Red (Jack of Hearts). In the western group on that row, go to the house at the east end. The horse in the yard is your landmark. Go to the rear bedroom and check the workbench below the mannequin’s hand.
  • Illuminaughty, Purple (The Legends of Ratface). Move to the middle group of houses and count two from the west. Use the garage entrance and check the noticeboard inside.
  • Angel Cienfuegos, Silver (Vice City Flamingos). Find the ring-shaped lake north of town. The white house immediately south of the lake has the card in its garage.

The Thirsty Wolf and two Xero stops

  • Delord Nathan Alford, Standard (Vice City Manatees). Go to the closed shops south of The Thirsty Wolf. Drop through the roof opening into the laundromat and follow the barking to the back room. Check the shelf for the card and just below it for the 62412 clue to the wall safe.
  • Chico Bonez, Standard (Backyard Wrestling). From the Xero Food Mart, check the garage northwest of the store.
  • Kelly, Pink (Jack of Hearts). Use the Xero on Caledonia Boulevard on the south side of Belville. Enter from behind the station and check the floor of the staff restroom.

Cellerica and Harmony Drive

  • Garth Buxton (Liberty City Swingers). Find the Cellerica warehouse east of Brilliant Cuts. The card is at the phone box on the back of the warehouse. Pick it up while you are in the area for Read the Label or Puff Puff Pass.
  • Leroy Rook, Gold (Vice City Narcos). Check the bedroom safe in the Harmony Drive house southeast of the Cellerica complex. The code for this safe is still unverified.

Cards off the main roads

  • J.D. Wild (Backyard Wrestling). In Grayson, look for a yellow box on the back deck of a house immediately north of Pay ’n’ Spray. PowerPyx also recorded the card inside a shack northeast of the Manatee Motel operation, at the gator prop.
  • Braelynn (NINE1NINE). At Hoffman Observatory in northern Kelly County, use the wrecked car to reach the platform outside, then follow it around and climb inside. Look near the magazines on the wooden platform below.
  • Malena, Pink (Jack of Hearts). Find the lookout tower just north of Rusty Willis Jr. Hunting Supplies on Forest Mill Road. At the top, check just inside the doorway on the left.
  • The Ratster, Purple (The Legends of Ratface). Climb the watchtower in the middle of Buckskin Swamp and check the upper railing by the binoculars.
  • MonaLi$a, Gold (Jack of Hearts). Take a watercraft southeast from Abismo Key. On the small island, look beside the deck chair near the boat.
  • Pricilla Pace, Silver (Jack of Hearts). On the middle island of Permit Keys, look along the north beach for wooden huts. Check the little book exchange outside.
  • Jesenia (NINE1NINE). Take a jet ski to the boats just north of Permit Keys. Look on the table under the pink umbrella.
  • Laki Maivia, Bronze (Los Santos Pounders). On the southwest shore of Cottonmouth Lake, look inside the trailer. Check the photos on the left wall for 44017, then use it to open the safe.
  • Maximus Goldbart, Yellow (Los Santos Pounders). In Waning Sands, check the villa garage east of Pay ’n’ Spray. It is the same stop as Randy’s Pegassi Vitello pickup.

Delights Dancers · Find all 18 cards

In Chapter 2, choose Help in Afternoon Delight if you want to follow the documented route to the Jason card. Once you get the club’s reopening text in Chapter 3, return to Delights for the card on the restroom counter. The sources don’t show a pickup after choosing the other branch.

Five cards already have routes above or in the early pickups guide. Check Vicki before leaving the motel, Harley on a Delights booth, Tracy at Salton’s water department, Thelma at the Badger tower and Perla above the Balsero rail yard.

Vice City and the airport area

  • Crystal. Off Royale Street in Downtown East, check the side alley and look under a chair on the raised stone patio.
  • Cyrus. At Park Apartments in the eastern VCIA district, go to the top floor and count two doors from the right. Look on the side of the kitchen fridge.
  • Daisy. In northern La Perle, go behind the Xero counter to the kitchenette and check the corkboard on the wall.
  • Kandi. At Northbrook Drive and NW 4th Avenue in Little Cuba, go upstairs from Farmacia Tu Salud. Check the toilet in the first apartment on the second floor.
  • Lance. Follow Vice Beach’s south shore to the lifeguard station. Look on the desk inside.
  • Lucky. Find the mobile home at Caldonia Boulevard and NW 9th Avenue in La Perle. Check the dresser by the bed.
  • Mario. Look along the roof edge of the Vice-Dale County Water and Sewage building where the airport area meets Southside.

Hamlet, Watson Bay and the inland stops

  • Nicole. On Formosa Avenue in central Hamlet, use the middle block of homes. Count two units from the right and look beside the bed.
  • Chip. On the northern island of Watson Bay, check the waterfront house on Kingston Road. Look in the bathroom by the toilet.
  • Amber. Go to the Agricore mining tower in southern Leonard County. Check underneath the chair on the outside landing by the side entrance.
  • Cleo. At the south end of Kalaga Reservoir, find the small utility building. Check the large battery along the back wall of the storage room.
  • Nevaeh. Go into the hills above Bonanza and look for a barn partly broken away by a landslide. Check the workbench inside the standing section.

  • Los Santos sports: Corkers, Panic and Pounders.

  • Comics: The Legends of Ratface, Right Wing: America’s Hero and Ed Comics Universe.

Open the set in your save for its card count. Published guides disagree on the Manatees total, so use the in-game count when planning that set. GTA6.fr’s French catalog and LorenZone’s English index list 480 variants. Both use the same underlying location catalog. The routes above cite the maps and field reports they use. Check the card in your collection after each stop.

Check the variant before selling

A different color of the same card can fill a separate slot. A second copy of the exact same variant adds no set progress. Cards already registered in Progress stay on that checklist when sold, even if they disappear from the physical collection at home. Look under Exploration → Collectors → Pawn Shops for a buyer that deals in cards.
