Make two stops inside Starlet Motel before Chapter 2 ends

  • Vicki (Delights Dancers). Enter reception, take the door opposite the counter and check the bathroom off the next room. The card is on the floor near the toilet.
  • Victor Maravilla (Los Santos Corkers). Check the refrigerator on the west side of the motel kitchen. Look for the card on the fridge door before leaving the motel.

Both motel rooms become unavailable after Chapter 2. If you missed Vicki, a second location is reported under a chair on the uppermost roof of the Southside apartments at SW 10th Ave and SW 11th St. Check Progress → Collections → Collectible Cards after any pickup.

Key Lento card route

  1. Aimie, Pink (NINE1NINE). In the Rusty Anchor, find the Captain’s Log Room and check the toilet stall. Some finders index this card as Amie.
  2. No. 3 Lionel Taber (Los Santos Pounders). Find the party house on Dolphin Drive in Key Lento, by the road toward Corsair Key. Climb the stairs outside and use the upper entrance. Turn left as you enter and pick up the card from the toilet in the bathroom.
  3. Georgia, Purple (NINE1NINE). From the Badger tower area, cross Sunbelt Highway to Steven Strachan Construction. Enter at the rear and look on the storage boxes next to a green container.
  4. Santiago Lopez, Standard (Los Santos Panic). From Jason’s first safehouse, head west along Peach Palm Drive. Enter the mobile home and check the table. The pickup notification should add Santiago to Los Santos Panic.
  5. Dartanyon Pearson (Liberty City Wrath). While you are in the north-central Key Lento house for The Big Lead, look along the fireplace ledge. A separate run got Dartanyon from a card box, so check the collection menu before revisiting the house.
  6. Wokester, Blue (The Legends of Ratface). At The Wonder Whale clothing shop, go around to the northeast side and inspect the bench.

Pair these stops with Thelma and the other early pickups.

Port Gellhorn card route

  • Harley (Delights Dancers). Look at the booth seat along the east wall inside Delights. If you miss the club in Chapter 2, check it again after it becomes accessible later.
  • Chrissie, Pink (NINE1NINE). Look for an open trailer on Baywood Road east of Uncle Jack’s Liquor. Inspect the back of the armchair.
  • Kelly, Deep Red (Jack of Hearts). In north Port Gellhorn, follow the dirt track west from Gellhorn Avenue to the pink-and-white trailer. Check the bottom drawer in the bedroom.
  • Lexi Campbell, Gold (Jack of Hearts). Return after obtaining Yasmani’s Snap Gun. At Gellhorn Drive-In, open the painted building beside the movie screen. Use the left-hand door and inspect the desk safe in the equipment room.

North side houses

  • Franklin Turner, Bronze (Los Santos Corkers). Find the row of houses west of Andy’s place on the north edge of town. Check the garage counter in the westernmost house.
  • Malena, Deep Red (Jack of Hearts). In the western group on that row, go to the house at the east end. The horse in the yard is your landmark. Go to the rear bedroom and check the workbench below the mannequin’s hand.

  • Delord Nathan Alford, Standard (Vice City Manatees). Go to the closed shops south of The Thirsty Wolf. Drop through the roof opening into the laundromat and follow the barking to the back room. Check the shelf for the card and just below it for the 62412 clue to the wall safe.
  • Chico Bonez, Standard (Backyard Wrestling). From the Xero Food Mart, check the garage northwest of the store.

Check the variant before selling

A different color of the same card can fill a separate slot. A second copy of the exact same variant adds no set progress. Cards already registered in Progress stay on that checklist when sold, even if they disappear from the physical collection at home. Look under Exploration → Collectors → Pawn Shops for a buyer that deals in cards.
