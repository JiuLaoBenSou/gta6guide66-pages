Style magazines and luxury watches

Scan style magazines and pick up luxury watches to fill Discovered Styles. The collection contains 79 entries. Any 70 count toward 100%.

Hair and makeupTattoosLuxury watchesKingpin BowlingMore watch leads

Scan magazines for hair and makeup

Use WAiNK on the magazine. If a character-specific style does not register, switch to the character it belongs to and check their collection.

  • Sunset Blush Makeup. On the north coast of Key Lento, enter the house and check the bedroom.
  • Ear Web Buzz Cut Hair. In Hamlet, try the trailer-park house west of Righto Cash & Carry.
  • Goth Garnet Makeup. Check the rear office of the warehouse just north of Hamlet Police Department.
  • High Bun and Mymy Clip. As Lucia, go upstairs in the house with the Franklin Turner Gold card, near the base of Capri’s southeast peninsula.
  • Light Blond Wavy Spikes. As Jason, check the sunbed in a back garden near the middle of the gated community south of Catalan Key.
  • Diamond Adorned Pink Cat Eye. Find the mannequin-filled house on Belville’s northern row and scan the magazine from the living-room table.
  • Spiky Bun and Framing. As Lucia, check the roof of the tall building above the three clothing stores at the south end of Vice Beach.

Find tattoo magazines

  • Graffiti Point. Check the room at the top of the Mount Kalaga firewatch tower.
  • The Tower and High Priestess. Look on the mattress on the ground floor of Hoffman Observatory.
  • Humanatee and Seductive Mermaid. At the Oceanarium loading area, enter the warehouse room on the northeast side of Catalan Key.
  • Glide or Die Hovercraft. Check the deck chairs at the hovercraft wreck on the southernmost Sandbars island.

Find the first watches

  • Novak Laurent Alcazar · Key Lento. At the Dolphin Drive party house, use the upstairs door and take the first bedroom on the left. Look by the window near Pêche.
  • Crowex Octane “Orca” · Kingpin Bowling. After Coffee Break in Chapter 2, track Hit Kingpin Bowling in Port Gellhorn. From the front desk, walk into the office with the security monitors. Check the small red room beside it for the watch on the round table. Look for the gold watch icon.
  • Crowex Octane “Elite” · Palmer Fishing Warehouse. Go to the fenced compound by the bridge on Bobcat Key. Check the lounge table for the gold pickup while sweeping the warehouse. Published directions differ on the floor, so follow the pickup icon.
  • B&G Ark Royal Quantum · Watson Bay Safehouse. Check the desk by the monitors before leaving the safehouse. See the upstairs route.
  • Crowex Octane “Bobby Ambrose” · Dubious Provenance. When Halifax offers to appraise the statue, accept his offer to get the watch as collateral. If you declined, use the optional call before the Chapter 3 cutoff. Go to the mission steps.
  • Crowex Ambassador “Breakfast” · Marbelle Hotel. Follow the roof-note sequence to unlock the ground-floor safe. Go to the hotel steps.

Check five more operations for watches

Open each operation’s map card and check Key Valuables before entering. These listings name the watch to look for. The pickup rooms still need confirmation.

  • Kamar Devon Community Center · Southside. Look for the Novak Laurent Argonaut at the PTT Youngin$ operation, which also lists their K17.
  • Southside Trap House · Southside. Check this separate PTT Youngin$ operation for a B&G Ark Royal Ashore.
  • Saber Shipping Facility · Balsero. The Guardia Brothers facility also lists an Ark Royal Ashore. Try it if you missed the Southside watch, and compare the collection count after pickup.
  • Casa Guardia · Little Cuba. Search near NW 4th Avenue for the Guardia Brothers operation. Check the map card for the Crowex Ambassador and the Benefactor Nightlight Schafter.
  • Leonida Carrier Warehouse · Balsero. Look by Omega Parkway for the warehouse with a Canis Gunrunner Kamacho and a GRX 556 on the valuables list. The same listing names the Crowex Octane “Midas.”

Use the gang operation maps and safe routes to find these buildings. Look for the gold watch icon in side rooms before leaving. There are 34 watches and 45 magazine unlocks, so even with all 45 magazine unlocks you need at least 25 watches for the 70-style target.

Keep your reads and styles separate

Check Progress → Collections → Discovered Styles for the watch and magazine categories. If a book only opens a reading screen, check Magazines, Books & Comics for that progress instead.
