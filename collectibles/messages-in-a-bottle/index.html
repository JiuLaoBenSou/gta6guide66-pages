Messages in a Bottle

Take a jet ski northwest from Key Lento toward the Shoeless Keys. All six bottles are on land. The order below follows the islands, with the number printed on each note.

Collect the six bottles around Gambit Bay

Use Examine at each bottle. Check the number on the paper before leaving the island so you can identify any you missed.

  1. Note 5/6 · Eastern Shoeless Keys. Start west of the causeway, north of Gambit Bay. Pick out the bent island and land near its northeast tip. Walk the sandy edge until the Examine prompt appears.
  2. Note 4/6 · Wedge-shaped sandbar. From the first island, ride southwest to the isolated wedge off northern Key Lento. Land on its southeast shore and check the sand by the low plants.
  3. Note 3/6 · Rocky islet. Head to the small islet just north of a larger curved island covered in mangroves. Walk among the exposed rocks instead of following the waterline.
  4. Note 6/6 · Under the mangroves. Move southwest to the hooked, green island and leave your jet ski at the shore. Walk inland to the sandy break under the bushes and search beside the dry plants.
  5. Note 1/6 · Blue boat wreck. Search the two close-set islands west of central Key Lento for a blue boat washed ashore. At the wreck, look by the damaged frame and the debris on the beach.
  6. Note 2/6 · Narrow north–south island. Find the slim island with a longer diagonal sandbar just below it on the map. Walk its south end. The bottle lies on the sand near dark plants and red debris.

Check that the collection is complete

After examining the sixth bottle, look for the Message in a Bottle Complete notice. The notes tell Yordanis Castillo Perez’s story. The recorded run ended with the collection notice, with no added dive marker or cash payout.

Find the separate underwater wrecks and Kenton’s treasure sets.
