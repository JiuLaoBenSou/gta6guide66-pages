Meteorite locations

Use the purple glow to spot fragments on the shore, in caves and in a few buildings. The routes cover 26 reported pickups, with a separate walkthrough for the purple crater on Mount Kalaga.

Find the purple crater near Hillside Campsite

Go to Hillside Campsite on Mount Kalaga, southwest of Dawson Dam. Walk off the path toward the purple haze in the trees. Look in the shallow crater and use the Meteorite pickup prompt.

Belville, Waning Sands and Killtelligence

  • Belville. On the middle stretch of the northern row of homes, check the second garage from the west. Look under the bed in the garage with Foiled and the Illuminaughty card.
  • Waning Sands. Enter the Sands of Time retirement home on Vinewood Beach. Go downstairs and past the pool table. Check the desk in the office beyond it.
  • Killtelligence Provisions. From Bret’s lodge, head southeast to the tower and look among the rocks below it. Use the nearby BASE jump and meditation spot to recognize the ledge.

Recover five fragments at Selkie Hole

  1. On the west edge of Emerald Springs, go to the buildings beside the small lake. Take the stairs down to the diving spot. The platform with the shaft of light is also a WAiNK scan.
  2. Enter the flooded tunnel and search for the first two purple pickups. Keep track of what you collect before continuing.
  3. For the last three, surface in the cavern, then dive through the hole in the center. Follow the lower passages and use the scanner to pick out the glow. The published route gives the cave path and the five-pickup total. Exact spots within the tunnels can be checked with the scanner.

Belpoint and Leonida Bay · four fragments

  • Belpoint. Go to the north shore and inspect the water and sand under the pier.
  • South of Bobcat Key. Land on the lone island in western Leonida Bay. Look on the SOS sign, then cross to the other beach for a second fragment.
  • Leonida Bay lighthouse. Visit its small island and look below the lighthouse.

The Sandbars, Zephyr and Featherstone · six fragments

  • Southern Sandbars island. Land on the north beach. Look around the shore near the hovercraft wreck.
  • Northern Sandbars island. On the largest island, look under the pier at the north of the group.
  • Eastern Sandbars. Check the north tip of the easternmost island. Then head south to the little island at the bottom of the eastern group and search its south tip.
  • Zephyr Islands. Check the northeast shore of the largest island. Use the stranded yacht as your landmark.
  • Featherstone Islands. At the Wreck of the Meerwunder, walk close to the hull and look below the fallen mast.

Saint Attaway, Hyde, Frampton and East Lummox · eight fragments

  • Saint Attaway Islands. Check the small island on the west edge of the group. For a second fragment, land on the southeastern island and cross the fallen trees to the high cliff. Search beside the trees at the top.
  • Hyde Island. Land at the little dock in the west inlet and head north. Look below the bridge.
  • Frampton Islands, western shore. Sweep the west shore of the large island. Continue a short way north to the cliff for another fragment.
  • Frampton Islands, southeastern shore. Check the southeast tip of the southeastern island. Then cross to the island directly south and check the same side of its shore.
  • East Lummox County. Zoom in on the tiny island just north of the letter M in the county label. Land there and check the log.

Check what you picked up

Check for a Meteorite pickup or a pawn shop’s collectibles list after exploring. The 100% checklist has no separate meteorite requirement. The total across Leonida and the relationship between the crater pickup and the reported fragment locations still need confirmation.
