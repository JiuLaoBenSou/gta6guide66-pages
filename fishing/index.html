How to catch a bigger fish for Cal in GTA 6

After Feng Shui, look for Cal’s text. At the marked spot in Gambit Bay, catch a stoplight parrotfish heavier than 2 lb and send it to him from the catch screen.

Get to the fishing marker

Track Cal’s challenge as Jason. Take a boat out to the marked water in Gambit Bay. If you’re doing it later and the boat at Jason’s place is gone, PowerPyx’s route suggests looking for boats near the Rusty Anchor docks. Use the boat’s vehicle menu to anchor so it stays at the spot.

If you’re in the area and nothing bites

  1. Equip the rod and use the Small Saltwater Lure. That basic lure worked in the documented challenge.
  2. Click both sticks to use Focus and look for fish before casting. Cast toward the activity you see. If the area is quiet, try at dawn or dusk and compare a different patch of water within the marker.
  3. Use the on-screen retrieve and line-tension cues. Change Settings → Gameplay → Fishing if the default reeling motion is difficult.

Make sure the catch counts

Check the species and weight on the catch screen. Cal asked for a stoplight parrotfish over 2 lb. Use the send action when the screen offers it for your qualifying catch. Check the objective afterward so you know it registered.
