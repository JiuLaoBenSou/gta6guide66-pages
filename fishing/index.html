GTA 6 fishing and Cal’s challenge

After Feng Shui, look for Cal’s text. At the marked spot in Gambit Bay, catch a stoplight parrotfish heavier than 2 lb and send it to him from the catch screen.

Catch a Bigger Fish in Gambit Bay

After Feng Shui, read Cal’s text as Jason and track Catch a Bigger Fish. Take a boat to the purple circle west of Key Lento. If the boat at Jason’s house is gone, check the Rusty Anchor docks. Hold D-pad Left, open Vehicle Properties and select Anchor once inside the circle.

If you’re in the area and nothing bites

  1. Equip the rod and use the Small Saltwater Lure. That basic lure worked in the documented challenge.
  2. Click both sticks to use Focus and look for fish before casting. Cast toward the activity you see. If the area is quiet, try at dawn or dusk and compare a different patch of water within the marker.
  3. Use the on-screen retrieve and line-tension cues. Change Settings → Gameplay → Fishing if the default reeling motion is difficult.

Send Cal a photo of the qualifying catch

Check the catch screen for Stoplight Parrotfish and a weight above 2 lb. Use Take Photo for Cal with Cross on PS5 or A on Xbox when the option appears. Check for the Baited My Tackle trophy or achievement.

Pick up a rod and reel in a fish

  1. In Chapter 1, walk to the jetty west of Jason’s house and pick up a rod. Hold L1 / LB and open the equipment slot to select it.
  2. Hold L2 / LT to grip the rod. Hold R2 / RT and release to cast. Rotate the right stick slowly to work the lure.
  3. On the bite prompt, press R2 / RT. Steer with the left stick while the fish pulls, then rotate the right stick to reel when it tires. If a cast goes quiet, move the lure or cast toward another patch of fish.

Choose a lure for the water

The rod comes with a Small Freshwater Lure and Small Saltwater Lure. At Chuck’s Tackle & Shot in Key Lento, fishing stock includes Medium Freshwater for GTA$45, Medium Saltwater for GTA$56 and Large Saltwater for GTA$125. Check for the fish icon when visiting other Ammu-Nation stores and confirm the price at the counter.

Get the Candy Cane Lure and catch the Candy Fish

  1. Look for a house in the grassland southwest of Ambrosia. Check the desk in the garage for the Candy Cane Lure. The street address of the pickup is still unverified.
  2. Take the lure to Lake Leonida. Equip it on the rod, use Focus to look for movement in the water, and cast toward the fish.
  3. After landing the catch, check for Candy Fish on the catch screen and under Progress → Animals → Fishing.

All 35 fish and their hotspots

Search for the fish by name in the Leonida map and equip the suggested lure before casting. The table uses community marker names and includes alternative fishing grounds where reported. If the nearby terrain and lure name seem to disagree, start with the lure listed for the species. The recommended size for Spanish Mackerel and any fish-specific lure for Black Drum are still unconfirmed.

Finish Pro Angler and check the fishing challenges

  • Pro Angler. Land all 35 species with the rod. Recheck Progress → Animals → Fishing after each trip and use the missing names to plan the next one. An underwater Study goes to the animal log separately.
  • Angler. Land four different species within six minutes. Scout a spot with several species before attempting the timed run.
  • Big Fish. Land a fish weighing at least 20 lb. Try a larger saltwater lure on a listed offshore species and check the weight on the catch screen.
  • Catch of the Day. Land three fish with each lure type in one game day. Buy or collect your lures first so the trip to the store doesn’t use up fishing time.
