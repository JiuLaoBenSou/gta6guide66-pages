Watson Bay Safehouse

In Watson Bay, look for the moth icon on the north side of the neighborhood in the Illegal Operations filter. Use the blue tarp on the fence to get inside. The rifle, safe and watch are upstairs.

EntranceVF-G1Safe codeWatchGarage

Find the blue tarp and the upstairs door

  1. Open Map → Robberies → Illegal Operations. Pick Watson Bay Safehouse at the gold moth icon. The game card names La Polilla Negra.
  2. Follow the outside fence toward the water until you see the blue tarp. Climb over that section.
  3. From the pool, face the house and take the stairs on the right. Use the white door with glass panels at the top.

Pick up the La Polilla Negra VF-G1

Step into the room off the kitchen. Look under the window for the open black hard case on the table. Use the pickup prompt for La Polilla Negra VF-G1 Assault Rifle. The variant in the recorded run had a sight and suppressor.

Read the kitchen email for the safe code

  1. Check the computer next to the kitchen window. Read the last line of the email. Two published runs recorded 1819. Use the code from your own email if it differs.
  2. Look beneath the microwave for the black safe. Select Enter Code and use the keypad. Open Notes on the keypad screen if you need to check the email again.
  3. Use the Cash prompt on both stacks after the safe opens. The map card’s Take Value includes more than the cash in the safe.

Collect the Ark Royal Quantum watch

Before leaving the upper floor, check the desk by the monitors for the B&G Ark Royal Quantum. Check the Luxury Watches menu after pickup.

Find the Black Moth Kamacho

Go back downstairs to the side of the house that faces the main gate. The garage door is next to stacked wooden crates. The operation lists the Canis Black Moth Kamacho as a key valuable. Check the in-game warning before changing its unique parts. Permanently storing this garage pickup was outside the documented run.
