GTA 6 Top Fun Drone Trials

Win one trial at every Top Fun venue for the trophy. For Story Mode 100%, finish all three trials at each of the 14 venues.

How to open all three trials

  1. Approach the venue until the blue drone icon appears. Select it under Map → Activities → Top Fun Drones. Bring a boat or aircraft to Dalton Island.
  2. Start Trial 1 and beat its par time. Do the same in Trial 2 to open the final course. If you keep missing par, get through the tight gaps cleanly before trying to hold full forward on the straights.
  3. Finish Trial 3 at any time for 100% credit. Check Progress → Activities → Top Fun Drones before leaving. You should have three finishes at each venue, with two under par.
