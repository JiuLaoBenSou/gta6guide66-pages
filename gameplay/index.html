  • Default and Classic controlsFind sprint, aiming and driving inputs.
  • Saving and replaying missionsFind manual slots and check what a checkpoint can restore.
  • Cash, BuckMe and ATMsStore cash and prepare for the Chapter 1 transition.

Activities

  • Strength, cardio and FocusUse strength and cardio equipment to train those two stats. Build Focus separately through meditation and story progression.
  • Meditation and FocusAt Jason’s Key Lento home, follow Lucia from the terrace to the water. Use both triggers for breathing and both sticks for the re-centering prompt.
  • Weather and time of dayFor a weather-dependent activity, first check the local conditions at its marker. Time and weather can change while you wait or move between areas.
  • GTA 6 nightclub locationsFind the entrances to NINE1NINE in Downtown Vice City, Delights in Port Gellhorn and Jack of Hearts in Crosstown. See when Delights reopens.
  • Catch a Bigger FishFind Cal’s fishing spot and catch a qualifying parrotfish.

Free roam & progression

  • Story safehousesYour active home changes through the story. Start with the current chapter’s map marker when an older door or storage spot is unavailable.
  • Earn and keep early cashWork toward your first GTA$3,000 with Chapter 1 job payouts, the Three Sheets Liquor stash and the Nova Auto Junkyard safe in Hamlet.
  • Wanted levels and search zonesCheck the on-screen search indicators. Breaking sight and changing the thing the game recognized solve different parts of the wanted state.
  • Partner commandsAfter Coffee Break in Chapter 2, use the partner menu in free roam. The available commands depend on who is with you and which activity is active.
  • Jason and Lucia’s relationshipFavor shared activities and positive choices from both leads. Use the in-game heart and progress feedback to see which actions counted in your save.
  • Auto DriveSet a destination, get into a car on the road, then enable Auto Drive from the cinematic camera.
  • Uncover the map and find an aircraftUse an aircraft in free roam to uncover several areas in one trip. Check the map between passes so you don’t miss narrow dark sections.
