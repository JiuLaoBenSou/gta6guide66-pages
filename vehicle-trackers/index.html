Vehicle trackers and registration

Scan the car with WAiNK first. A lock, an alarm and a tracker each appear separately, and they affect different actions.

Look at the Tracker line

  1. Use the phone’s WAiNK scanner and aim at the car. On the PlayStation layout the scan prompt uses R1. Follow the displayed button on your controller.
  2. None. For Moses’s Chapter 2 registration job, use a car that shows Tracker: None, then finish the garage’s Register prompt.
  3. Standard or Advanced. If you are trying to sell or keep the car later in the story, read Randy’s current instructions first. Advanced trackers require a later unlock for some garage interactions. A paint job by itself is not proof that a tracker is gone.

If the shop rejects the car

Check whether the car fits the current request, whether the search has ended, and whether you have unlocked the shop interaction. Confirm the game’s registration or sale message before leaving.
