GTA 6 Standard Issue Weapons

Story Mode 100% asks for 50 of the 66 Standard Issue Weapons. Pick up or equip a base item to log it, then check Progress → Weapons. Start with story weapons and ordinary shop stock.

How to register a weapon

  1. Use the full pickup or inventory label. When you obtain a new base item, check Progress → Weapons for its entry.
  2. A weapon you pick up can count without a purchase. If you plan to keep it, move it to the loadout, trunk or weapon locker and check the relevant prompt. Many yellow names are unique variants. Check the base entry before counting either version.
  3. For missing stock, locate one of the Expanded Ammu-Nation branches. Read the line on the weapon card before trying to pay.

What the shop’s status lines mean

Requires a bank account points to the BuckMe unlock. If the card asks for three or five more gun purchases, clear that prerequisite first. Currently sold out gives no exact restock time.

Compare the Select Weapon screen during Gear Up with regular shop prices. The Moreland 850 and Mediator were discounted at Chuck’s in the recorded run.

Some weapons have a separate activity requirement. The M306 TMR card points to shooting-range gold medals, while the SMS-01 is tied to Bret’s ninth hunting deck.
