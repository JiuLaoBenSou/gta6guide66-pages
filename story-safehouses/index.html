Story safehouses

Your active home changes through the story. Start with the current chapter’s map marker when an older door or storage spot is unavailable.

Recognize the early story bases

  • Chapter 1 · Key Lento. Jason’s home is in the Keys. Check the house’s own interaction prompts for training, repairs and storage.
  • Chapter 2 · Starlet Motel. Follow the motel objective in Port Gellhorn. See Linda through Overnight Charges when the story asks about the room. The temporary stay ends when you check out.
  • Chapter 3 · Bayside apartment. After arriving in Vice City, use the current apartment marker. Returning to the apartment can also trigger story work.
  • Chapter 4 · Y Hotel. Use the hotel map marker and read the objective title. Your room, the pool and the front desk can be separate destinations in the same building.
  • Chapter 5 · Penthouse. Top of the World, Ma opens in the penthouse bedroom. For the later apartment objectives, follow the current hotel or penthouse marker. The Chapter 5 garage and the story interior have separate map entries.

Before you leave a temporary home

Make a separate manual save in free roam. Use the in-game cash guide to check which storage method is accessible before the transition.
