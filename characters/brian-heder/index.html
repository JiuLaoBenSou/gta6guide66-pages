Brian Heder

Brian runs a boatyard in the Keys with Lori. Jason has a place to live through Brian and works for him in return.

Where Brian Heder fits into the story

Brian is the link between Jason’s home and the first set of local errands. Pay attention to the wording under each Story marker when it mentions him. Going to see Brian and collecting Brian’s rent can lead to separate jobs.
