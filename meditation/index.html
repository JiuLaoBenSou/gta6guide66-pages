Meditation and Focus

Find all ten meditation spots with maps and in-game screenshots. Check the Chapter 1 scene, the controller prompts, and the 15-session target for 100% completion.

Finish the first meditation

  1. During Chapter 1, wait for Lucia to go to the workout terrace. Look at her with L2 on PlayStation or LT on Xbox, accept the invitation and follow her down.
  2. Follow the breathing rings with L2 + R2 on PlayStation or LT + RT on Xbox. Match the prompts for pressing and releasing.
  3. When the view doubles and the stick markers appear, move both sticks toward the center of their rings. Stay through the HUD confirmation that Focus has been buffed.

All ten meditation locations

There are ten physical locations and eighteen recorded sessions. Do the opening Key Lento session in Chapter 1. After Coffee Break unlocks character switching, revisit the eight shared spots with the other character and check the counter under Progress → Collections → Exploration.

  • Jason’s house, Key Lento. Accept Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony invitation when it appears. It counts once.

  • Smooth Sand, Mariana County. Get to the small sandbar west of Capri.

  • Swamp, Kelly County. Check the dock below the lookout in Buckskin Swamp.

  • Worn Stone, Kelly County. Search the Emerald River bank at Camp Moonflower.

  • Red Rocks, Kelly County. Look where the railway crosses the road in Manifest Canyon.
    Map and location screenshot
  • Water Wheel, Lummox County. Go to the ruined mill by the river south of Moreau.
    Map and location screenshot
  • Jutting Cliff, Lummox County. Look by the tower above the mine in Mount Kalaga National Park.
    Map and location screenshot
  • Waterfall, Lummox County. Follow the river northwest of Tallaloko to the falls.
    Map and location screenshot
  • Seaside, Leonard County. Check the rocks along the beach east of Waning Sands.

  • Ascent Mountain, Lummox County. After the earlier spots, follow the new purple marker to the stone circle on Mount Kalaga’s summit. This last spot counts once.

100% completion and the trophy

Any 15 sessions satisfy the 100% completion checklist. The Chapter 1 terrace counts once. Skipping it has an unconfirmed effect on the separate meditation trophy and 18/18 total. Keep a Chapter 1 save if you plan to collect them all.
