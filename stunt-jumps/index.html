GTA 6 stunt jump locations

Find the 130 road jumps across Leonida. Check the map entry after you land to see whether your jump counted toward Cunning Stunts and Story Mode 100%.

What counts toward 100%?

Land the road jump and check that the game marked it complete. The challenge on the map marker is an extra objective. You can leave the flips, special vehicles and radio requirements for later. For Cunning Stunts, complete 250 stunts. For Story Mode 100%, complete 300. Both totals combine road jumps and the 267 flight and parachute stunts.

The Unlimited Nitrous cheat has a separate unlock requirement of 50 completed road jumps. Keep cheats off while you build progress you want to save.

Check whether a stunt counted

  1. Open Progress → Activities → Stunts and select the category. On Stunt Jumps, press the right stick to check your numbers by county.
  2. On the pause map, choose Exploration → Stunt Spots for the named entries. Purple means found. White means completed. Check the named entry before repeating a jump you already landed.

Mile Zero Air Station Southeast

  1. Drive to Goose Key. Take the lower branch at the split east of the air station to reach the southeast fence.
  2. After the barricades, line up with the dirt mound toward the fence. Leave room to accelerate and land in the airfield. See the Mile Zero access guide before entering the restricted area.

Pairs to look for in the Keys

  • Saltrock Bridge East and West. Look for the two named takeoffs on the old crossing between Abismo Key and Goose Key.
  • Key Lento Bridge East and West. Search the old Abismo Key–Key Lento crossing. Use the map names to tell the two takeoffs apart.

To try a bridge’s bonus challenge, aim to complete both halves in succession. For the completion counter, check each marker individually.
