Mile Zero Air Station

Find Mile Zero Air Station near Southwind. Decide whether you need the base itself or the nearby public route to the helicopter.

Enter Mile Zero Air Station from Goose Key

Set your waypoint to Mile Zero Air Station on Goose Key, at the southwest end of Mariana County. At the road split by the installation, the upper branch on the map in the recording goes to the front checkpoint. For the stunt jump, take the southern access road to the base’s southeast corner.

  1. Follow the road past the in-game barricades until you see a dirt mound facing the perimeter fence. The in-game jump is named Mile Zero Air Station Southeast.
  2. Line up the car with the mound and drive over it to land on the air station grounds. The front checkpoint and the jump both lead into a restricted area and can trigger an immediate wanted response.
  3. Once inside, check the vehicle rows by the large buildings for tanks or APCs. The clip also shows a helicopter on the open pavement and jets near the rounded hangars. Spawns can differ by visit.

Use the public waterfront for the early Maverick

For the early civilian helicopter, use the Southwind dock and boatworks route. That pickup is outside the air station.
