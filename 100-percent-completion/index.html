GTA 6 100% completion checklist

Finish the campaign to view Progress → Stats → 100% Completion. Earlier progress carries over. Use these 35 rows to work toward A Long Road before and after the credits.

Story & TasksExploration & ActivitiesDiscovery

What to track during the story

  1. Use the Vehicles, Weapons, Animals and Collections pages in Progress as you play. You can log finds before the overall checklist opens.
  2. Pair optional tasks with nearby collectible routes. Use fast routes for general counts and reserve complete sweeps for rows that require every entry.
  3. After the credits, compare your totals with Progress → Stats → 100% Completion. You can finish these requirements in post-story free roam. If you want a specific time-limited item or scene, check the missables guide while playing.

If you are one counter short

  • Bank jobs track a drawer and a safe as well as the vault at each large bank. Check that you have both the Bank Jobs and Vaults Hit objectives.
  • Check land jumps and the three air categories together. They feed the same 300-stunt row.
  • Keep the 33 dive discoveries, the five Kenton sets and the optional Exploration challenges separate when checking the menu.
