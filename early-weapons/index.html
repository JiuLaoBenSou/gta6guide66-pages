Early weapons and named variants

Match the pickup label to the weapon you are looking for. The same weapon family can have several named variants in different locations.

Starting gearRiflesPistolsShotgunLauncher

Set up a loadout you can recover

Finish Gear Up and use the personal car’s trunk and a store weapon locker once the relevant prompts appear. The 3DM free gear clip checks a parked patrol car’s trunk for a pickup. A nearby in-game officer, a missing interaction or a temporary pickup can change the result. For dependable body armor stock, check an Ammu-Nation.

Rifles by their in-game names

  • Marabunta Grande KZ-59. Find the Nova Auto Junkyard operation in Hamlet. Look for the gold rifle around the worktable at the covered area and check its pickup name. A gold vehicle in the same location is a separate pickup. Scan its Tracker field before trying to keep it.
  • Kingfishers W-22. Look for the Fishing Shacks operation in Wetman Bay after the early Kingfishers jobs. Confirm that the pickup says Kingfishers W-22 rather than a regular small-game rifle.
  • Free SM-69 at the Key Lento Badger tower. Find the fenced site beside Nifty Threads Islandwear. Cross the gate, climb the exterior stairs and ladders, then take the door by the control panels out onto the balcony. Look against the railing for the SM-69 Sniper pickup. Thelma is inside the control room. A separate named Killtelligence SM-69 is a reward from Bret’s Veteran Hunted.
  • Gun Nut SMS-01 lead near Jason’s house. Use the waypoint to find the two-story house in Key Lento. Go upstairs, pass the white couches and check the rifle case on the kitchen counter. The creator reports an intermittent pickup, so use a separate save and verify the exact variant on your own pickup screen.
  • FF-SR at Watson Bay. Find the fenced industrial yard on the shoreline peninsula across the channel from the main waterfront buildings. Take the gap in the chain-link fence and check the stacked pallets and wooden crates near the yellow wall for the FF-SR Sniper prompt. The named Internal Affairs FF-SR is listed separately at a Washington Beach rooftop helipad on Northside Avenue.
  • Kingfishers Laird 350 at Manatee Motel. Find the motel near Pelican Valley, south of Hamlet. Check the gun case in the room next to the cages. Confirm the complete variant name in the pickup text.
  • Hunter’s Laird 350 from the roadside encounter. During Chapter 1 free roam, check the junction at the Grassrivers edge south of Hamlet. If you hear shouting near the Pelican Valley Recreation Area sign, follow it into the brush. Offer help to the wounded hunter, get into the pickup when the objective appears and drive to the hospital. Check the rifle prompt after the drop-off. The encounter has appeared without a map marker and may be absent on a given visit.

Revolvers and the Type 9

  • Conspiracist Type 9. Check the Balsero mobile-home hatch southwest of Griffin Shooting Range for the underground armory. Compare the item name with the gun-store Type 9 variants.
  • Turnt Nipper .38 at the glider. Look for a paramotor caught in trees on the shore at the north end of Mariana County. In the recorded run, shooting the suspended glider brings it down. Approach the seat and check for the Turnt Nipper .38 pickup. The separate Port Vice City Cargo H&L .38 is listed in a yellow SCOOP container.
  • Donnie’s H&L .38. Make a manual save before starting Clear Skies. The source clip places the reported drop at the airfield after Jason frees Donnie. Check the pickup beside him if Donnie is down and the game still accepts input. Look for his name in the pickup label. Forcing the encounter can fail the mission, and repeatability across saves remains unconfirmed.

Find the Marabunta Grande Hobo shotgun in Hamlet

Check the storage buildings on the east side of town beside the highway. In the filmed encounter, a parked vehicle is playing music and voices come from inside.

Go through the right-side warehouse entrance and check the open weapon case in the room with chairs and cash. The game shows Marabunta Grande Hobo Shotgun at the case.

If the encounter does not appear, look for a regular shotgun at a gun store.

Find the free Hauners H-AT rocket launcher in Balsero

  1. Finish the Chapter 1 gun-store objective and verify that you can store weapons in Jason’s vehicle. The recorded route starts after this unlock.
  2. On the map, look north of Vice City International Airport, in Balsero. Go southwest of Griffin Shooting Range and use the impound lot in the footage as a landmark. Look for a weathered mobile home with a cluster of antennas on its roof.
  3. Enter the mobile home and open its floor hatch. In the room below, look for the Hauners H-AT propped beside the safe. Check the case next to the red tool chest for the Conspiracist Type 9. Confirm both names at their pickup prompts.

To buy a launcher, find an Expanded gun store and check its current stock and price.
