Early weapons and named variants

Match the pickup label to the weapon you are looking for. The same weapon family can have several named variants in different locations.

Starting gearRiflesPistolsShotgunLauncher

Set up a loadout you can recover

Finish Gear Up and use the personal car’s trunk and a store weapon locker once the relevant prompts appear. The 3DM free gear clip checks a parked patrol car’s trunk for a pickup. A nearby in-game officer, a missing interaction or a temporary pickup can change the result. For dependable body armor stock, check an Ammu-Nation.

Rifles by their in-game names

  • Marabunta Grande KZ-59. Find the Nova Auto Junkyard operation in Hamlet. Look for the gold rifle around the worktable at the covered area and check its pickup name. A gold vehicle in the same location is a separate pickup. Scan its Tracker field before trying to keep it.
  • Kingfishers W-22. Look for the Fishing Shacks operation in Wetman Bay after the early Kingfishers jobs. Confirm that the pickup says Kingfishers W-22 rather than a regular small-game rifle.
  • Free SM-69 at the Key Lento Badger tower. Find the fenced site beside Nifty Threads Islandwear. Cross the gate, climb the exterior stairs and ladders, then take the door by the control panels out onto the balcony. Look against the railing for the SM-69 Sniper pickup. Thelma is inside the control room. A separate named Killtelligence SM-69 is a reward from Bret’s Veteran Hunted.
  • Gun Nut SMS-01. A launch clip checks an upstairs room near Jason’s home. That pickup is not established across saves. If the display or floor spot is empty, check the name in your collection instead of treating another SMS-01 as the same variant.
  • FF-SR. The photographed weapon index places Internal Affairs FF-SR at a helipad rooftop on Northside Avenue, Washington Beach. An early source points to a fenced industrial location for an FF-SR without corroborating that variant name. Match the pickup label before marking it complete.
  • Kingfishers Laird 350 at Manatee Motel. Find the motel near Pelican Valley, south of Hamlet. Check the gun case in the room next to the cages. Confirm the complete variant name in the pickup text. The separate Grass Rivers roadside pickup in the 3DM clip still needs its own label and event trigger checked.

Revolvers and the Type 9

  • Conspiracist Type 9. Check the Balsero mobile-home hatch southwest of Griffin Shooting Range for the underground armory. Compare the item name with the gun-store Type 9 variants.
  • H&L .38 variants. The Port Vice City Cargo H&L .38 is listed in a yellow SCOOP container. The early glider clip is a separate reported pickup. Use the game label to distinguish these from the regular revolver.
  • Donnie’s H&L .38. Make a manual save before starting Clear Skies. The source clip places the reported drop at the airfield after Jason frees Donnie. Check the pickup beside him if Donnie is down and the game still accepts input. Look for his name in the pickup label. Forcing the encounter can fail the mission, and repeatability across saves remains unconfirmed. See the original in-game pickup clip.

Hamlet’s reported sawed-off pickup

The recording’s starting landmark is a vehicle playing music beside a warehouse in Hamlet. Follow the location footage and use the name shown on the pickup. Store-owned shotguns are a fallback when the event does not appear.

Get a launcher through a shop

Look for a gun store whose map tooltip says Expanded, then inspect the launcher rack. The imported route near the airport shows a separate reported pickup. Verify the label on the item before counting it as a named launcher.
