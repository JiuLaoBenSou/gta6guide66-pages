Early weapons and named variants

Match the pickup label to the weapon you are looking for. The same weapon family can have several named variants in different locations.

Set up a loadout you can recover

Finish Gear Up and use the personal car’s trunk and a store weapon locker once the relevant prompts appear. The 3DM free gear clip checks a parked patrol car’s trunk for a pickup. A nearby in-game officer, a missing interaction or a temporary pickup can change the result. For dependable body armor stock, check an Ammu-Nation.

Rifles by their in-game names

  • Marabunta Grande KZ-59. Find the Nova Auto Junkyard operation in Hamlet. Look for the gold rifle around the worktable at the covered area and check its pickup name. A gold vehicle in the same location is a separate pickup. Scan its Tracker field before trying to keep it.
  • Kingfishers W-22. Look for the Fishing Shacks operation in Wetman Bay after the early Kingfishers jobs. Confirm that the pickup says Kingfishers W-22 rather than a regular small-game rifle.
  • Free SM-69 at the Key Lento Badger tower. Find the fenced site beside Nifty Threads Islandwear. Cross the gate, climb the exterior stairs and ladders, then take the door by the control panels out onto the balcony. Look against the railing for the SM-69 Sniper pickup. Thelma is inside the control room. A separate named Killtelligence SM-69 is a reward from Bret’s Veteran Hunted.
  • Gun Nut SMS-01 lead near Jason’s house. Use the waypoint to find the two-story house in Key Lento. Go upstairs, pass the white couches and check the rifle case on the kitchen counter. The creator reports an intermittent pickup, so use a separate save and verify the exact variant on your own pickup screen.
  • FF-SR at Watson Bay. Find the fenced industrial yard on the shoreline peninsula across the channel from the main waterfront buildings. Take the gap in the chain-link fence and check the stacked pallets and wooden crates near the yellow wall for the FF-SR Sniper prompt. The named Internal Affairs FF-SR has a separate pickup on a Washington Beach rooftop.
  • Kingfishers Laird 350 at Manatee Motel. Find the motel near Pelican Valley, south of Hamlet. Check the gun case in the room next to the cages. Confirm the complete variant name in the pickup text.
  • Hunter’s Laird 350 from the roadside encounter. During Chapter 1 free roam, check the junction at the Grassrivers edge south of Hamlet. If you hear shouting near the Pelican Valley Recreation Area sign, follow it into the brush. Offer help to the wounded hunter, get into the pickup when the objective appears and drive to the hospital. Check the rifle prompt after the drop-off. The encounter has appeared without a map marker and may be absent on a given visit.

Hidden rifles and machine guns

Clunker 556 · Brooks Boatworks

Go to Brooks Boatworks on the north Port Gellhorn waterfront. Cross to the small two-story building on the north side of the yard. Look inside the upstairs office for an open gun case.

Cockroach Xtreme 556 · Sunrise Motel

In Tequesta, go into the Sunrise Motel courtyard. Use the south opening of the single-car garage and check the crates behind the stacked toilet paper.

Cold War 556 · Big Pine bunker

In Big Pine, North Mariana County, find the bunker and approach its entrance from the southwest. If it is already open, check the back-left corner just inside. The rifle leans against a wooden shipping crate. The conditions for opening the bunker are still unclear.

Drug Mule KZ-59 · north of Riquezas Keys

Take a boat off the north side of Riquezas Keys, northwest of Mile Zero Air Station. Watch for floating packages. Dive below them and sweep under the sunken speedboat for the rifle.

Black Eagle Import M306 TMR · Balsero warehouse

  1. Switch to Lucia. Use the shared warehouse map to find the building beside the Black Eagle gun store. Enter through the garage and go behind the cargo truck to the barred doorway.
  2. Just inside the bars, turn left and squeeze through the space between the cardboard boxes and the chain-link wall. Check the open case on the other side. The route through this gap requires Lucia.

Redneck W-22 · Moreau railcar

Find the abandoned yellow railcar on the north bank of the river in Moreau. Climb through the opening on its south side and look on the floor toward the far end.

Twin’s Laird 350 · Kalaga Reservoir woods

Head into the woods east of Kalaga Reservoir. Look for the raised hunting platform and search the leaf litter directly below it near the hunter’s accident scene.

Cruel Huntsman’s SM-69 · Pelican Valley marsh

Find the pair of hunter’s cabins in the Pelican Valley marsh in Marion County. Step onto the connecting boardwalk and check the planks by the alligator carcasses.

Three rooftop sniper rifles in Vice Beach

Each rooftop pickup has a related mission prerequisite. The exact mission names are still unclear. If the marked rooftop is empty, come back after advancing the story.

  • IAA SMS-01. Go to Aichi Condominiums on Northside Avenue in Washington Beach. Search the highest roof section beside the triangular structures.
  • FIB SM-69. Find the high-rise between the two hospitals in Vice Beach. Check the top of the building near the edge of the rooftop.
  • Internal Affairs FF-SR. Find the tall building with the helipad on Northside Avenue in Washington Beach. From the pad, sweep the northwest edge and look by the railing and vents.

Mercenary LMG 6 · Port Vice City container

Approach the north cargo area of Port Vice City from the water. Climb the waterside ladder, use the blue container to get up to the beige one and look for the yellow SCOOP container. Open it to find the rifle case.

Revolvers and the Type 9

  • Conspiracist Type 9. Check the Balsero mobile-home hatch southwest of Griffin Shooting Range for the underground armory. Compare the item name with the gun-store Type 9 variants.
  • Turnt Nipper .38 at the glider. Look for a paramotor caught in trees on the shore at the north end of Mariana County. In the recorded run, shooting the suspended glider brings it down. Approach the seat and check for the Turnt Nipper .38 pickup. The separate Port Vice City Cargo H&L .38 is listed in a yellow SCOOP container.
  • Donnie’s H&L .38. Make a manual save before starting Clear Skies. The source clip places the reported drop at the airfield after Jason frees Donnie. Check the pickup beside him if Donnie is down and the game still accepts input. Look for his name in the pickup label. Forcing the encounter can fail the mission, and repeatability across saves remains unconfirmed.

Where to pick up hidden pistols

Deadringer 1992 and Money Bag 1992 · Moreau River

  1. In East Lummox County, follow the road along the south bank of the Moreau River. Look for a cream-and-red mobile home in a gravel clearing on the river side of the road.
  2. Once inside, look down by the seated man’s shoes for the light-colored Deadringer 1992.
  3. Check the sofa on your right before leaving. The darker Money Bag 1992 rests on the seat cushion.

Martha’s 1992 · Outlaw’s Cabin

  1. Find the abandoned cabin in Moreau. Use the stairs on the southeast side to enter the cellar, then climb the stairs inside to reach the main room.
  2. Search the fireplace for Martha’s 1992. The variant has a worn wooden grip carved with M&L.

Stoner K17 MCD · Big Day Motel

  1. Take US-35 north of Ambrosia and look for the Big Day Motel. Find room 115.
  2. Inside room 115, check the nightstand to the right of the bed for the Stoner K17 MCD.

The Patriarch DRX · Kalaga Reservoir

  1. Start on the southwest bank of Kalaga Reservoir. Look for scattered luggage at the waterline, then swim past the algae and dive by the submerged car.
  2. Search the bottom beside the vehicle for The Patriarch DRX.

Assassin Nipper .38 · Permit Keys wreck

Go to the southeast edge of Permit Keys, east of Mile Zero Air Station. Find the wrecked boat and inspect the small hatch in its floor. The Assassin Nipper .38 is part of the spy boat puzzle.

Black Eagle Import Kalinga · Balsero warehouse

  1. Find the warehouse beside the Black Eagle gun store in Balsero. Inside, look for the enclosed office in the front corner. Its entrance requires a separate keypad code.
  2. Once you can enter the office, check the notes on the desktop’s sign-in screen for the safe clue. The Black Eagle Import Kalinga is inside the office safe.

Find the Marabunta Grande Hobo shotgun in Hamlet

Check the storage buildings on the east side of town beside the highway. In the filmed encounter, a parked vehicle is playing music and voices come from inside.

Go through the right-side warehouse entrance and check the open weapon case in the room with chairs and cash. The game shows Marabunta Grande Hobo Shotgun at the case.

If the encounter does not appear, look for a regular shotgun at a gun store.

Hidden shotguns

Smuggler Gorse 425 · Permit Keys wreck

Go east from Mile Zero Air Station to the small island marked below. Follow its shore to the beached smuggler’s boat and check inside the hull.

Dirty Drifter · Bonanza mudslide

Along the coastal road in Bonanza, look for a blue car partially buried uphill in a mudslide. Climb the bank and open its trunk.

Unpacked Comshot 12 · Key Lento bathroom

Find the house on Key Lento’s northwest coast. Take the exterior stairs to the second floor. Inside, turn right down the hallway, take the back bedroom on the left and go into its ensuite bathroom. The shotgun is behind the bathroom door.

Hellspawn Comshot 12 · Circles of Hell encounter

  1. Take the mountain side road in western North Kelly County to the campsite. Look for the carved wooden throne and check whether the game gives you an interaction at the item on the seat. The conditions that make the prompt appear are still unclear.
  2. Follow the encounter path to the boss. Watch the four blue-green torches: each defeated enemy puts one out. After the fourth, attack while the boss’s health bar is red. Repeat this cycle as the boss becomes invulnerable again. Keep your distance from exploding demons.
  3. After clearing the fight, return to the throne and check for the Hellspawn Comshot 12 leaning against it. The encounter also awards the Circles of Hell shirt.

Find the free Hauners H-AT rocket launcher in Balsero

  1. Finish the Chapter 1 gun-store objective and verify that you can store weapons in Jason’s vehicle. The recorded route starts after this unlock.
  2. On the map, look north of Vice City International Airport, in Balsero. Go southwest of Griffin Shooting Range and use the impound lot in the footage as a landmark. Look for a weathered mobile home with a cluster of antennas on its roof.
  3. Enter the mobile home and open its floor hatch. In the room below, look for the Hauners H-AT propped beside the safe. Check the case next to the red tool chest for the Conspiracist Type 9. Confirm both names at their pickup prompts.

To buy a launcher, find an Expanded gun store and check its current stock and price.
