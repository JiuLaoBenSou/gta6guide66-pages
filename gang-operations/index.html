GTA 6 gang operation locations

Find the 39 operations by gang. Open a location for its in-game map and directions to the safe or other key valuables.

How to clear an Illegal Operation

  1. Open Map → Robberies → Illegal Operations. A yellow Robbery Opportunity can show its full name after you approach. Open the card and check the number of key valuables.
  2. Collect every item the game counts as a key valuable. Check the side rooms for gold pickup icons, and finish taking the cash after opening a safe. Some codes vary between runs. Use the safe combination guide if your keypad rejects a reported code.
  3. Check Progress → Gangs for credit before heading to the next site. Cleared sites can become available again after time passes. Return if you left a key item behind.

Pest Control: Clear one operation in each of the nine progress groups, including Other Criminals. For Story Mode 100%, clear all 39. The Ultimate Edition shop is a separate bonus.

Kingfishers · 3

  • Fishing Shacks

    Watson Bay. Go to the fishing compound on the waterfront and look for the Kingfishers W-22 rifle. W-22 pickup

  • Sunrise RV Park

    Pelican Valley. Use the screwdriver prompt on the safe behind the main office counter. The note beside it gives 6769 for the second safe by the Live Bait & Tackle sign in the dock building. For the Kingfishers Akulus, check the bed in the orange-and-white RV north of the dock.

  • Manatee Motel

    Grassrivers. Search the motel rooms for the safe and the Kingfishers Laird 350 rifle.

Kolt & Associates · 5

  • Kingpin Bowling

    Port Gellhorn, off US-9. The watch is on a round table in the southeast office area. The Gutterball Moreland 850 is on the northeast pool table. Check behind the northeast Storage door for the safe. The Brooks Boatworks clue gives 0300 in recorded runs. Watch room pictures

  • June Warehouse

    Port Gellhorn. Match the June Warehouse marker. Check for the safe, the Kolt & Associates Vatra and the Truth & Justice Caracara on the operation card.

  • La Cascara Motel

    Port Gellhorn beach. Use the stairs at the north end to reach the secured room on the northwest side of the top floor. Look for 6969 in the notes from Brooks Boatworks for this safe. The map also lists the Scrapper VF5C and Firebug Dominator.

  • Brooks Boatworks

    North Port Gellhorn, off Gellhorn Avenue. For the warehouse safe, enter the southeast shutters and climb the stairs on the left. Check the wall map for 6823. For the separate office safe, use the outside stairs on the fishery’s northeast side and enter via the open upper window.

  • Stash House

    Port Gellhorn, Gellhorn Avenue. Facing the house, take the door on the right side. Pass the fridge and look right just beyond the kitchen doorway.

M7 · 2

  • M7 Compound

    Hamlet. The Nova Auto Junkyard marker identifies the compound. Find the garage safe in the main building and check the operation card for the Marabunta Grande KZ-59 and Teniente Manana. Recorded runs opened this safe with 7777. Junkyard pickup route

  • Weed Grow

    East Lummox County near Tallaloko. Search the RV on the compound’s northwest side for the first safe. The second is in the large warehouse office by its southwest entrance.

La Polilla Negra · 2

  • Palmer Fishing Warehouse

    South Bobcat Key. Cross to the Palmer Fisheries building and check the upstairs office corner for the safe. The key valuables also include an Elite watch and the gang’s KZ-59. Watch pickup notes

  • Watson Bay Safehouse

    Watson Bay. Look upstairs in the kitchen for the safe. Check the desk by the monitors for the Ark Royal Quantum watch. Find the blue-tarp entrance, code and weapon pickup.

Final Chapter · 4

  • Farm Fix Meth Lab

    Rural Belville, off Chastity Avenue. The safe is at the back of the upstairs southeast office. A route from the north-side AC unit leads to the skylight above that office. See the safe clue and the two standout pickups

  • Biker Hideout

    The Thirsty Wolf on SR-27 in north Belville. Find the restricted northwest room. The safe is immediately to the right when you enter through the rear door.

  • Hog Stars Garage

    Acacia Street, Ambrosia. Find the back office beneath the Live and Ride Another Day banner. The southwest ladder and open skylight provide another way inside.

  • Irons Building Supply

    Ambrosia. Go upstairs, past the exercise equipment on the balcony, to reach the office safe.

San4San · 5

  • Sound4Sound Studio

    Stockyard. Enter from the east to reach the safe in the recording booth’s control room. The operation also lists the San4San K17 and Bourbon Windsor.

  • La Perle Trap House

    La Perle. Check the operation card for the Charred Toros and Chromium W-10. Use the marker to identify the correct property.

  • Illegal Sneaker Trade

    Drip Drops on Del Rio Boulevard in La Perle. The safe is behind the staff-only door. The alley on the east side leads to the back entrance.

  • JanJak’s Garage

    La Perle, just north of Del Rio Boulevard. Head in through the north garage entrance to the office safe. The west-side ladder is another way to reach the office.

  • Parlor Apartments

    Northbrook Drive, Rockridge. Find the first-floor apartment and check its rear living area. From the west garage, climb through the opening and turn left toward the apartment door.

PTT Youngin$ · 2

  • Southside Trap House

    Southside. Look for the Ark Royal Ashore watch in the map card. Keep this separate from the La Perle Trap House and the Saber watch with the same name.

  • Kamar Devon Community Center

    Southside. The operation card lists the Novak Laurent Argonaut watch and PTT Youngin$ K17.

The Guardia Brothers · 3

  • Saber Shipping Yard

    Northbrook Drive, Little Cuba. The back office is on the west side of the north building. The office safe marker may say Saber Shipping Facility even though this location is the Yard.

  • Casa Guardia

    Little Cuba. Approach the abandoned building from the NW 3rd Avenue side or use the west ladder. Check the southwest corner of the upstairs balcony for the safe. The operation also lists a Crowex Ambassador watch.

  • Saber Shipping Facility

    Westbrook Drive, Balsero. The roof door leads to the upstairs office safe. Check the map card for the Ark Royal Ashore and Guardia Brothers VF5.

Other Criminals · 13

  • Exotic Animals Front

    Key Lento. Check behind Three Sheets Liquor off Sunbelt Highway. The safe is in the rear storage room. The parrot also leads to a separate stash. Parrot and cash route

  • Mill Station Radar Base

    Grassrivers. Enter from the underpass and turn left into the side rooms. Look next to the desk in the improvised storeroom.

  • Industrial Weed Grow

    North Mariana County. In the warehouse, head to the raised office at its far northwest end. The card also lists the Cultivator Sandking and Chronic Vatra.

  • Slator Towing Inc

    Salton, between SW 7th and SW 8th avenues. Check the office safe. The low wall beside the SW 8th Avenue gate gives another way into the lot.

  • Leonida Carrier Warehouse

    Balsero. Enter at the west garage door and pass the office into the back room. The safe is in the corner. The operation also lists the Midas watch.

  • Cigar Import Front

    Palmis Liquor, Del Rio Boulevard in La Perle. Go through the staff-only door behind the counter to the safe.

  • Chatfield Storage & Logistics

    Waxman Island, Vice Beach. Go to the second floor of the site office for the safe. The card also lists the Purple Drank Emerus and Quarter Mile ES9.

  • Ocean Beach Lab

    Gabriel Wexler salon near Atlantic Avenue. The west-side alley leads to the lab. The back office with the safe is on the right.

  • Vespucci Mansion

    Neptune Circle, Vespucci Island. Both armory safes share an upstairs room. Look for the west-facing glass doors above the pool and canal.

  • Gas Station Meth Front

    The 247 Gas off County Road 62 in Emerald Springs. The safe is in the back room beyond the staff-only door.

  • Walker Farm

    Off SR-32 in Frogswater. Look by the computers just inside the main north entrance under the awning.

  • Backroom Weed Front

    Pearl House Hair Salon in south Port Gellhorn. Check the rear room, which you can approach from the west side.

  • Carter Mining Boathouse

    Near Bonanza in Lummox County. Take the main southeast entrance and look immediately to the left for the locked safe.

Farm Fix Meth Lab safe, vehicle and Kyle’s final sighting

  1. After Buddy? (Check on Kyle), return to Farm Fix Meth Lab north of Belville. You can go without another text. If the gate is damaged, enter the large warehouse on the left and use Focus to locate Kyle upstairs. Approach him to see his last scene.
  2. From the warehouse floor, use the white double doors below the left end of the black banner. Continue through the next doors and turn left before the exit to enter the room with blue shelves. Read the paper at the desk in the back corner. The documented code is 2121.
  3. Return to the main room and take the stairs on the right side of the banner. Continue into the upstairs office and find the safe in the back-left corner. Enter the code and collect the contents.
  4. Leave the office and turn left onto the walkway. Check the counter on the right by the fan for the Throttler shotgun. Go back downstairs for the Western Searing Zombie Robber, the black motorcycle with yellow flames. Check both pickup names and the operation progress before leaving.

Ultimate Edition bonus

  • Illegal Goods Store

    Krystal Inc. Electronics, off SW 13th Street in Southside. The office door on the southeast side is locked from the outside. Use the stacked containers to reach the roof and drop in through the southern opening.
