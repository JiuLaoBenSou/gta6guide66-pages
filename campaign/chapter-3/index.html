Welcome to Vice City

  1. At Jack of Hearts, speak to security at the front entrance first. Wait for Go to the back before walking around the exterior to look for Dre’Quan.
  2. Park in the covered bay at Bayside. Get out and use the front lobby. Apartment 201 is upstairs.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_32
  • Aquish GTA · the front entrance prompt

Dealer’s Choice

  1. As Jason, accept Go See Dre’Quan and head to the Only Raw studio upstairs at Jack of Hearts. Follow the new marker to Tyrone and inspect the two options on the table.
  2. At the delivery stop, park, take the bag and follow Dre’Quan on foot. The handoff prompt appears by the waiting group. Take the payment bag only after the inspection.
Choices and consequences

Pure advances to the check and payment. Cut prompts the buyer to question the contents. The final net payout has not been compared in matching saves.

Caught Slipping

  1. When Bayside’s marker changes, return to the apartment. Pursue the marked character when Catch the thief appears and complete the bag searches for Jason and Lucia.
  2. At Big House Storage, walk toward the rear corner of the parking lot with the yellow vans. Take the elevator to the corridor with the blue doors. Match the marker to A726 on the seventh floor in the documented route.

Dubious Provenance

  1. Back at Bayside, inspect the closet bag. Track Visit the Antique Store to Capri and find the dark-blue awning of Evelyn Andrews Antiques. Try the front door, go upstairs and then speak to Halifax by the outside steps.
  2. On the return visit, use the staircase behind the store and step onto the lower roof. The open window is above the external air conditioner. Enter as Lucia and use Let Jason in on the marked door.
  3. Check the drawer of the desk in the northern corner for the address. At the motel next door, take the west staircase and find room 110.
Choices and consequences

Handing over the statue opens the appraisal thread. If you kept it, look for Call Halifax about the Statue before Chapter 3 ends. In room 110, Trust Halifax opens the later lead. Wait for Evelyn’s next call, return to the shop for the address and follow the marker to Maryam’s house northwest of Capri. During the pursuit, bring the watercraft alongside the marked boat and wait for the game’s boarding prompt.

Mami Knows Best

  1. Accept Catch Up With Mia as Lucia. Take Mia’s blue hatchback to the house and check the far end of the hallway.
  2. Take the marked debris to the blue dumpster out front. At the pool, hold both triggers and press Cross/A when the mattress interaction appears.
  3. Follow the orange excavator prompt across the street, then return to the hatchback and take Mia back to her office.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_36
  • Aquish GTA · the pool mattress prompt

Collect Chemicals for Kyle

  1. Finish Check Out McKittrick Farm and wait for Kyle’s text. Track the Leonard County objective and talk to the woman at the gate.
  2. Walk to the white van at the far end and inspect the marked cargo. Check the available dialogue options and follow the resulting marker.
  3. Return the van to Kyle’s barn. If the game gives a pursuit objective, finish it before driving to the handoff.

Locate Kyle’s crop-duster introduction

Veteran Hunted

  1. After Coffee Break, play as Jason and look for Bret Vogel’s text. Visit the Lummox County marker without Lucia.
  2. Use Focus on the tracks and study the deer with the scope or binoculars when prompted. Complete Bret’s hunting tutorial to unlock the Killtelligence SM-69.
  3. Put the game’s marked carcass in Bret’s truck, return to the cabin and follow the processing-area marker.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_38
  • PowerPyx · solo requirement and tutorial

Meet the Heders

  1. Track Meet Brian & Lori at Effluvia. Ride the elevator up and look for the bar on the roof.
  2. When the game switches to Lucia, inspect the white PORT VICECITY van. At the docks, look up at the upper containers in the center stack for the orange SCOOP marker.
  3. Follow the cutting meter and take the marked vehicle to the warehouse after the route updates.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_39
  • Aquish GTA · container cutting indicator

Car Service

  1. As Lucia, choose Steal the Car for Raul. At Slator Towing Inc. in Salton, recognize the white van behind the metal entrance and find the Schafter V12 at the covered space.
  2. Deliver the car to Leonida Auto World in Balsero. Drive in by the blue lights and continue inside to Randy.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_40
  • Sirloin · towing yard approach

Boobie Time

  1. As Jason, select Talk to Boobie at Jack of Hearts. Drive his red car, pull up by the apartment staircase and use the horn prompt.
  2. At the waterfront, follow the bag marker on the Seashark. Pick up the bag and wait for Return to Boobie before turning back.
  3. Follow the next route to Tropik Haze and finish the introduction to Yasmani.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_41
  • Sirloin · the water pickup

Here, Kitty Kitty

  1. As Jason, select Speak with Tyrone. Knock at the marked apartment, give your name and finish the conversation. Check the message after you leave.
  2. At Charles’s building, go into apartment 209 and use Examine by the lit bedside table.
  3. Look for NW 11th & Del Rio in the chat and follow the route to the La Perle camp beneath the concrete structure. Use the stairs at the far end when the marker moves.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_42
  • Sirloin · examine prompt in room 209

Top Billing

  1. Select Watch the Real Dimez at Jack of Hearts. Ask where to find the dressing room and follow Roxy from backstage.
  2. When Go to Peewees appears, head to the counter and complete its interaction. At the party, use Lucia to find Roxy in a rear room.
  3. During the switch to Jason, check the new marker outside before returning to the group.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_43
  • Sirloin · the Peewees counter

First Impressions

  1. After Car Service, meet Raul through the Waxman Island objective. At the roller door, use the wall keypad on its right. The Notes menu shows 2404 in this route.
  2. Take the dark car to the meetup on the south side of Ocean Beach. After control switches back to Lucia, reverse out and follow the Rialto Islands route.
  3. When Go to the parking lot appears, switch to the marked van there before taking Raul to Bayside.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_44
  • Sirloin · 2404 at the Waxman Island keypad

Crosstown Traffic

  1. Start as Lucia at the Courtyard Bar in Stockyard with Meet Raul, or as Jason at the La Perle boatyard with Meet Karim.
  2. When Lucia misses the train, continue to the next station and enter through the rear car. Look for the passenger in the pineapple shirt.
  3. When the emergency-stop objective appears, head back through the last carriage. Follow the new route to Jason’s boat.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_45
  • Sirloin · finding the passenger

Credit Check

  1. Follow Meet Raul’s Crew to the planning room. Upstairs, select two masks at Andrea’s rack. Go to Karim’s blue-lit workstation for the next interaction.
  2. At the first Fleeca branch, start at the counter by the entrance. After each branch objective clears, exit and wait for the next marker. The HUD identifies the three stops.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_46
  • Sirloin · Choose Masks objective

Join a Street Race

  1. Play as Jason after Dealer’s Choice and check Manuel’s group message. The optional race marker is in Salton.
  2. Choose a rental, complete the course and talk to Yago at the end. Rental costs and placement rewards appear in the menus. The tested first-place payout was $3,000.
  3. Keep an eye on Yago’s messages for Bumper to Bumper and Race Against Yago. The later race can expire.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_47
  • PowerPyx · unlock, rentals and follow-up list

Ride Or Die

  1. After Credit Check, wait for Dre’Quan’s message and head to Jack of Hearts as Jason alone. Follow him to Eris.
  2. Look upstairs near the DJ for the man with the bag of DWNPLY sneakers. Get the shoes before following Dre’Quan into the VIP lounge.
  3. After the lounge interaction, go to the marked parking area and identify DWNPLY’s car. Follow the Speedshop marker and then the escape objective.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_49
  • PowerPyx · shoes, lobby and car objectives

Climbing the Cellerichain

  1. As Lucia, check Andy’s text for Visit Andy’s New House. Take the Essentials Kit from her kitchen. At the Waning Sands address, use the side path to the white canopy and find the marked delivery table.
  2. On Andy’s next call, take a photo of her on the green sofa. Carry the new package to the Cellerica stall under the white tents at Creative Market in Decor Plaza.
  3. For the third delivery, check Andy’s next message and pick up the parcel in her garage. In Ambrosia, hold on to it when the person at the front door asks for it. Wait for the garage to open, then use the delivery prompt.

Pay the Cellerica Fee

  1. Complete all three Andy deliveries and stay on Lucia in free roam while you wait for Andy to call. After the call, look for Pay the Cellerica Fee in Map → Story.
  2. Open BuckMe on Lucia’s phone and send Andy the $5,000 initiation payment. The objective can be completed from wherever you are.
  3. Keep playing as Lucia until Andy texts again. The next marker is in Belville.

Next Andy objective: Peach!

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_50

Peach! (Join the Cellerica Initiation)

  1. After Andy’s text, track Join the Cellerica Initiation in Belville as Lucia. Interact with the bench at the marker to meet Andy.
  2. Follow Andy until you reach the room with mats. Use the white side door and interact with the box of clothes in the changing room.
  3. Finish the initiation, take the golf cart to the mansion, and sit on the lobby sofa to start the meeting. Leave the mansion and speak to Andy outside.
Choices and consequences

The initiation’s optional interactions change the dialogue. Either approach leads to the mansion and leaves the next job available.

Next Andy objective: Read the Label

References

Read the Label (Retrieve the Cellerica Van)

  1. Finish Peach! and continue free roam as Lucia until Cecilia gets in touch. Track Retrieve the Cellerica Van in northern Vice City.
  2. At the marker, clear the encounter with the two people beside the van and get in. Drive to the Belville warehouse and stop in the yellow parking bay to end the delivery.
  3. Check Lucia’s BuckMe for Cecilia’s $1,800 payment. Stay on Lucia while waiting for the next warehouse message.

Next Andy objective: Puff Puff Pass

References

Puff Puff Pass (Meet at the Warehouse)

  1. As Lucia, wait for Cecilia’s follow-up after Read the Label. Track Meet at the Warehouse to Belville and stay close to Felix as he guides you to the upstairs room.
  2. When Escape the warehouse appears on the HUD, complete the encounter and follow the mansion marker that appears after the exit.
  3. Inside the mansion, take the right-hand door from the lobby. Go upstairs and open the last door along the hallway. Finish the marked encounter to close the questline.
Choices and consequences

The finale awards Road to Rehabilitation. When Escape the warehouse appears, keep Andy alive until the exit opens.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_50
  • PowerPyx · warehouse escape, mansion route and trophy

Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS

  1. Track Steal the Zorrusso for Randy to Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach. When Randy calls, set up Key Cloner in the in-game phone settings.
  2. At ground level, scan the red Granforma Zorrusso and verify Access: Key Cloner. Use the driver’s door and rotate the right stick until the circle fills.
  3. Deliver the car to Randy’s garage and check the Wish List on the display board.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_51
  • MKIceAndFire · Key Cloner and the target car

Sinfrontera National Bank

  1. Select Scope Out the Bank in the heist prep list and go inside the building marked SNB. Sit by the low tables in the pale-blue lobby.
  2. When camera movement becomes available, use Study on each marked feature. Go upstairs once the prompt changes and find the waiting area by the light-blue rail and SNB wall sign.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_53
  • MKIceAndFire · lobby and study objective

Security Expert

  1. Select Find Nicole in the prep list. At Waning Sands, go around to the back and look for the lit entrance beside the pool.
  2. Taking control of Lucia, look for the bright computer screen and beige chair inside. Finish the room sequence, regroup with Jason and return to the planning room.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_53
  • MKIceAndFire · pool-side entrance

Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs

  1. After Boobie Time, check Yasmani’s new markers. Track the store you want and compare the keypad’s in-game clue to the destination.
  2. PowerPyx recorded 1680 for the Casa de Empeno back office, 6815 in the northeast corner of the Richardson central room and 0104 in the rear hallway of Donovan Banks & Co.
  3. After taking the marked piece, bring it to Yasmani at Tropik Haze. The first delivery can make the Snap Gun available for the remaining jobs. Finish Sell Jewelry to Yasmani to clear the list.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_54
  • PowerPyx · safes and first-delivery reward

Company Property

  1. Select Meet Brian at Skeeter’s and follow him to the airstrip. Wait for the plane’s side door to open before moving the marked crates to the truck bed.
  2. After the character switch, watch for Land the plane and approach through the open field. Follow the new route to Felipe’s garage.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_55
  • Sirloin · crates and truck bed

Mall Rats

  1. Finish Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, then wait for Billy’s next message. Track Join Billy on a Job around Ocean Beach and travel without a partner for Mall Rats.
  2. Follow the marked garage route through the mall. In the jewelry store take the game’s safe interaction as it appears. If Billy obtains a code, read the value shown in your own run before using the keypad.

Start Billy’s jobs at the Mud Club

References

The Impaler

  1. After Mall Rats, wait for Billy’s call and reply to Wyman’s phone message. The car objective appears at the Ambrosia impound.
  2. At the lot, look for the black-and-white Impaler at the back. Follow the marked exit and clear the in-game wanted level.
  3. Use the new drop-off marker after the pursuit ends. Park in the tunnel without damaging the car and leave the delivery area.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_56
  • PowerPyx · call trigger and drop-off

Hometown Heroes

  1. After The Impaler, wait for Billy’s message, then check the Thrillbilly Mud Club marker in Kelly County.
  2. Complete the raceway objectives until you switch to Lucia. At the office, use the open safe, then follow Billy’s route on the dirt bike.
  3. When you switch back to Jason, head to the sugar factory and look for Lucia upstairs. Follow the escape markers to finish the chain and check for The Lone Rider.
