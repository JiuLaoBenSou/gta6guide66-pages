Chapter 5 walkthrough

Bird in the Hand

  1. Follow Vinny to the rooftop helicopter. Use the binoculars when prompted and sweep the Coast Guard docks, following each highlighted object until the objective updates.
  2. At the base, wait until Go to the hangar appears. Find the ladder on the outside wall, go up to the roof and follow the boarding marker.

References

  • Shirrako · exterior hangar ladder

Taking Liberties

  1. Finish Bird in the Hand and return to Giorgio’s penthouse in Tequesta Retreat. Taking Liberties starts there and Meet with Giorgio appears automatically.
  2. Go to the spa next to the gym. Once Giorgio leaves the spa, follow him upstairs to his office and let the conversation finish.
  3. Back in the penthouse, look for Vinny by the pool or Mattia near the lounge. Doggie Bag and VC Baby can both appear at this point.

References

Doggie Bag

  1. Select Meet Vinny and follow the cruise dock marker. Once on board, take the exterior deck with the red running track.
  2. After picking up Rocco, take the stairs toward the marked helicopter pickup. If you loop back around the pool, return to the red track and check the pink marker.

References

  • Sirloin · red running track
  • Shirrako · stairs while carrying Rocco

VC Baby (Talk to Mattia)

  1. Track Talk to Mattia at the penthouse. Follow him from the lounge down the hallway with red lights to the glowing SKY GARAGE doors.
  2. When the choice appears, press Cross on PS5 to help Mattia or Square to refuse. The two recorded routes both return to Meet up with Vinny. Choose Help to play the extra trip.
  3. On the Help route, follow Lucia into the lounge. Wait near the pink sculpture until she leads Dardan out. Once Steal the key fob appears, check the window end of the curved beige sofa and use Triangle when KEY FOB appears.
  4. Follow Go back to Mattia past the red-leaf tree, then use the garage exit. At St. Vitus Medical Center, follow Mattia past the teal Maternity Care wall to the Labor & Delivery doors.

If you’re stuck If the sofa has no prompt, wait for Steal the key fob to appear before approaching it. The footage confirms the immediate next mission on both routes. Later effects remain unclear.

References

  • Sirloin · Help or Refuse

Operation Savage Glory

  1. Track Meet up with Vinny and attend the briefing. On the ship, follow Rocco until Find the cargo appears, then check the newly marked containers.
  2. After the switch to the other team, complete the loading prompts. When the truck is at pickup, use the helicopter’s marked cargo interaction and fly to the drop zone.

If you’re stuck If a marker has not switched, wait for the other character to reach the container or loading point.

References

  • Sirloin · cargo area objective

Honest Abe

  1. After Operation Savage Glory, leave the penthouse at Tequesta Retreat. Look in Story for Negotiate a Deal with Abe once you are outside.
  2. Follow the marker to Abe’s office Downtown. Finish the meeting and leave the office to return to free roam.
  3. When Sandy sends the bill, open it from the Chapter 5 fee objective or in BuckMe. The later Meet with Abe marker is the start of Protective Custody.

Unfinished Business

  1. After Honest Abe, check Giorgio’s messages and Map → Story. Select the pink Giorgio entry to read Unfinished Business.
  2. In the recorded route, the card appears after the $150,000 receipt and says you can finish outstanding tasks or continue the agreement. For the latter, use the separate Meet With Abe card.

If you’re stuck The recorded card gives no separate destination. No universal condition that removes the card has been verified.

References

  • Sirloin · Giorgio’s Story card

Pay Abe’s Fee — Chapter 5

  1. After speaking to Abe and progressing the next Story prompts, read Sandy’s message from Lucretia Law Group. The Chapter 5 version in the recording requests $150,000.
  2. Open the request in BuckMe and confirm the amount displayed by your game. When the receipt appears, verify the recipient and Mr. Gonzalez’s Fee before looking for Meet with Abe.

Protective Custody

  1. After handling the fee, use Meet with Abe. Read the signing prompt and make a separate save before proceeding if you want to revisit this point.
  2. At the waterfront, stop near the lit sculpture at Wait for the contact to arrive. Follow the updated markers when control changes to Lucia.
  3. Follow the guide to the armory as Jason. When Clear a path to the warehouse appears, follow the warehouse marker beyond the police car.

References

  • Sirloin · signing prompt
  • Sirloin · warehouse objective

The End of the Road

  1. Leave a manual save before meeting Cal. At the garage, take the marked equipment after the briefing and follow the team to the helicopter.
  2. At Use the computer in the tower, check the desk by the window. Wait for Download Complete before following the exit marker.
  3. At the construction site, take the cage lift to the bottom and follow the Flemming Key route. Pause to read the game’s choices at the bridge.
Choices and consequences

MELOO’s and Sirloin’s saves show different consequences after Swim for it. In MELOO’s recording, Lucia reaches the water and Jason is taken into custody. Sirloin records both characters being shot during a water escape. A specific in-game threshold for those outcomes remains unverified.
