Ocean Beach

  1. After The Cartel Heist, return to the Bayside apartment and start packing with Lucia. Drive to the Y Hotel and check in with the concierge.
  2. Find Valentina on the right side of the lobby. Take the elevator to floor 7, turn left at the end of the hall and enter room 701.
  3. Check the wardrobe when prompted. Pick up both champagne glasses from the counter and bring one to Lucia on the balcony.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Another Life

  1. Pick up the phone from the table, then go to the crib at the end of the hallway for Tomás.
  2. Take Tomás into the living room and use the marked interactions to settle him. Turn up the radio at the counter and finish the dance prompts.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Pool Day

  1. As Jason on the balcony after the flashback, agree to go to the pool. Walk past the right side of the pool to the bar and order a Tequila Sunrise.
  2. Talk to Eddie at the bar. His party can become available later regardless of your reply. Bring Lucia her drink and follow her down to the beach.
  3. Finish the beach conversation. If you are still in the viewing prompt, check the flamingo float, the group on the left and the woman in the large hat to the right. Watch for Valentina’s call afterward.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Company Cars

  1. After Pool Day, track Speak to the Concierge and talk to Santino in the Y Hotel lobby. Choose to see the cars now and take the hotel ride to Status Auto Imports.
  2. Find Rob near the red car and follow him to the selection. You can scan the parked cars with WAiNK to compare them. Get into the driver’s seat of your pick and confirm it, then repeat when control switches to Lucia.
  3. Complete the race back to the hotel to finish the handover.

If you’re stuck Try speaking to Santino in the lobby if the Story card still shows a lock after Pool Day.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Lawyer Up

  1. When Valentina gets in touch after Pool Day, track Meet Abe Gonzalez to the law office downtown.
  2. Check in at reception, speak to Sandy toward the back and head upstairs to Abe. Let his meeting finish to open the next calls.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Heavy Lifting

  1. After Lawyer Up, take Raul’s call. Retrieve the hard drive from the storage unit, then go to the houseboat. Take the stairs on the right to Karim and drive the boat south to the Ajax Gym dock.
  2. Start the tour as Jason. When you switch to Lucia, change at the marked locker and check the locker immediately to its left for Raina’s bag. Follow the workout station prompts.
  3. Enter the tanning area while Jason keeps the attendant busy. Find Nate in the second room on the right. Check the floor, bed and bag for the marked interactions, read his WhatUp messages and release him before leaving.
  4. Follow the car at a distance and use its direction arrow to keep track of turns. At Grand Majestic Aparthotel, take the elevator to the roof.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

It’s A Date!

  1. After Heavy Lifting, take both Jason and Lucia to Stockyard Courtyard Bar for this optional outing.
  2. Buy a drink and dance if you want to. Walk out to the street to complete the date and register the relationship gain.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Party Hard

  1. After Lawyer Up, track Work Personal Security to the airport. Follow the lead car, pick up the order in Little Cuba and continue to Megamundo downtown.
  2. Follow Petra to the elevator and conference room. After reaching the first floor during the attack, retrieve the marked radio and take the low route to the left as Lucia.
  3. Follow the character switch prompts. As Lucia, reach the party floor through the opening and vent above the stairs. Once the floor is clear, untie Valentina downstairs and check the marked door for the Navarros.
  4. As Jason, board the departing helicopter from the roof and finish the struggle prompts. When Jason takes control, land on the Megamundo rooftop helipad.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Pay Abe’s Fee — Chapter 4

  1. After Party Hard, find Pay Abe’s Fee on Map → Story. The Chapter 4 card in the recording shows $75,000. Use Open BuckMe from the objective or open the request sent by Sandy.
  2. In BuckMe, open Payment Requests and select Mr. Gonzalez’s Fee from Lucretia Law Group. Confirm the request amount using your own game.
  3. After paying, check the transaction receipt, then go back to the Story objective. The recorded receipt shows −$75,000.

Check the separate Chapter 5 request

Photo/Shoot

  1. Track Shoot a Promo in Tequesta after Party Hard. Follow Dre’Quan through the building and take the elevator to the roof.
  2. Use Lucia’s phone camera to photograph the Dimez from the helicopter. After the ambush, follow Dre’Quan’s car from the air. Switch back to Lucia when it disappears indoors.
  3. Stay on the target’s trail until other vehicles join the chase after the second ramp. At the underpass, check both the cars on the right and the garages on the left before approaching the rescue marker at the far end.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Bad Driver!

  1. After Party Hard, check for Find Felipe and meet Andrea at the bar in Millport. Go toward the restroom at the back and follow Felipe when he runs.
  2. When you catch up, use Focus to open the choice. If you want the follow-up vehicle reward, spare him and watch for Collect Felipe’s Gift.

If you’re stuck Do this optional job before Power Play if you want to see it.

Choices and consequences

Sparing Felipe opens Collect Felipe’s Gift. Killing him pays GTA$20,000 and closes that follow-up.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Attend Eddie Acosta’s Party

  1. Meet Eddie at the bar during Pool Day. After he texts, visit Heathcliff Heights with Jason or Lucia alone and take the lobby elevator to the party.
  2. Meet Eddie on the roof and decide whether to buy his GTA$50,000 NFT. If you refuse, use the motorcycle and solar-panel ramp to reach the neighboring roof, then take the elevator out.

If you’re stuck Separate the two characters before heading to the party if the marker is locked.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Making Gainz

  1. After Heavy Lifting, wait for Raul to contact Lucia and go to the planning room as Lucia alone. At Ajax Gym, talk to Nate by the upstairs benches until the workout begins.
  2. Follow the bench press, squat and outdoor deadlift prompts, then use the bike across from the pool. Finish the remaining equipment prompts and decide whether to take Nate’s offer.
  3. Check the Trauma Floor, then meet Jason on the north side and use the intercom at the planning room.

If you’re stuck If you want to do an outing with both characters, finish it before this job. After Making Gainz, they can meet in their hotel room but cannot go out together until after VCIA Takedown.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Blood Sweat and Gear

  1. After Making Gainz, return to Ajax Gym as Lucia. On the Trauma Floor, use the locker on the right and speak to Nate downstairs.
  2. Follow the stations near Raina, Nate and Francisco. Use the deadlift station onstage with Kordell, then finish the conversations and leave.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Too Much Baggage

  1. As Jason after Making Gainz, track Scope Out the Airport and meet Andrea at the office by the parking area. During Omar’s tour, scan the boxed targets until they turn green.
  2. Reverse the tug into both trailers to connect them, then drive to the plane on the southeast side. Release the bag prompt slightly before the halfway line because the meter continues briefly.
  3. Leave the loading bay and follow the wall south to the last double doors on the left. Stay left inside, take the blue office door on the right and check the restroom immediately inside. Place the device behind the toilet, return to Omar and accept through the Focus conversation.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Watch It

  1. As Jason after Making Gainz, track Meet Dre’Quan and go upstairs in his store. Drive to the top level of the Cruda garage and find the marked car.
  2. Finish the rooftop encounter and leave. At Nola, choose either mannequin outfit covered by Dre’Quan. Follow him through Doight d’Or to the rear exit.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Amateur Hour

  1. As Lucia after Blood Sweat and Gear, go to the Trauma Floor and talk to Francisco by the stage deadlift. Change at the locker and follow him through the back door to his car.
  2. Park at the marked spot on Washington Island and wait on the bench. When the Coochie Camper arrives, get back in Francisco’s car and follow it. A hit to its tires can bring the in-game chase to a stop.
  3. Let Francisco collect the money, drive him behind Ajax Gym and park in his original space to finish.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Call Nate

  1. After Amateur Hour, read Nate’s text as Lucia and call him from the phone. He can also call Lucia himself after some time.
  2. Once Hang Out With Nate appears, meet him at Muscle Beach on Ocean Beach to start Cash Injections.

References

Cash Injections

  1. As Lucia, track Hang Out With Nate to Muscle Beach. Follow him to his car and drive to the marked alley. At the trunk, follow the disarm prompt and defend Nate.
  2. Take the car left in the alley and stay behind Nate’s stolen car. The chase moves to a motel lot, where you need to clear the ground level and watch the upper walkway before recovering the car.
  3. Leave the lot, clear any wanted level and take Nate to the marked ice cream shop on the north side of Vice Beach.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Dirty Work

  1. Complete Too Much Baggage and Cash Injections, then track Deal with Raina to Andrea’s planning room. Drive to the Velvet Palm Motel in Tequesta.
  2. As Lucia, use the containers facing the motel windows. As Jason, finish the prompts until he opens the window. Switch to Lucia and take photos while Jason is visible until the scene advances.
  3. Drive around the building to regroup in the alley. The next day, go upstairs to the gym pool as Lucia and talk to Nate in the chair.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Nate Will Be in Touch

  1. After Dirty Work, continue Jason’s available Story missions while Lucia waits for Nate. Check the story list again after Child’s Play.
  2. When Help Kordell’s Crew appears for Lucia, follow it to Salton to start Power Play.

Adventures in Babysitting

  1. As Jason after Making Gainz, track Work Security for Andrés to Starfish Island. Follow Paulo to the back of the house, then drive Andrés to The Vault.
  2. Use the bar to the left on the first dance floor. Find Andrés by the stairs to give him his drink. Follow the photo and upstairs booth requests until he disappears from the DJ booth.
  3. Check the booth, then the bathroom on the far side of the floor. After talking to the dealer, return to Andrés at the southeast bar. Follow the fight and escape prompts as they move through the club.
  4. Follow Paulo up the escalator and help Andrés cross the tracks. At the helipad, move him into the helicopter. During the final defense, wait for the launcher’s red lock-on indicator before firing.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Rafa’s Kidnapping

  1. After Adventures in Babysitting, check the Story list as Jason and follow Andrés’s call. If there is no rescue marker yet, continue with Fresh Evidence.
  2. After Fresh Evidence, look for Rescue Rafa from Kidnappers at Megamundo HQ downtown. That objective starts Child’s Play.

Fresh Evidence

  1. Finish Watch It and Adventures in Babysitting, then meet Dre’Quan in his Jack of Hearts office as Jason. Ask if he is ready and take the waiting car to Starfish Island.
  2. Climb where Dre’Quan stops at the wall, go up the boxes and enter the gym. Hide the guard in the closet Dre’Quan opens. Inside the mansion, take the key card from the table left of the bed.
  3. Follow Dre’Quan over the balcony and down the cabanas. At ground level, the poolside bar has a bottle for the guard distraction. The fridge on the right of the living room has raw meat for the tiger.
  4. In the basement, find the round room with the gold mural. If Jason loses rock paper scissors, take the blue prop with the hand-and-microphone symbol from the far-right display and insert it into the wall opening.
  5. Review the footage with Dre’Quan while the files copy. Escape through the elevator and drop down from the far balcony again. Take Dante’s speedboat from the dock and lose the police.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Child’s Play

  1. After Fresh Evidence, track Rescue Rafa from Kidnappers to Megamundo HQ and drive to the quarry south of the airport. As Lucia, aim the binoculars at the upper walkway beneath the row of red lights to locate Rafa.
  2. Approach on the right and climb the central stairs and ladder. After reaching Rafa and defending Andrés as Jason, switch back to Lucia. Carry Rafa north along the walkway, let him descend the ladder and pick him up again below.
  3. Follow Rafa’s forklift at ground level and take him to Gustavo’s car when it pulls up. Continue the pursuit and use one of the nearby bikes after the crash.
  4. At the SUV on the train tracks, open the door and follow the prompts to cut the seat belt and pull Rafa out. Continue after Gustavo up to the highway.

If you’re stuck The hourglass on this mission still leads to a required Story job. Complete it to move on to Power Play.

This is a Free Call From…

  1. After Lawyer Up, drive through or near Hamlet as Lucia and watch for Shayna’s message. Track Pick up Shayna to the prison as soon as it appears. This optional errand can expire.
  2. Drive Shayna to Raph’s house in Southside and follow her inside. Respond when the conversation turns into a standoff, then follow Shayna’s prompt to split the bag and leave the area.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Power Play

  1. After Child’s Play, take Lucia to the Help Kordell’s Crew marker in Salton. Ask the receptionist for Kordell and go down the hall to the left.
  2. After switching to Jason, climb the ladder on the right side of the building. Cross the roof and drop to the lower roof on the far side to find the open door. Go downstairs and use the Snap Gun on the planning room door.
  3. Interact with the whiteboard to turn it, then photograph the plans on the reverse. Other evidence in the room does not clear the plans objective.
  4. As Lucia, set the cones across the road and follow Kordell. Pick up the marked weapon when he opens the bag, cover the crew and follow their escape through the construction site. Take the marked vehicle and clear the wanted level.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Close Surveillance

  1. After Power Play, meet Karim at the van on the top level of the Ocean Beach parking garage as Jason. Fly the drone to The Calique and check beneath the smaller canopy on the north side of the roof.
  2. When the targets take the elevator, find their car in the alley to its right. Stay high enough to clear obstacles and close enough to keep the audio meter green. Enter the apartment through the open door on the third balcony.
  3. Place the camera by the TV and on the opposite shelf. When Omar moves away, place the kitchen-counter, fridge-top and entry-shelf cameras. Follow him into the bedroom when he opens it and check the shelf to the right of the bed.
  4. Switch camera feeds to follow the conversation. Escape the apartment and fly the drone to the van’s green marker. Drop Lucia off behind Ajax Gym.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Delivery Method

  1. After Power Play, track Help Andrea to the planning room as Jason. Ride the delivery bike to the airport gate and take the TSS office entrance on the right.
  2. Talk to the receptionist and use the flash drive at her desk when she steps away. Remove it once the first progress bar finishes, then follow the evacuation objective at the alarm behind reception.
  3. When the office empties, go up one floor and into the back-left office. Use the flash drive on that computer, retrieve it after the transfer and leave on the bike.

If you’re stuck There are two separate computers. Head upstairs promptly once the evacuation clears the stairway.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Team Building

  1. As Lucia after Power Play, track Meet Kordell to Ajax Gym. Follow him outside to the alley and drive his car to the warehouse. Use the door on the left side of the building.
  2. Block or dodge in the cage, then counter. Fight the first two opponents and finish the next phase with Kordell.
  3. Take Kordell’s car to Riverbank Waterside Bar and follow him inside to finish.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Fully Catered

  1. After Power Play, wait for Raul’s request to Steal a Catering Truck. Reach the marked Plane & Simple vehicles at VCIA.
  2. Use the lower roof on the right of the building and cross it. Drop on the left side of the middle truck, use the Slim Jim prompt and drive to the drop-off in Rockridge.
  3. Back in the planning room, move the marked bundles of fake cash into the bags during Raul’s conversation until the setup clears.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Heist Plans in Place

  1. Open Map → Story → VCIA Takedown and look for Complete prep work. Check the list as Lucia and Jason. A pending objective can belong to the other character.
  2. After Nate calls Lucia, complete her handoff to Raul. At the next planning-room marker, find the painted door beside the blue dumpster.

Headliners

  1. After the airport setups, follow Help With The Launch Party to Club VYB. After Dre’Quan’s tour, try the lights at the DJ booth and exit the controls when you are ready.
  2. Assign all three VIP tables as Jason and hold the confirmation prompt. The table choices affect the offer money and follower gains. As Lucia, check Maleek Rivers and handle KD’s photo if you invited him.
  3. Meet Dre’Quan outside, open the passenger door he points out and escort Sexxy Red downstairs. Pick a group to move from one of your assigned tables. The Game Developers are a good choice if you already took their up-front offer.
  4. Follow Jason to the balcony and grab DWNPLY from the front right below the stage. Finish both characters’ fights and speak to Dre’Quan after the Dimez perform.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

VCIA Takedown

  1. Complete the prep list, go to Raul’s planning room and choose Start the Airport Heist.
  2. When control switches to Jason and Go to the network terminal appears, take the marked roof passage before the timer runs out.
  3. In the baggage section, check the open container by Inbound Belt 1. On the tarmac, drive alongside the plane and wait for Lucia’s boarding prompt.

Tie Up Loose Ends

  1. After the airport job, open Map → Story and read the Tie Up Loose Ends card. If it says Wait for a message, do other available jobs and check again after the next call.
  2. Wait at the Cafe in the same area of the menu is an optional objective. Follow the pending Story message if you want to advance the main route.

Follow Wait at the Cafe to unlock bank vaults

That’s Where The Money Is…

  1. After VCIA Takedown, track the optional Wait at the Cafe task from Yasmani. Sit at a window seat in Cafe Orquidea and watch for the security van.
  2. Follow the van at a distance and follow the guard into the bank. Use the marked vault door prompt. Take the marked bills from the cart, handing a bag to your partner if you need room for more.
  3. Leave through the front, escape the wanted level and return to Yasmani. After the job, check his stock for security van tips.

Call Valentina Back

  1. After VCIA Takedown, wait for Valentina’s update about the hard drive. As either character, call her from the phone or select Call Valentina Back in the Story list.
  2. Calling advances the wait for Sunset. Once it appears, follow Stop by the Front Desk to the Y Hotel.

References

  • PowerPyx · Mission walkthrough

Sunset

  1. After the airport sequence and Call Valentina Back, follow Stop by the Front Desk to the Y Hotel lobby. Talk to Santino and read the letter at the counter.
  2. When Go to the marina appears, go to Raul’s houseboat. Check the door by the stairs for Look for Raul. Follow the new departure markers down the dock to the boat.

References

  • Sirloin · Santino and the front desk

Starting From Zero

  1. After Sunset, follow Go to the Aqua Stadium to the seating by the water. Wait until the route changes to Big House Storage Inc, then collect the marked bag.
  2. For the rooftop meeting, find the pink HIGH LIFE HOTEL sign at street level. Use the elevator when Take the elevator to the terrace appears.
  3. Let the scene play until the gold Chapter 5 title appears with Bird in the Hand.

If you’re stuck Açai Dreams is the rooftop bar. Use the hotel sign to find the street entrance.
