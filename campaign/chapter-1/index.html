Gone Fishing

  1. Follow the seaplane in Jason’s boat. As a floating package comes into view, click both sticks for Focus, line up the bow with the marker and slow down so Cal can hook it. Repeat for the three packages.
  2. When the objective changes, stop offshore and find the “Welcome to Paradise” sign through the binoculars. Wait for the scan to register before docking.
  3. At Sunrise RV Park, head for the cabin beside the wind turbine and follow the new search and exit markers.

If you’re stuck If Cal misses a package, turn the boat around and make a slower, straight approach.

Gear Up

  1. Select Pick Up Another Gun in the Story list. At the shop counter, look for the Purchase a longarm objective and open the long-gun category.
  2. Buy a weapon that meets the prompt, such as the available shotgun. Wait for Purchase a longarm to clear before leaving.

Lab Fees

  1. Track Collect Brian’s Rent. Follow the phone prompt outside Moonstone Boutique, then speak to the clerk. Go behind the counter, enter 0420 on the green-wall keypad and take the marked bag of acid from the lab.
  2. Follow the phone prompt, then go to Isla Tropical Private Club. Use the fish mural to locate Brian’s Lock Up and enter 7700.
  3. Deposit the bag in the marked safe near the red tool cabinet. Finish that interaction to clear the job. Check the mailbox later when Brian tells you your share is ready.

If you’re stuck The two doors use different codes. Entering the lockup alone does not complete the deposit.

Just a Scratch

  1. Follow Fix Your Car to Joey’s workshop in Jason’s Ganado. Speak to Joey at the roller door, park in the work bay and confirm the basic repair when offered.
  2. Open Performance, then Full Engine. Check the price on Stock Engine (Restore) before confirming. Recorded menus show both free and $100 restorations.
  3. Open Cosmetics, then Respray. Finish the active paint objective and leave the bay after it clears.

If you’re stuck The free basic repair prompt and the engine restoration are separate interactions.

Pipework

  1. Choose Collect Mandy’s Rent and follow her inside. After she mentions the sink, leave the bedroom and pick up the plunger from the bathroom across the hall.
  2. Return to the kitchen sink beneath the window. Use the right stick when Plunge appears, then follow the wrench prompt to secure the pipe.
  3. Finish the conversation before leaving. Look for the optional cigar box outside if Mandy throws it into the road.

Unwanted Attention

  1. Follow Go See Cal to the impound. Find the red Phoenix behind the tow truck and follow the two-stick hotwire prompt.
  2. At the marked shop, choose Repair before Respray. Pick a visibly different color and then park all the way inside Brian’s striped garage bay.
  3. When the game asks you to change identified accessories, hold L1/LB, then use R1/RB to open Items. Remove the marked cap or glasses.

If you’re stuck If the objective remains open, check both accessory slots and make sure the car is fully in the bay.

Hey Arnold!

  1. Meet Brian at the pool bar on south Key Lento and pick up Cal at the liquor store. Head toward the radar-station buildings in the Maroon Key search area.
  2. Keep Arnold’s car in sight until he abandons it by the docks. Get out and follow him along the pier until the game offers the final interaction.
Choices and consequences

Letting Arnold go also completes the job.

Island Dreams

  1. Select Work for Kenton. Collect the yellow diving gear from the stern and stay with the other divers down to the trench.
  2. Sweep for three caches outside the wreck. Start by the broken pots, continue north along the edge of the coral ridge, then check the sand by the large rock. Follow each gold marker and finish the Search prompt.
  3. When Dobbs needs help, swim to the metal grate on top of the wreck. Hold both triggers and use the button shown on screen to free him. Then escort the group back to the boat.

If you’re stuck If the detector light goes out, stop swimming and turn until it goes green again.

Feng Shui

  1. After Island Dreams, track Meet Cal at Jason’s house. Get a beer from the refrigerator and sit down. Help move the TV, coffee table, sofa and chair when prompted.
  2. Play through the Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III prompts. Let the scene finish and wait for Jason’s next call.

Coming Home

  1. Take the phone call after Feng Shui. For the optional clothes, meet Andy at Manatee’s Rest, collect the bag by the waterfront and use the store prompt at your own car’s trunk. Do any other preparation before going to the prison.
  2. Park at the Hamlet Correctional Institution marker and walk into reception. Once Lucia gets into your car, follow the Rusty Anchor route and go inside with her.

Clean Up After Donnie

  1. Track Donnie’s Drop to the Hamlet dock. Speak to the contact and examine the first package along the bank to update the search area.
  2. Follow the airboat route, then cross the water on foot to the shack inside the marker. Take what the game highlights and deliver it to the marked container near Donnie’s Key Lento trailer.
  3. If a mailbox reward appears later, use the new marker to collect it.

Buck Me

  1. Select Set Up Bank Account, meet Lucia at Fleeca and wait for the manager to take you into the office.
  2. Once the account is active, open BuckMe on the in-game phone. Transfer the tutorial’s $100 from Checking Account to Family Savings and check for confirmation.

Down to Earth

  1. Meet Brian in the marina office. Use the pressure washer on the green marks aboard the boat, then board the seaplane when Lucia arrives.
  2. Land in Bobcat Bay near the large vessel. When Lucia takes control, line up the marked package and work the hook. Retake the pilot seat when prompted.
  3. Land for the first two deliveries. When the final objective says Fly alongside the boat, stay airborne and match it at low altitude.
  4. Back home, put the package in the washing machine in the lower shed. Return to the front door and follow Lucia to the deck.

Clear Skies

  1. After Down to Earth, answer Brian. Check the Southwind floatplane in the afternoon or use the wait prompt. Speak to Donnie, then look for Brian at the nearby tiki bar.
  2. Taxi into open water and build speed on a straight line. In the escort, match Pete’s heading and fly above his plane until it lands.
  3. Follow the second airstrip objective. Find Donnie in the building deeper into the strip, free him and follow the escape marker.
  4. Once Donnie takes the plane, use the marked bike to reach the rendezvous east of Watson Bay. Board when prompted.

Building a Life

  1. After Clear Skies, approach the left side of the coffee table frame. When the help prompt appears, join Lucia on the floor.
  2. Turn the left stick along the on-screen arc to work each bolt. The scene calls for taking some bolts back out to correct the frame. Follow the new prompt and finish the assembly.
  3. Lift the glass top from the cardboard box behind Lucia and set it onto the frame. The next scene starts after the tabletop is in place.

Day Release

  1. Order a drink at the back bar in Rusty Anchor to meet Mattia and Kat. Take the boat behind the bar to the yacht and finish the watercraft race. You can continue even if you lose.
  2. At Find Mattia, descend one level and cross the exterior walkway to the other staircase. Go down again to the central door on the bottom deck.
  3. Follow Mattia until the waypoint changes to Huxley Motel, then proceed into Nine1Nine. When control switches to Lucia in the later hallway, check the door at the far right.

Swamped

  1. Check Meet Brian at the marina after the other Chapter 1 errands are complete. Find him in the back building and follow his green marker through the camp.
  2. In the airboat, close in alongside Lori’s boat and match its direction and speed. Hold Square on PS5 or X on Xbox once the jump prompt appears.

If you’re stuck Approaching directly behind the other boat may leave the jump prompt hidden.

Don Gone

  1. At Donnie’s trailer, collect the bag on the bed and use Focus to highlight the packing steps. Check the photo by the TV, schedule under the Patriot Beer sign, clothes, ID by the door and map near the fridge.
  2. Leave when the phone and exit prompts appear. If you collect unique items, stop and check the red hatchback’s trunk for Donnie’s H&L .38 Revolver before you complete the vehicle disposal.

Recalibration

  1. After the trailer sequence, wait for Lucia’s call and return to the house. Buy the six-pack at the marked cooler and look for Cal at the water-side dock behind his place.
  2. Cast at the fishing marker. When you hook a fish, rotate the right stick to reel and push the left stick opposite the fish’s direction of travel.
  3. At the thatched dock, cook both sides of three portions on the grill. Wait until Cal collects them before looking for Lucia out front.

Tourist Trap

  1. Before going to south Key Lento beach, make a manual save, finish any Shirley errands you want and check your cash storage options.
  2. At the visitor center, take the long ramp and follow the covered walkway with pale blue columns. Follow Get the bag from the locker and the terminal prompt, then return when instructed.
  3. After the character switch, protect Jason through the display cases and courtyard to the dock. Follow the boat route offshore before heading to Cal’s place.

Check Chapter 1 activities before leaving

Home Sweet Home

  1. On returning to the Keys house, enter through the front door and follow the escape prompts as Lucia.
  2. Once the objective changes to leaving the house, take the front door and clear the displayed search area. Follow Andy’s pickup marker if you arranged it.
Choices and consequences

Once the house is on fire, the objective is escape. Searching the interior for Jason’s cash will not advance the scene.

Discharge Time

  1. At Vice City Hospital, go upstairs and follow the pink floor route to room 203. Room 211 is a door you pass on the way.
  2. When the game switches to Jason, follow the floor-level Crawl to the gun objective and finish the marked hospital escape to reach Chapter 2.
