What’s in the Bag?

  1. In the Starlet Motel room, use Flip to turn the statue over. Read the paper attached to its base.
  2. Put the statue down and inspect the hard drive on the table. The room interaction ends after you check both objects.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_18
  • MKIceAndFire · the note beneath the statue

Take Care of Jason

  1. After What’s in the Bag?, look under Map → Story for the ongoing check-in objective. It has no separate map pin.
  2. Come back to Jason’s room at the Starlet Motel between jobs. Keep clearing the other Story objectives until the motel marker turns pink for Breakfast Run. The check-in objective clears after that mission.

References

  • PowerPyx · where to find the objective and when it clears
  • IGN · Play Nurse and the Take Care of Jason objective

Overnight Charges

  1. After What’s in the Bag?, leave the Starlet Motel room as Lucia and enter reception on the left. Speak to Linda at the desk.
  2. If Jason gave Lucia the necklace in Chapter 1, she leaves it with Linda as collateral. Letting the dialogue timer run out leads to the same handover. Paying the room bill later gets the necklace back.
  3. Before leaving reception, inspect the wall map if you want to reveal Port Gellhorn on your map.

References

  • PowerPyx · motel reception and necklace

Just Moved In

  1. Cross from Starlet Motel to the Alpha gas station shop. Pay for the eight highlighted supplies through the shelf interactions.
  2. If only soup remains, check the end of an aisle for Soup. Once the HUD says Take the supplies back to the motel, carry the paper bag to Jason’s room.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_19
  • MKIceAndFire · shop supplies

Girls’ Trip

  1. After the motel charge and supply run, ask Zach about painkillers by the office, then knock on Vicki’s blue door with the oval 10 plate. Once she lets Lucia in, check the bathroom while Vicki gets the pills. If you take them to Jason first, return to Vicki after he identifies them as Zombix.
  2. Finish the on-screen struggle and help Vicki up. Get in her convertible and drive to Delights in Port Gellhorn. Follow her inside until she collects her paycheck, then return to the car.
  3. Check Dunk’s trailer nearby. When he is absent, follow the new marker to the boatyard. Drive through the open warehouse door and stop by the people sitting on couches to get the car wash location.
  4. Enter the car wash alone. Choose Defuse on each prompt to clear the encounter peacefully. If the confrontation turns into a fight, complete it and wait for the shutter-door prompt. Open the shutter and get back in Vicki’s car.
  5. Follow Dunk down the ramp, turn right onto the main road and take the left by the shooting range. If you lose him, Vicki’s follow-up call gives you the location. At the crash, guide Dunk to Vicki’s car and take him back to his trailer.

Pick Up a Gun

  1. After Girl’s Trip, Lucia can take the optional Gellhorn Outfitters objective in Port Gellhorn.
  2. Either buy a weapon at the shop counter or use the green locker to collect a weapon you already own. Both satisfy the objective.

References

  • PowerPyx · shop and locker options

Moses

  1. After Girl’s Trip, take a vehicle to Moses in Port Gellhorn. In the yard, open WAiNK, point at a car and hold R1/RB. Look at Tracker separately from the Security result.
  2. Pick up the Slim Jim and use it on the tutorial’s Montagne XL. Rotate the right stick slowly until the game highlights the interaction.
  3. When the shop asks for a car, bring in one marked Tracker: None. Complete the respray and select Register until the ownership confirmation appears.

If you’re stuck Alarmed describes a security feature. A car can show Alarmed and still qualify if its tracker reads None.

New Look

  1. Once Girl’s Trip ends, track the hair salon objective as Lucia and go to Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn.
  2. Speak to the clerk and pay for any hairstyle. Confirm the purchase if you have only previewed a style.

References

  • PowerPyx · salon location and purchase requirement

How Do I Look?

  1. After Girl’s Trip, take Lucia to a clothing store such as Bargain World in southern Port Gellhorn.
  2. The outfit objective checks both categories. Buy a new top and a pair of trousers or shorts, then check the Story list for completion.

References

  • PowerPyx · the two clothing categories

Painkiller Supplier

  1. After Girl’s Trip, the Story list asks Lucia to wait for Dunk. Keep doing the available jobs in Port Gellhorn, including Moses, the clothing and salon objectives, and Ambulance Chaser.
  2. After the first call, the objective can change to Dunk Will Be in Touch. Keep playing until he calls again and opens Slam Dunk. Check the Story tab if there is no map pin yet.

Ambulance Chaser

  1. Select Move the Ambulance, circle the barn east of Starlet Motel and use the outside steps behind it to trigger the job.
  2. Follow the red vehicle marker. If it stops in the clearing, wait for Destroy the ambulance and follow its marked interactions. If it goes into the water, continue to the next objective and watch for completion.

Behind on Payments

  1. Once Overnight Charges is clear, return to the Starlet Motel reception as Lucia with GTA$900 in cash. If the money is in BuckMe, withdraw it at an ATM before speaking to Linda.
  2. If you are short, advance the other Chapter 2 objectives first. Paying clears the room balance and returns the necklace if Linda took it as collateral.

Daily Driver

  1. After Moses, check the phone for the ’82 Crawler photo. In the search area, go to the piles of concrete pipes and look for the cream open-top CRAWLER.
  2. Follow the return route to Watkins Auto Parts and park inside Moses’s marked workshop bay.

Prepare for Vice City

  1. After Coffee Break, check Map → Story for your remaining objectives and watch for Vicki’s call about Afternoon Delight. Finish that mission and wait for Cal’s message about the couple found near Port Gellhorn.
  2. If you plan to use the Afternoon Delight payout for the cash objective, wait for BuckMe to confirm the deposit.
  3. If you still need money after completing Moses, scan vehicles on Port Gellhorn’s main road with WAiNK and bring one showing Tracker: None to his garage. If the shop has just closed after a sale, give it a couple of minutes to reopen.
  4. If your Story list still shows a GTA$15,000 target, check your BuckMe Checking balance. IGN reports that reaching this amount made the motel marker appear sooner. Check the marker and your current objective before doing more cash runs.

If you’re stuck Clear outstanding Story markers and give the phone time to receive Cal’s message. Then check the Starlet Motel room for Move to Vice City.

Slam Dunk

  1. Wait for Dunk’s follow-up call after Girl’s Trip, then meet him at his Port Gellhorn trailer. Follow him behind the dumpster until Bill goes inside the shop.
  2. Follow the dock and compound prompts. When Escape the compound appears, reverse back out through the gate before continuing.
  3. Take Bill to the southwestern airstrip with the domed radar structure. When Find Dunk appears, head to the main building in the middle of the search area.

Check Out McKittrick Farm

  1. Find the pilot flyer on the wall by the Starlet Motel office door. Hold L2 on PS5 or LT on Xbox to examine it. Follow the new KM marker east of Port Gellhorn, wait for the crop duster to stop on the grass strip and accept Kyle’s job.
  2. Take off along the strip and line up with the pink dot marking Kyle. Choose Spray Chemicals if the wheel appears, then hold R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox when the spray prompt appears. Circle after each pass. Three confirmed passes advance the job.
  3. Line up with the same grass strip, reduce power and land. Check BuckMe for the recorded GTA$400 payment, then keep playing until Kyle texts about the van.

Continue with Kyle’s van request and negotiation choices

Collect Chemicals for Kyle

  1. After the crop-dusting job, track Kyle’s text to Farm Fix Meth Lab north of Belville. Talk to the gate guard about the chemicals and approach the white van when it pulls in.
  2. The first offer is GTA$5,000. To lower it, choose Negotiate, Pay Less, then Pay Now for the recorded GTA$3,000 deal. To avoid paying cash, choose Negotiate, Pay Later, then Give Collateral to offer Kyle’s plane.
  3. Drive the van into the marked barn at McKittrick Farm. Clear any pursuit before returning if you took the van by force. Finish Kyle’s conversation and check for GTA$1,000 in BuckMe. You can leave after the handoff or try the large strawberry before going.

Follow Kyle’s later call for help

Breakfast Run

  1. After Slam Dunk and the other active jobs, wait for Jason to call. Return to the pink Starlet Motel marker and go with him to Hank’s Waffles.
  2. Follow the counter prompts before the timer in the lower-right runs out. Return to the car when the HUD says Get in the car and complete the next driving objective.
  3. Back at the motel lot, look for the personal vehicle notices. Coffee Break follows.

References

Coffee Break

  1. After Breakfast Run, use the motel kitchen coffee machine, choose Coffee ($2), then return to the red car.
  2. In free roam, hold D-pad Right to open the partner menu. Use R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to switch. During a character-locked mission, wait until free roam returns.

References

Hit Avery Pawn

  1. After Coffee Break, track Hit Avery Pawn in Port Gellhorn. Follow the Rob the Pawn Shop objective and use the display case interactions to collect jewelry.
  2. In the back office, find the black keypad safe beside the desk with upright folders. If the game offers a safe-code dialogue prompt, use the number you obtain in your own run. Once the safe opens, take the cash from the middle shelf.
  3. Finish the Lose the Cops objective. If you picked up jewelry, follow Fence Stolen Goods to Dunk’s trailer to turn the loot into cash.

Hit Kingpin Bowling

  1. After Coffee Break, track Hit Kingpin Bowling in south Port Gellhorn. For Let’s Go Bowling, check the southeast office by the lanes for the watch, then the green pool table in the northeast for the Gutterball Moreland 850.
  2. Find the safe in the small office off the northeast hallway. If the keypad is available, enter 0300 including the first zero. The number appears on a map in the building east of the Brooks Boatworks warehouse.
  3. Take the cash and check that Key Valuables Found shows all three. Leave and finish the wanted-level objective before checking for the trophy notification.

If you’re stuck If the safe offers Open with Explosive, the in-game Sticky Bomb interaction is another way to open it. If you missed an item, the operation can reappear later so you can collect the remaining valuable.

Impounded Vehicle

  1. After Breakfast Run impounds Lucia’s personal car, finish Coffee Break and look for the green car icon by the Port Gellhorn police station.
  2. Enter the impound, get into Lucia’s marked vehicle and follow the escape objective. The marker is for Lucia’s car. If you are looking for the Phoenix, use the separate Chapter 1 guide.

Thrillbillies

  1. After Coffee Break, choose Check Out the Mud Club in Kelly County. Finish the outfit objective at the MERCH stand before going to the rentals.
  2. Complete two laps through the pink checkpoints. The recorded rental menu shows $250 for a Sanchez and the first-place result pays $2,500.
  3. Finish the conversation beside Billy’s purple bike to set up his next job.

Ultimate Edition Dominator Buggy

  1. Use a profile that has the Ultimate Edition entitlement. In Chapter 2, complete the Mud Club introduction and the dirt-track event.
  2. Look for the new collection objective in the map’s Exploration section. It points to Paradise Garage in Watson Bay, where the ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy is stored.

If you’re stuck If you own Ultimate but see no marker, check the installed add-on and the Mud Club progress first.

Veteran Hunted

  1. After Coffee Break, play as Jason and watch for Bret Vogel’s text while raising the money for Vice City. Go to the marker in northern Lummox County alone. If Lucia is with you, separate before starting.
  2. Follow the truck objective, then use Focus to pick up the trail. Study the deer through binoculars or the rifle scope when prompted.
  3. Finish the hunt and use the truck and cabin prompts to hand over the deer. The documented rewards are GTA$500 via BuckMe and the Killtelligence SM-69. Watch for Bret’s Target Decks afterward.

Afternoon Delight

  1. After Coffee Break, wait for Vicki to call and track Rob Vicki’s Boss at Delights. Go in as Lucia. As Jason, walk along the building, climb the metal fence on the right and use the open backstage door.
  2. Make a dressing-room choice. If you Help, complete Jason’s stage prompts and approach Mario by Lucia to move the scene along. When Vicki calls Lucia over, go through the kitchen to the office. If you choose Don’t Help, wait for Jason’s portrait to pulse, then hold D-pad Right and tap R1 or RB to switch. Move through the club to the office door by the bar.
  3. Pick up the loose cash on Frankie’s desk and side table. Vicki’s message gives safe code 2008. Open the safe and take both bundles of marked bills before leaving the office.
  4. Follow the escape objective and lose the bikers and any police pursuit. On the Help route, change Jason’s clothes at the motel or thrift store when the objective appears, then return to the car.
  5. Meet Vicki behind the abandoned bumper cars at Gellhorn Fun Fair. IGN recorded GTA$1,800 from the office and GTA$10,000 from Vicki. Before leaving, scan the nearby Ferris wheel with WAiNK to log the landmark.

Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab

  1. After Thrillbillies, wait for Billy’s message and track Join Billy on a Job in Port Gellhorn. Take the bike near the yellow donation bin and follow him to the rear of the billboard.
  2. When Pick up the bag appears, take it before remounting. Follow Billy through the lit tunnel to the rocky clearing and wait for the settlement.

Checking Out

  1. When Move to Vice City appears at Starlet Motel, make a separate manual save if you want to revisit Chapter 2. Open the bathroom door of the marked room to start packing.
  2. When you regain control of Jason, take the Moreland 850 shotgun from the bed. Use the doorway for cover until you can reach the kitchen marker. Regroup with Lucia there if you want to preserve the relationship benefit.
  3. Hold the kitchen during Defend Yourself. If you need recovery items, the wall cabinet between the coffee machine and sink holds Zombix and Adrenaline. Follow Jason outside once the objective changes.
  4. Get onto the motorcycle and follow Jason’s pink marker through the construction site. If you lose sight of the car, use Focus to see its route.
  5. Use the straight road along the railway to catch up. Come alongside at a similar speed and hold Square on PS5 or X on Xbox when Jump to the car appears. Follow the final pursuit objectives from the passenger seat to reach Welcome to Vice City.

If you’re stuck If a checkpoint retry leaves Jason’s marker missing, try restarting the mission from the beginning.
