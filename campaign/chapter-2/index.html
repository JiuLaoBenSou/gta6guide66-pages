What’s in the Bag?

  1. In the Starlet Motel room, use Flip to turn the statue over. Read the paper attached to its base.
  2. Put the statue down and inspect the hard drive on the table. The room interaction ends after you check both objects.

Just Moved In

  1. Cross from Starlet Motel to the Alpha gas station shop. Pay for the eight highlighted supplies through the shelf interactions.
  2. If only soup remains, check the end of an aisle for Soup. Once the HUD says Take the supplies back to the motel, carry the paper bag to Jason’s room.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_19
  • MKIceAndFire · shop supplies

Girl’s Trip

  1. After handling the motel charge and shopping, look for Zach by the office window. Knock on Vicki’s blue door with the oval 10 plate, return to Jason, then go back when the marker updates.
  2. Follow Vicki through Delights, Dunk’s trailer and the boatyard to the car wash. The game offers Defuse during the confrontation. Continue when the roller door becomes the target.
  3. Follow Dunk to the farm with two silos. Finish the conversation and return him to the trailer in Vicki’s car.

References

Moses

  1. After Girl’s Trip, take a vehicle to Moses in Port Gellhorn. In the yard, open WAiNK, point at a car and hold R1/RB. Look at Tracker separately from the Security result.
  2. Pick up the Slim Jim and use it on the tutorial’s Montagne XL. Rotate the right stick slowly until the game highlights the interaction.
  3. When the shop asks for a car, bring in one marked Tracker: None. Complete the respray and select Register until the ownership confirmation appears.

If you’re stuck Alarmed describes a security feature. A car can show Alarmed and still qualify if its tracker reads None.

Ambulance Chaser

  1. Select Move the Ambulance, circle the barn east of Starlet Motel and use the outside steps behind it to trigger the job.
  2. Follow the red vehicle marker. If it stops in the clearing, wait for Destroy the ambulance and follow its marked interactions. If it goes into the water, continue to the next objective and watch for completion.

Daily Driver

  1. After Moses, check the phone for the ’82 Crawler photo. In the search area, go to the piles of concrete pipes and look for the cream open-top CRAWLER.
  2. Follow the return route to Watkins Auto Parts and park inside Moses’s marked workshop bay.

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_21

Prepare for Vice City

  1. After Coffee Break, check Map → Story. The recorded objective requires $15,000 alongside the remaining story tasks.
  2. Clear the outstanding jobs and watch for Vicki’s call about Afternoon Delight. If you use that payout, wait for BuckMe to confirm it.
  3. If you still need money after completing Moses, scan vehicles on Port Gellhorn’s main road with WAiNK and bring one showing Tracker: None to his garage. If the shop has just closed after a sale, give it a couple of minutes to reopen.
  4. When both conditions are satisfied, return to Starlet Motel and look for Checking Out at the room.

If you’re stuck If you already have the cash, check the remaining Story entries and calls. The cash requirement alone does not open the room marker.

Slam Dunk

  1. Wait for Dunk’s follow-up call after Girl’s Trip, then meet him at his Port Gellhorn trailer. Follow him behind the dumpster until Bill goes inside the shop.
  2. Follow the dock and compound prompts. When Escape the compound appears, reverse back out through the gate before continuing.
  3. Take Bill to the southwestern airstrip with the domed radar structure. When Find Dunk appears, head to the main building in the middle of the search area.

Check Out McKittrick Farm

  1. Read the pilot-job flyer on the wall outside Starlet Motel reception. At McKittrick Farm, wait for Kyle to get out of the plane and accept the job.
  2. Take off along the strip. When Crop dust over Kyle appears, choose Spray Chemicals from the wheel and use the spray control as you fly over Kyle’s marker.
  3. When the aircraft return objective appears, follow it back to the strip. Wait for Kyle’s later message for Collect Chemicals for Kyle.

Breakfast Run

  1. After Slam Dunk and the other active jobs, wait for Jason to call. Return to the pink Starlet Motel marker and go with him to Hank’s Waffles.
  2. Follow the counter prompts before the timer in the lower-right runs out. Return to the car when the HUD says Get in the car and complete the next driving objective.
  3. Back at the motel lot, look for the personal vehicle notices. Coffee Break follows.

References

Coffee Break

  1. After Breakfast Run, use the motel kitchen coffee machine, choose Coffee ($2), then return to the red car.
  2. In free roam, hold D-pad Right to open the partner menu. Use R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to switch. During a character-locked mission, wait until free roam returns.

Hit Avery Pawn

  1. After Coffee Break, track Hit Avery Pawn in Port Gellhorn. Follow the Rob the Pawn Shop objective and use the display case interactions to collect jewelry.
  2. In the back office, find the black keypad safe beside the desk with upright folders. If the game offers a safe-code dialogue prompt, use the number you obtain in your own run. Once the safe opens, take the cash from the middle shelf.
  3. Finish the Lose the Cops objective. If you picked up jewelry, follow Fence Stolen Goods to Dunk’s trailer to turn the loot into cash.

Hit Kingpin Bowling

  1. After Coffee Break, track Hit Kingpin Bowling in south Port Gellhorn. For Let’s Go Bowling, check the southeast office by the lanes for the watch, then the green pool table in the northeast for the Gutterball Moreland 850.
  2. Find the safe in the small office off the northeast hallway. If the keypad is available, enter 0300 including the first zero. The number appears on a map in the building east of the Brooks Boatworks warehouse.
  3. Take the cash and check that Key Valuables Found shows all three. Leave and finish the wanted-level objective before checking for the trophy notification.

If you’re stuck If the safe offers Open with Explosive, the in-game Sticky Bomb interaction is another way to open it. If you missed an item, the operation can reappear later so you can collect the remaining valuable.

Thrillbillies

  1. After Coffee Break, choose Check Out the Mud Club in Kelly County. Finish the outfit objective at the MERCH stand before going to the rentals.
  2. Complete two laps through the pink checkpoints. The recorded rental menu shows $250 for a Sanchez and the first-place result pays $2,500.
  3. Finish the conversation beside Billy’s purple bike to set up his next job.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_27

Ultimate Edition Dominator Buggy

  1. Use a profile that has the Ultimate Edition entitlement. In Chapter 2, complete the Mud Club introduction and the dirt-track event.
  2. Look for the new collection objective in the map’s Exploration section. It points to Paradise Garage in Watson Bay, where the ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy is stored.

If you’re stuck If you own Ultimate but see no marker, check the installed add-on and the Mud Club progress first.

Afternoon Delight

  1. After Coffee Break, wait for Vicki’s call and track Rob Vicki’s Boss to Delights. Enter through the front as Lucia and follow the rear entrance objective when control switches to Jason.
  2. After the dressing-room decision, find the safe next to the security monitors. The in-game Notes menu shows 2008 in the recorded route. Collect the marked bills and follow the escape objective.
Choices and consequences

Help sends Jason into the performance while Lucia heads to the safe. On that route, complete Jason’s clothing stop before meeting Vicki at the pier. Don’t Help brings both leads to the same back-office safe and goes to the pier after the escape.

Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab

  1. After Thrillbillies, wait for Billy’s message and track Join Billy on a Job in Port Gellhorn. Take the bike near the yellow donation bin and follow him to the rear of the billboard.
  2. When Pick up the bag appears, take it before remounting. Follow Billy through the lit tunnel to the rocky clearing and wait for the settlement.

References

  • 3DM · Reference installment 3996395_30
  • MKIceAndFire · bag pickup prompt

Checking Out

  1. Once Prepare for Vice City clears, go back to the marked Starlet Motel room. Save before interacting with the bathroom door if you want to return to Chapter 2.
  2. When Regroup with Lucia appears, use the restaurant marker. On the motorcycle, approach Jason’s car from the side at a similar speed. Hold Square on PS5 or X on Xbox when Jump to the car appears.

If you’re stuck If a checkpoint retry leaves Jason’s marker missing, a full mission restart is the next thing to try.
