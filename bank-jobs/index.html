All 22 GTA 6 bank jobs

Credit Check unlocks 14 small-bank jobs. Wait at the Cafe unlocks eight vault jobs later in Chapter 4. Open a branch below for its in-game map and the clues that distinguish it from nearby banks.

Small banksUnlock vaultsEight vaults100% and trophy

Small banks · 14 locations

  1. Finish Credit Check (Meet Raul’s Crew) and pick up the Dye Pack Jammer during the mission.
  2. Reveal the district on the pause map and select Robberies → Bank Jobs. Check the selected branch’s map card for Cash Drawer and Safe.
  3. Take the cash from the counter and loot the safe as separate objectives. After the wanted level clears, check the card again. A bright green marker indicates a completed job.

Mariana County

  • Fleeca · Key Lento

    Set the waypoint on the beachside road at the northeast end of Key Lento.

  • Shorefront · Watson Bay

    Look just inland from the shore, beside the curving waterfront road.

  • Gibson Brothers Bank

    Match the branch in the street grid west of the round lake. This job has a drawer and safe on its map card.

Vice-Dale County

  • Lombank · Peacock Bay

    Use the Peacock Bay marker on the north side of the road toward the water. Fleeca has a separate marker nearby.

  • Fleeca · Peacock Bay

    Look beside the park on the inland side of the Peacock Bay marina.

  • Shorefront · Downtown

    Use the marker by the park and the road leading toward the bridge. Its Downtown neighbors in Big Bank Jobs are different jobs.

  • Shorefront · Rockridge

    Look in the Rockridge street grid west of the freeway ramps.

  • Lombank · Bayside

    Use the Bayside coast south of the highway exit. The map card sits across from the narrow offshore island.

  • Fleeca · Washington Beach

    Go to the inland edge of Washington Beach, near the bridges over the canal.

  • Lombank · Ocean Beach

    Use the Bank Jobs entry north of the Y Hotel. The Big Bank Jobs menu has two more Ocean Beach Lombanks.

  • Fleeca · Ocean Beach

    Use the Bank Jobs entry on the coastward side of the beach street grid. It tracks two objectives.

  • Shorefront · Ocean Beach

    Match the Bank Jobs card beside the curving beach road. Use the objective list to distinguish it from the Shorefront vault.

Leonard County

  • Druthers State Bank

    Look in the built-up grid south of the two small ponds.

Kelly County

  • Shorefront · Ambrosia

    Set the waypoint near the waterfront road at Ambrosia.

Unlock vaults at Cafe Orquidea

  1. After VCIA Takedown, check Yasmani’s message and choose Wait at the Cafe in the Story list. Go to Cafe Orquidea in Ocean Beach and take the seat by the window.
  1. When Tail the security van appears, get in your vehicle and follow the van to the bank. At the stop, wait for the guard to carry the case inside and follow the objective into the building.
  1. Inside, follow the mission prompts past the back door. Look for the cart of Marked Bills inside the vault. If one bag fills up, use the partner handoff prompt so you can pick up more.
  1. Clear the four-star wanted level and deliver the bag to Yasmani. For later vault runs, follow a security van to a branch and use the delivery window. Yasmani also sells GTA$10,000 tips after his earlier Payday Loans jobs.

Large banks · eight vaults

After the café mission, look under Robberies → Big Bank Jobs. At a branch with a vault, the map card tracks three objectives: Cash Drawer, Safe and Vault. Use the card and screenshots to pick the correct branch when a name repeats.

All eight vaults

  • Fleeca · VCIA

    Look at the road interchange north of the Vice City International Airport grounds.

  • Fleeca · Tequesta

    Find the marker in the curved Tequesta street grid near the water.

  • Lombank · Downtown

    Find the red-security vault on the mainland side of the Downtown bridge, by the park.

  • Fleeca · Downtown

    In Downtown, choose the Fleeca vault west of Lombank.

  • Lombank · Ocean Beach (high security)

    The high-security Lombank is farther north, in the blocks near the beach. Match the red security indicator on the map card.

  • Fleeca · Ocean Beach

    Check the Big Bank Jobs map card near the middle of the island. Confirm that Vault is one of its objectives.

  • Lombank · Ocean Beach (medium security)

    The other Lombank vault is farther south, toward the inlet. Match the orange security indicator on the map card.

  • Shorefront · Ocean Beach

    Look near the large green park. Select the branch that displays the Vault objective.

100% and Maximum Withdrawal

Maximum Withdrawal takes five large vaults. For Story Mode 100%, finish all 22 locations and check the map cards for any missing drawer or safe objective. Take Marked Bills to a fence to turn them into spendable money.
